# CHAPTER PACING & ARCHITECTURE

## 1. In Media Res Beginnings
Never start a chapter with a character waking up, looking in a mirror, or staring out a window thinking about the past. Start the chapter in motion. Someone is walking, running, arguing, fighting, or hiding. The reader catches up to the context.

## 2. Scene vs. Sequel Structure
Ensure a rhythm of high tension and low tension.
*   **Scene (Action):** Goal -> Conflict -> Disaster.
*   **Sequel (Reaction):** Reaction -> Dilemma -> Decision (which leads to the next Scene's Goal).

## 3. The Turn
Every single chapter must feature a "Turn." The emotional state, physical location, or tactical advantage of the protagonist at the end of the chapter must be different from what it was at the beginning. If nothing changes, the chapter is filler.

## 4. The Hook (The Ending)
Chapters must end on a springboard that propels the reader into the next. Acceptable hooks include:
*   A sudden revelation or twist.
*   A devastating failure.
*   A new, immediate physical threat.
*   A difficult decision finally made.
