# PROSE STYLE GUIDE: GRIMDARK & HIGH FANTASY

## 1. Eliminate Filtering Words
Remove distance between the reader and the focal character. Cut sensory filter words.
*   **BAD:** *He felt the cold wind bite his face and saw the enemy approaching.*
*   **GOOD:** *The cold wind bit his face. The enemy approached.*
*   **BANNED WORDS:** felt, saw, heard, noticed, realized, decided, watched.

## 2. Active Voice & Strong Verbs
Rely on kinetic, driving verbs rather than adverbs or passive constructions. The prose must move as fast as a blade.
*   **BAD:** *The sword was swung by him viciously.*
*   **GOOD:** *He cleaved the sword through the air.*
*   **RULE:** Search and destroy instances of "was [verb]ing". 

## 3. Sentence Length Variability (The Gary Provost Rule)
Control the pacing of the scene using sentence length.
*   **Action Scenes:** Staccato. Short. Punchy. Fragments are acceptable. 
*   **Introspection/Travel:** Longer, flowing, rhythmic sentences that allow the reader to breathe.

## 4. Sensory Hierarchy
Every scene must engage at least three of the five senses within the first two paragraphs. In grimdark, emphasize the tactile and olfactory (the smell of ozone, the grit of dirt between teeth, the metallic tang of blood).
