## SECTION 1: THE TEMPORAL ENGINE

Time dilation does not merely alter the mechanical tick of a clock; it actively crushes the atmosphere. Inside the Breakfast Court Hall, the temporal shear thickens the air until every inhalation tastes of scorched ozone, wet copper, and the humming static of violent magic. One minute inside this heavily warded sanctuary equals exactly fifteen seconds in the sunlit world above. The temporal discrepancy transforms the grand chamber into a sovereign pressure engine. Commands go out. The dead return. Administrative networks collapse under the weight of brutal efficiency. Ledgers scream as their ink is forcibly rewritten by telepathic consensus. Yet the chamber holds its shape—beautiful, stratified, and entirely merciless, anchored solely by the immense, quiet weight of the throne.

Meat, heavily salted biscuit, thin bone broth, and clarified butter rest untouched upon the heavy oak table. They are the practical remains of rulership, the biological necessities that persist even when time itself bends violently over the anvil.

Sovereignty does not require puppeteering every knife-stroke from the chair. The Hall remains the beating heart; Sable acts as the hand. Out in the damp, blood-soaked field, moving through the shattered remnants of the blood chapel and the descent-sanctum, Sable operates entirely under delegated law—*Field Sovereignty Active.* The Hall awaits her return, synchronized with the slaughter unfolding in real-time beneath the city streets, feeding logic and commands directly into the combat zone without ever slowing Sable's blade.

The chamber stratifies into distinct layers of specialized purpose, each humming with organized intent beneath the Soft Epic Model B logistical framework, tracking exactly nine hundred and seventy-nine active followers. The ten-body discrepancy resolved itself hours ago through the accelerated integration of incoming ranks, seamlessly absorbed into the hierarchy.

On the center floor, the resurrected bent woman hangs suspended in mid-air within a ring of glowing binding runes. She no longer attempts to mask her identity or feign innocence; she is simply a local node in the enemy's network, dragged violently above the level of her own usefulness. Death provided no lasting sanctuary from the audit. The resurrection sickness leaves her skin translucent, glistening with cold sweat, her frame shivering violently. Her surface thoughts bleed raw, unfiltered terror into the ambient psychic field—fear of ancient names she refuses to consciously speak, exhaustion tearing at the frayed edges of her mind, and the absolute, soul-rending terror of realizing that dying did not mean she was finished answering questions.

Kestrel rests near the edge of the dais, her plumage bristling with aristocratic violence. She tracks every minute flinch of the suspended woman, a low, rattling click vibrating in her throat whenever the captive’s pulse spikes. Vera Sorn steps forward, three heavy slate tablets tucked beneath one arm. Her face holds the unyielding severity of sharpened winter.

"My lord, the Hall is prepared," she announces, the words clipping sharply through the heavy, time-dilated air.

The Shadow-Tainted aura rolls outward in a suffocating wave, tempered only slightly by the ambient healing magic required to keep her shattered mind from fully collapsing under the immense strain. Movement stuttered violently between the hearing-related spaces and the lower holding pens. Disrupted expectations ripped through the enemy hierarchy, sparking vicious internal strife over what should have moved upward and what should have been destroyed. The collapse affected multiple subterranean layers simultaneously, turning a synchronized machine into a panicked, self-cannibalizing mob.

Lower attendants stopped trusting their peers. Route runners deferred upward in blind panic, abandoning their posts. A sudden, paralyzing fixation gripped the mid-level handlers regarding authorized movement:

*Wait for comparison.*

The phrase repeats endlessly in her fractured mind—a frantic, echoing mantra bearing undeniable weight. It is the procedural terror that bound their society together.

*The mother-line one.*

Not a title, but a relational tag born of pure, biological panic. Panic over the blood-truth.

*Which line? Cleared by whom? Who authorized carry?*

The hidden chain was never simple human trafficking or mundane vice. It was a massive evaluative architecture—a sprawling, subterranean preparation engine for blood-marked, answer-bearing individuals. They sorted genetic traits and lineages, hunting for specific souls that could answer inward under immense pressure without shattering into useless fragments. Public house lineage meant nothing to them; they distrusted the declared house story and tested the blood-truth directly. The mother-blood frequently carried the truest door, watched meticulously over decades, marked in the blood chapel with living wall-pages and the bleached bones of failed ascendants. Children and family lines were monitored over years, named and revisited later when the awakening arrived.

Maelin and Halren work the sprawling map tables, translating the psychic bleeding into a city-wide anatomy of concealment. Bren Issel places heavy iron markers, mapping the false-routing and deliberate queue poisoning. The strategy forms rapidly: weaponize the deception, fracture the upper chain with contradictory orders before reopening upward, and maintain the impenetrable seal on the throat of the tunnels.

On the recovery side of the Hall, Mera Sol and Sivra Denn sit wrapped in thick wool blankets. They eat slowly, chewing salted biscuits with mechanical focus, clawing their way back to personhood. They stare across the room at the evidence lane, helping to identify the exact gears of the machine that tried to erase their existence. A directional silence drops over the ward-circle, severing the acoustic link so sound from inside cannot escape. Each mind is peeled back, oriented, and catalogued against the master ledger. The Hall Return structure processes the living, the dead, and the anomalous remains with industrial indifference.

The focus shifts from the interrogation floor to the heavy, spell-locked isolation chamber in the deep background: the PINK Tier Lab, housing the living aberration.

The creature occupies a completely sealed arcane quarantine cell. It defies simple biological classification; it is not human, nor does it resemble any beast born under the sun. It is a mass of shifting, wet tissue—a localized biological contradiction cultivated within the dark confines of the blood chapel. Patches of pale human skin stretch painfully over rigid, asymmetrical chitinous bone structures. Clusters of mismatched, unblinking eyes—some human, some completely alien—weep a viscous gray fluid that sizzles faintly when it strikes the reinforced stone floor. The creature breathes in ragged, wet gasps, its flesh bubbling and constantly reshaping itself to match the fluctuating ambient magical frequencies of the containment cell.

It served the enemy as a nonhuman comparison subject—a doctrinal proof-organism, a heavily controlled anomaly in their twisted ascent taxonomy, used to calibrate the suffering of human captives.

Ordinary questioning remains entirely useless; the creature possesses no language, no structured thought, only a swirling storm of raw sensation and inherited agony.

Nonverbal first-contact initiates. The psionic connection bridges the gap, bypassing the need for a shared vocabulary. The mind of the aberration is a jagged, treacherous landscape composed of broken glass, screaming static, and blinding flashes of ancestral trauma.

The origin trace cross-references flawlessly against the blood chapel materials. The page-growth substrate—the terrifying living walls of the subterranean chapel that absorbed fresh blood and spat out the names of the chosen—echoes deeply in the creature's genetic memory. The aberration is a direct product of contradiction logic.

The enemy's core procedure relies entirely on forcing impossible paradoxes into the mind and body of the subject. The freeze-state formula reveals itself in the creature's physical trauma: *Anchor unresolved. Comparison spoiled. Do not carry.* The contradiction forces the biology to warp, creating a localized terror object that deliberately breaks the ascending chain, generating immense magical friction in the process. Contradiction-forcing remains one of the strongest procedural terror objects in the chain's twisted logic. Tokens, phrase strips, trays, packets, and bodies all share one single, bloody procedural grammar.

The upper layers of the enemy hierarchy fear contamination above all else; they escalate without descent, terrified of the ruined, broken things left rotting in the lower holding pens. The hearing-bodies—the unfortunate mortals physically modified to hear the deep chorus of the entity—are merely lower-chain instrument-bodies, discarded the instant they shatter under the sheer weight of the Listening.

The High Reckoning looms in the extracted data like a dark, crushing tide. It is an event—a harvest, the ultimate purpose of the viable souls and prepared bloodlines—hollowing out humanity to make room for something ancient and hungry.

Daelkyr authorship remains unproven. Null-Fang’s brutal assessment rings true in the telepathic link: this architecture lacks the alien perfection of the true Daelkyr. It feels entirely mortal—reeking of mortal arrogance, lesser beings constructing an ascent-engine from stolen, fragmented truths, butchering innocent lineages to build a door they do not truly deserve to open, playing with cosmic forces that view them as nothing more than kindling.

The aberration writhes in the containment cell—a grotesque victim of that mortal hubris. Its pain translates into raw telemetry. The page-growth substrate requires fresh human blood to function; the maternal anchors are relational and fear-topological, prioritizing the horror of the mother over the title of the house; the false-house contradiction triggers the automated purge.

The data integrates instantly into the active operational strategy. Updated sabotage templates form. Counter-ledger anchor-hypothesis rules lock into place. If the enemy relies entirely on strict procedural grammar to run their death-engine, that exact grammar can be lethally poisoned. A false token introduced into the blood chapel system will cause the comparison logic to devour itself, turning their own heavily warded architecture into a self-destruct mechanism.

Sel Veyran, Tobin Krail, Lysa Pellen, and two newly restored unknown humans wait in the stabilization wing, their identities verified against the purge lists. The children, the missing, the blurred-identity review—all city trace-back purges accelerate to recover remaining at-risk branches and hidden archive sites before the enemy can erase them. The auction settlement standard practice ensures all incoming consideration is itemized, scanned with psionic detection, appraised, and stamped, maintaining the release calendar without dropping a single coin. Mandatory disclosure forces the Sablehook Black Contingency Team to immediately report any cross-breeding or derivative work involving black or pink illithid-related items, locking the organization into absolute compliance.

## SECTION 2: PROJECT SECOND BITE

The logistics of the Hall demand constant attention, but the true crucible of the war effort lies deep within the beast-program: Project Second Bite—the Dire Displacer Rhino infrastructure.

The initiative shifted aggressively from doctrine-finalization into early execution preparation, moving with the absolute weight of sovereign construction priority. Rovan Pell, newly promoted to Head Foreman in Charge, oversees the massive overbuild package alongside Seris Vale’s provisional auxiliary core. Feed remains the primary bottleneck; Toman Hedd’s feed and protein structure barely manages to sustain the herd. A project of this biological magnitude requires staggering amounts of dense protein, and the supply lines groan under the constant demand of massive carnivores.

The true Stitchroom is no mere metaphor for the Hall above; it is a physical reality in the subterranean depths—a vast cavern dedicated entirely to flesh-weaving, forced evolution, and biological warfare. Black Lab Two operates as the primary testing ground.

The air in the Stitchroom hangs thick with the suffocating stench of copper, ozone, burnt hair, and raw, exposed meat. Heavy alchemical lamps cast a flickering green glare over damp stone walls. Immense, rust-pitted chains rattle against the vaulted ceiling, thick enough to anchor a warship. Reinforced iron cages line the perimeter, their bars scarred by brutal physical impacts and deep acidic burns from previous failures.

The visceral body-horror of the breeding program defies simple documentation.

Six massive Dire Rhinos form the foundation stock: the Caravan Line. Originally envisioned as the ultimate beasts of burden to secure trade routes, the project mutated into something far darker when the calculus of siege warfare hijacked the architectural blueprints.

They are no longer simple rhinos; they are forced hybrids.

The surgical process requires monstrous precision. The immense subjects are chained to the floor, restrained by heavy iron plates bolted into bedrock, and sedated with exotic paralytic venoms that freeze voluntary muscles while leaving the nervous system agonizingly awake and aware of every cut. The displacer-beast tissue, harvested at immense cost from the deep wilds, cannot simply be sewn onto hide like a patch; it must be woven directly into the marrow.

The grafting turns the Stitchroom into an industrial slaughterhouse. Teams of specialized healers stand by to forcefully regenerate torn flesh the instant foreign tissue takes root, trapping the beast in an endless cycle of mutilation and rapid healing. Acidic, glowing runes are burned into the rhinos' heavy musculature, creating permanent arcane anchor points for the displacer organs. Razor-tipped tentacles—the signature, deadly limbs of the displacer beast—are braided directly into the upper spinal column. The procedure forces the host's native nervous system to violently accept a completely alien spatial awareness.

Blood flows in dark rivers, washing over wide drainage grates. The beasts scream—a deep, chest-rattling bellow that shakes fine dust from the cavern ceiling and vibrates in the teeth of the handlers. The violent merging of two disparate evolutionary paths results in massive cellular rejection, localized necrosis, and sudden bone-growth that splinters through skin.

The first living proof of success stands trembling in the center pen: Proto-1.

Once a magnificent, reliable female Dire Rhino, she is now a towering engine of violence. Displacer tissue anchors along her muscled shoulders in thick plates of black, light-absorbing armor. Two massive, barbed tentacles rise above her forequarters, twitching with autonomous malice. When she draws a heavy breath, shadows around her warp and fracture. When she shifts her weight, the displacement illusion drags a fraction of a second behind—a shimmering, nausea-inducing afterimage that renders targeting her true center of mass impossible.

The tissue integration is a waking nightmare of biology. Her new spatial organs constantly feed her contradictory information: the displacer instinct urges her to view every shadow as an ambush, while her rhino heritage demands she lower her horn and charge the threat. The internal conflict manifests as pure rage. She snaps at phantom movements, her armored head swinging wildly, her tentacles lashing the stone floor and scoring deep gouges into masonry.

Darric clutches his wooden clipboard, his knuckles white. Hesta Bramblewake grips the heavy emergency kill-cord with terrified intensity, ready to drop the massive iron guillotine block suspended above the pen. Kessa monitors defensive wards from behind a pane of spell-hardened glass, her lips moving in silent prayer. Mirell, the kraken-kin scholar, watches from the aquatic tank connected to the laboratory, her massive, scarred tentacles shifting restlessly in the dark water.

Mental subjugation will solve the wrong problem. Mirell's internal notes remain clear on this point: dominating the beast's will through brute arcane force creates merely a temporary slave—a compressed spring waiting to snap back the moment the caster loses focus. Forcing obedience onto a creature experiencing constant spatial agony is a recipe for massacre.

The true solution lies in rank-cadence.

Psionic control applied not as a restrictive leash, but as a fundamental restructuring of the beast's inherent worldview—rewriting the definition of reality within the creature's mind.

Banki steps slowly toward the thick red salt line painted on the damp stone floor.

The working begins in silence. The Sovereign Thread weaves through the heavy air, glowing faintly with stolen heat. The magic does not seek to crush Proto-1's mind; it seeks the heavy, load-bearing tendons of her psyche. It bypasses surface rage, slides past the agonizing memory of surgical grafts, and taps directly into the deep-rooted herd memory encoded in her blood.

The psionic connection establishes a brutal hierarchy. Banki does not present himself as a human master; he presents himself as the absolute apex of the pack.

Concepts flow down the tether, translated into the beast's instinctual grammar:

*Hearth. Gate. Pack. Ward.*

Proto-1 lunges violently, slamming her bulk against the iron gate. The reinforced bars groan; stone walls shudder. Her newly grafted tentacles lash out blindly, seeking the unseen source of psychic pressure. The displacement illusion warps the air, hiding the trajectory of her strikes. One barbed limb hooks empty air; the other strikes the floor with enough force to shatter thick shock-absorbing tiles. Dust blooms in the air.

Kestrel’s mind slides into the psychic link, sharp as a winter blade. *Do not force calm. Establish rank.*

The rank-cadence pushes deeper into gray matter, organizing the chaotic static inside the rhino's skull. Displacer tissue is no longer perceived as a foreign parasite; it is reclassified as a tool for the survival of the pack. Spatial distortion is no longer a lie; it is a defensive shield.

*Probe. Brace the load. Catch the strike. Redirect the force.*

Proto-1 abruptly halts. White steam plumes from her flared nostrils. The shimmering afterimage stabilizes rapidly, locking into a controlled distortion. The frantic battering ceases. The beast stands ready, muscles coiled, blood dripping slowly from her altered shoulders, awaiting the pack-leader's explicit direction.

The child-shape test commences immediately.

Darric pulls the heavy lever. The winch releases. A child-sized heat dummy rolls smoothly into the outer testing lane.

The beast's predatory override engages instantly. The target is small, warm, vulnerable. The grafted displacer instinct screams for the easy kill. Massive tentacles snap open, barbed hooks spreading wide to crush the dummy into paste. Heavy muscles in her hind legs bunch, preparing for the devastating charge that will splatter the target across glass.

The Sovereign Thread pulses with an absolute command, overriding biology with hierarchical law:

*Warm-small does not equal prey. Ward.*

The tension in the observation deck becomes suffocating. The room holds its collective breath.

The tentacle slams flat against the stone floor, rigid as a forged iron bar, forming an impenetrable defensive barricade directly in front of the heat dummy. Proto-1 shifts her bulk with surprising grace, deliberately interposing her armored body between the child-shape and the rest of the room. She assumes a protective stance, her displacement field widening to completely conceal the vulnerable target from sight. Her head lowers, the massive horn pointed directly at the handlers behind the glass, entirely willing to gut anyone who approaches the protected shape.

Protector-grade cognitive hybrid.

The success is undeniable, heavy, and profound. But the cost remains exorbitant, the failures permanently scarring everyone involved in the breeding program.

Ash-Halter: the dark memory of the failed subject burns in the ledger. She was a patient beast selected specifically for the Caravan Line. The military graft took too deeply, mutating her personality. The displacer tissue violently rejected the gentle touch required to fit a harness. Her grafted tentacles barricaded the outer gate magnificently against intruders, but any attempt by a handler to secure a riding saddle resulted in violent, murderous spasms. The beast literally tore herself apart from the inside, her augmented nervous system screaming in excruciating static. The physical rejection caused the displacer tissue to attack the rhino host, the tentacles attempting to rip the beast's own throat out to halt the pain.

The horrific sound she made when merely attempting to stand still haunts the veteran handlers to this day.

The Stitchroom is a crucible of severe consequences. Success does not guarantee safety. The living components learn the concept of lethal pressure far faster than inert frames. A massive grapnel fired a fraction of a second early during a stress test, crushing a handler's hand into bloody pulp. Banki absorbed the exorbitant cost of healing mages, pouring his own vitality into the spell to reconstruct the ruined fingers perfectly, while the beast simply waited, executing its design without remorse. The beast did not understand the error because, structurally, there was no error. The flesh simply failed to withstand the mechanics.

Breeding is strictly prohibited until behavioral refinement is absolute.

The Dire Displacer Rhino project advances relentlessly into early execution preparation. The beasts will guard supply lines; they will shatter enemy formations. But they will do so entirely because the psionic rank-cadence forces them to view the kingdom as their personal herd, and fragile citizens as the warm-small targets they must fight to the death to protect.

## SECTION 3: THE EXTRACTION OF MEMORY

The absolute horror of the Stitchroom extends far beyond beasts; it applies equally to human enemies of the Hall.

Odran Vey sits strapped tightly to a rusted iron chair in the secondary processing chamber, a leather mouth-guard forced past his teeth. He sold names to the deep chorus for six months, justifying betrayals as simple survival. He provided intelligence that allowed the blood chapel to hunt the innocent; he pointed hunters toward mother-lines.

The interrogation does not rely on crude physical torture; the invasive magic possesses chilling surgical elegance. Liquid silver filaments slide down the psionic tether, bypassing mental shields entirely. The magic sinks into the motor-cortex, seeking mental knots where valuable secrets are stored: the complex lock-picking reflex, whispered countersigns, the instinctual flinch that signals a lie.

The extraction hollows him out completely—subtraction performed with razor-sharp psychic instruments, infinitely crueller than mere forgetting. Memories unravel rapidly from his tendons, rushing up glowing ethereal threads into Banki's waiting grasp. Odran suddenly forgets how to pick a canal lock; he completely forgets winding alleyway routes. He desperately tries to hold onto the memory of his mother's face, using it as a shield, but the silver thread bypasses sentiment entirely and rips away his ability to recognize human affection.

The physical memory of his betrayal is fully harvested. His right hand twitches against the leather strap, abruptly forgetting why fingers possess joints. The loss arrives as a shining line of subtracted skill. Each extraction hollows him further, leaving him present in the room but increasingly uninhabited—a vast house whose doors have been thrown wide, its furniture stolen, its empty rooms swept clean of dust.

Through the haze of violation, Odran stares at Banki's motionless face.

The complete lack of expression renders the agony infinitely worse. It is the detached focus of a craftsman shaping wood. Banki does not hate him; Banki simply requires the materials stored inside his skull.

When the glowing filaments finally withdraw, Odran remains breathing in the iron chair. His lungs expand with the memory of labor; his heart pounds a steady rhythm. His eyes track green light reflecting off stone ceiling tiles. He opens his jaw against the mouth-guard, entirely unable to remember why his throat feels raw from screaming.

Banki stands over the ruined man, flexing newly acquired knowledge, organizing stolen skills within his mind. A complex canal lock clicks open in his imagination, and he hums a Choir countersign flawlessly, using the exact cadence of Odran's stolen voice.

"Archive him," Banki orders the guards, turning away without a second glance.

The Hall remains the beating heart; the Stitchroom remains the unforgiving forge. The brutal war against the hidden chain, the deep Listening, and the looming High Reckoning will be fought with stitched flesh, stolen memories, and the crushing weight of sovereign time. Every horrific compromise made in the dark below serves to keep the sunlit world above completely ignorant of the true cost of survival. The Dire Rhinos will bleed. The captives will shatter. The ledgers will balance.

And Banki will continue to weave the Sovereign Thread through the madness, establishing rank in a world desperate for a master.
