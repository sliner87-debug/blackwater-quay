# BLACKWATER QUAY
## Master Graphic Novel Series Outline & Pitch Document

### LOGLINE
In a subterranean, dystopian harbor city built around a massive psychic crystal holding back a realm of madness, a ruthless smuggler and her shapeshifting fey enforcer must defend their syndicate from rogue biomancers, religious inquisitors, and the creeping static of an alien mind.

### THE PITCH
*Blackwater Quay* is a 6-Volume dark fantasy/cyberpunk-adjacent comic series. It explores the intersection of gritty organized crime, eldritch horror, and systemic corruption. Think *Peaky Blinders* meets *Lovecraftian Horror* in a subterranean *Blade Runner* setting. The narrative centers on Sablehook, a smuggling syndicate that relies on a massive anti-divination ward to hide illicit magic and unregulated technology from the fanatical surface-dwelling Templars.

---

### STRUCTURAL BREAKDOWN
**Total Length:** 6 Volumes (Trade Paperbacks)
**Issue Structure:** Standard 22-page issues, 5-6 issues per Volume.
**Art Direction:** High-contrast, gritty inks with a muted, cold color palette (blues, greys, stark blacks) punctuated by visceral, glowing neons (violet for Aether-Sight, sickly green for biomancy).

## VOLUME 1: The Lantern That Did Not Cast Light 
**(Issues 1-6)**

**A-Plot (The Syndicate Defense):** Sable and Banki discover a plot by the Choir of the Below (a psychic cult) to sabotage the Tri-Weave Shroud—the very magic that keeps Blackwater Quay hidden from the Templars. They must secure their territory and eliminate the Saboteur.
**B-Plot (The Fey Identity):** Banki’s struggle to maintain his human facade while his ancient, ruthless fey nature begins to bleed through as the stakes escalate.

*   **Issue #1 (Pages 1-22): The Shape of a Lie**
    *   **Action:** Introduces Sable and Banki orchestrating a brutal intelligence extraction on the Greybridge road. Establishes Banki's Aether-Sight and fey origins.
    *   **Pacing:** Ends on a splash page cliffhanger revealing the sprawling, subterranean nightmare of Blackwater Quay.
*   **Issue #2 (Pages 23-44): The Choir of Frightened Minds**
    *   **Action:** Introduction to the Quay's factions. The Choir of the Below launches a sudden, violent strike on the Sluices (The Kill Lane).
    *   **Pacing:** Action-heavy. Establishes Sable’s sheer ruthlessness in combat.
*   **Issue #3 (Pages 45-66): The Saboteur’s Thoughts**
    *   **Action:** A detective/noir procedural issue. Banki uses Mind-Sense to interrogate captives. They track the source of the attack to the Dead-Drop Chapel.
*   **Issue #4 (Pages 67-88): The Signature That Bled**
    *   **Action:** Infiltration of the Chapel. They discover the Saboteur is tapping into the Elder Node’s psychic static to unravel the Shroud.
*   **Issue #5 (Pages 89-110): A Knife Wakes Sharp**
    *   **Action:** The assassination attempt on the Saboteur goes wrong. A brutal, close-quarters fight in the flooded counting rooms.
*   **Issue #6 (Pages 111-132): Sable's Sorting**
    *   **Action:** Sable secures the Shroud but realizes the Choir was just a proxy for a much larger threat.
    *   **Cliffhanger:** The Elder Node pulses, cracking the sky of the cavern.

## VOLUME 2: The Night Market (Issues 7-12)
*Focuses on the Belowmarket economy, introducing the merchants, the Rank Trials against rival gangs, and the arrival of Kael (The Planar Compass).*

## VOLUME 3: The Ironhorn Passage (Issues 13-18)
*A wilderness survival/heist arc. The syndicate must transport a high-value asset through the mutated Brinewood, culminating in an infiltration of a Thessalan Consortium flesh-auction.*

## VOLUME 4: Project Second Bite (Issues 19-24)
*Body-horror and biological warfare. Suture takes center stage as the syndicate uncovers the Consortium's plan to clone an avatar of the Elder Node.*

## VOLUME 5: The Door Marked Reconciliation (Issues 25-30)
*Psychological horror. The syndicate is forced to delve into the deepest, madness-infected layers of the Quay to secure an alliance with the Lords of Dust. Contingency Black is enacted.*

## VOLUME 6: The Sovereign's Choice (Issues 31-36)
*The Climax. The Templars breach the Shroud. A massive, cinematic war arc defending Sablehook, culminating in a reality-bending showdown at the Rift Core.*
