### OVERVIEW
The surface is blind. They look at the sinkholes in the Greybridge district and see only poor engineering. But the rot goes deeper than cracked foundations. For three months, I have embedded myself in the lower sumps, tracking the movement of illicit magical components. The trail does not lead out of the city. It leads *down*.

There is a thriving, unregulated harbor built into the bedrock beneath us. The locals call it **Blackwater Quay**.

### THE TRI-WEAVE SHROUD
My attempts to scry the location of the Quay have met with catastrophic failure. Acolyte Renis attempted a standard *Clairvoyance* ritual yesterday. The feedback shattered his divination crystal and ruptured the blood vessels in his left eye. He babbled about "static" and "stained glass wings."

We are not dealing with a localized ward. The Quay is protected by a city-spanning anti-divination lattice. The smugglers refer to it as **The Tri-Weave Shroud**. It acts as a psychic Faraday cage. Whoever built it has an understanding of aetheric mechanics that borders on the heretical. We cannot see them, but I fear they can see us.

### THE ELDER NODE (UNCONFIRMED)
The most disturbing rumor pertains to the power source of the Shroud. Informants speak in hushed, terrified tones of a massive crystalline structure at the deepest point of the Quay—an **Elder Node**. 

If the ancient texts are correct, these nodes are not just batteries. They are *scars*. Plugs meant to hold shut dimensional tears. The locals claim the crystal "leaks static," causing spontaneous mutations and psychic bleeding among the populace. If the smugglers are tapping into this anomaly to power their Shroud, they risk unraveling the spatial integrity of the entire region.

### THE SYNDICATE
The primary black-market operation is known as **Sablehook**. They control the smuggling routes via lead-lined barges that evade our detection grids. They are led by a human woman named Sable, described as ruthless and efficient. 

However, my primary concern is her chief enforcer. 

He operates under the alias "Banki." Witnesses describe him as human, but the psychic footprint he leaves behind is ancient, cold, and utterly terrifying. He is the architect of the Shroud. I believe he is not human at all, but a creature of the old magic—perhaps a fey entity that survived the Cleansing by hiding in a mortal skin.
