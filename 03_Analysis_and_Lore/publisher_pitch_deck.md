# BLACKWATER QUAY
## Publisher Pitch Deck

**Creator:** Antigravity 
**Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Cyberpunk-Adjacent / Crime Noir
**Format:** 6-Volume Graphic Novel Series (Standard 22-page issues)
**Audience Comps:** *Monstress* meets *Peaky Blinders* in a subterranean *Blade Runner* setting.

---

### THE LOGLINE
In a dystopian underground harbor city built around a massive psychic crystal holding back a realm of madness, a ruthless smuggler and her shapeshifting fey enforcer must defend their syndicate from rogue biomancers, religious inquisitors, and the creeping static of an alien mind.

### THE SYNOPSIS (Volume 1)
Blackwater Quay is a city that shouldn't exist. Built in the cavernous bedrock beneath the civilised world, it is a haven for illicit magic, black-market technology, and outcasts. It remains hidden only because of the **Tri-Weave Shroud**, a massive anti-divination ward maintained by a smuggling syndicate called Sablehook.

But the Shroud is failing. 

When Sable (a ruthless syndicate boss) and Banki (a terrifyingly ancient fey wearing a human skin) uncover a plot to sabotage the Shroud, they realize they aren't just fighting rival gangs. The sabotage is connected to the **Elder Node**—the colossal, sentient crystal at the heart of the city that is slowly leaking psychic static into the minds of the populace. If the Shroud falls, the fanatical surface Templars will burn the city to the ground. If the Node shatters, the dimensional rift it seals will consume reality itself.

### WHY IT WORKS
*   **A Unique Setting:** Blackwater Quay merges the grit of organized crime syndicates (smuggling routes, territory wars, black markets) with the aesthetics of high-concept dark fantasy (fleshwarped biomancers, planar bleeding, marrow-magic).
*   **Deep Lore via Backmatter:** Inspired by *Saga* and *DIE*, the worldbuilding is expansive but presented seamlessly through end-of-issue "Backmatter" (Inquisition reports, merchant ledgers, medical notes), keeping the comic script fast-paced and action-heavy.
*   **Visceral Art Direction:** High-contrast inks, muted cold palettes (greys, deep blues), punctuated by glowing neons representing different magics (violet for aether, sickly green for biomancy).

### ISSUE #1 VARIANT COVERS STRATEGY

To maximize initial sales and collector engagement, Issue #1 will launch with a three-cover strategy:

*   **Cover A (Standard Trade Dress):**
    *   *Concept:* A cinematic, wide shot of Blackwater Quay. Sable and Banki stand on a rain-slicked iron gantry overlooking the neon-lit, subterranean harbor, the glowing violet Elder Node looming ominously in the deep background.
*   **Cover B (Character Focus / Foil Variant):**
    *   *Concept:* A split-composition portrait of Banki. The left half shows his weary human disguise; the right half reveals his terrifying, stained-glass winged fey true form. The title logo is stamped in metallic violet foil.
*   **Cover C (Virgin Art / 1:25 Incentive):**
    *   *Concept:* Pure, textless environmental art. The grisly aftermath of marrow-magic combat in the Belowmarket, highlighting the dark, gritty, mature tone of the series. Designed specifically for collectors.
