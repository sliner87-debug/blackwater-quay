# Act III: Crimson Sails Over the Quay

**Campaign Level:** 12 (Milestone to 13 at completion)
**Setting:** The Harbor Vaults of Sablehook, Blackwater Quay

---

## Adventure Overview
A Githyanki raiding fleet, allied with the Elder Node, attacks Blackwater Quay. Their objective is to hunt down the deserter Zaniph, the rift-sealer Suture, and the "aberrant" fey pirate Kael. 

Act III is a massive **Event-Based Siege Module**. The party must defend Sablehook's walls, accumulate Victory Points (VP) through tactical decisions across a sprawling battlefield, and execute a daring boarding action against the Githyanki flagship, *Vlaakith's Pride*. The objective is to steal its core component—a silver greatsword—and use it to trigger the **Tri-Weave Shroud** ritual.

---

## Phase 1: The Sky Cracks (Battlefield Sandbox)

The ceiling of the subterranean harbor cracks as three massive Githyanki spelljamming galleons break through the upper rock strata. Their silver hulls catch the green glow of the quay's alchemical lights, and psychic fire rains down.

The party has **30 minutes (in-game)** before the flagship docks. They can tackle **two** of the following three battlefield nodes to accumulate Victory Points.

### Node A: The Harbor Ballistas (Artillery Defense)
*   **The Situation:** Githyanki raiders are swarming the primary defense cannons to prevent Sablehook from firing back.
*   **Encounter (CR 12):** 4x Githyanki Raiders (Fighters), 1x Red Dragon Wyrmling.
*   **Tactics:** The raiders try to sabotage the winch gears (Disable Device checks) while the wyrmling strafes the party with fire breath.
*   **Victory (+2 VP):** If the party clears the wall within 5 rounds, the ballistas fire, destroying one of the smaller galleon's main cannons and preventing orbital bombardment in Phase 2.

### Node B: The Civilian Bunkers (Rescue)
*   **The Situation:** Invisible stalkers have slipped past the frontline and are hunting refugees in the dark alleys of the lower market.
*   **Encounter (CR 11):** 3x Astral Stalkers (invisible assassins).
*   **Hazard - Collapsing Architecture:** Every round on Initiative 10, debris falls from the cracking cavern ceiling. (Reflex DC 15 or take 3d6 bludgeoning damage).
*   **Victory (+2 VP):** If the party defeats the stalkers before they kill 3 refugees, they save the merchants. A grateful alchemist gifts the party a *Potion of Haste* and a *Potion of Superior Healing*.

### Node C: The Null-Steel Vault (Sabotage)
*   **The Situation:** Templar Inquisitors, taking advantage of the chaos, are trying to steal Sablehook's cache of Null-Steel.
*   **Encounter (CR 12):** 2x Templar Knights, 1x Inquisitor-Captain.
*   **The Moral Choice:** The party can fight the Templars to keep the steel for the Syndicate, OR let the Templars take it in exchange for the Templars providing covering fire against the Githyanki during Phase 2. 
*   **Victory (+1 VP for Sablehook, or +2 VP for Templar Alliance).**

## Phase 2: Flagship Boarding Action (Dungeon Crawl)

To power the Tri-Weave Shroud, Banki needs an astral conduit. Kael rams the *Onyx Wake* into the side of the Githyanki flagship, *Vlaakith's Pride*, creating a bridge of splintered wood and groaning metal. The party must cross the gap and fight their way to the command deck.

### The Chasm Crossing (Hazard)
*   **Sensory:** The roar of the red dragon overhead, the sickening crunch of timber, and the ozone smell of psychic evocation.
*   **Mechanic:** The gap between the ships is 15 feet wide, with a 200-foot drop. The ships grind together. Every round on Initiative 0, combatants must succeed on a **DC 15 Acrobatics/Balance** check or fall prone.

### The Lower Decks (Encounter)
*   **Description:** The teleportation bays of the flagship. A massive astral astrolabe spins in the center of the room.
*   **Encounter:** 4x Githyanki Gish (Eldritch Knights).
*   **Interactive Terrain:** The astrolabe pulses with gravity magic. Anyone pushed into the astrolabe is teleported 30 feet in a random direction and takes 2d6 force damage.

### The Command Deck (Boss Encounter)
*   **Boss Profile: Commander K'thrall (CR 13)**
    *   **NPC Profile:** K'thrall (Level 11 Fighter / 2 Blackguard) is a fanatic loyal to Vlaakith. He views the party as disgusting aberrations to be purged.
    *   **Tactics:** K'thrall wields a *+2 Silver Greatsword* (destined to become the Tri-Weave Blade). He focuses entirely on the PCs, trying to sunder their weapons and push them off the shifting deck into the chasm. 
    *   **Enemies:** Commander K'thrall, 2x Githyanki Knights.

## Phase 3: The Shroud Core (Defend the Ritual)

With the silver sword secured, the party retreats to the Sablehook vaults where Banki and Suture initiate the Shroud ritual. However, astral stalkers have breached the inner sanctum.

### The Defense Mechanic (CR 13)
*   **Enemies:** 3x Astral Stalkers, 2x Githyanki Assassins.
*   **Objective:** Keep the enemies away from Banki and Suture for **5 rounds**. 
*   **Mechanic:** Banki and Suture have an AC of 12 and 50 HP each. For every hit a ritualist takes, the Shroud's power drops by 10%. If it drops below 50%, the party loses -2 VP. If a ritualist dies, the campaign enters the "Bad Ending" route.
*   **Success:** On Round 5, the Tri-Weave Blade ignites, casting a massive anti-magic, anti-psionic dome over Blackwater Quay. The Githyanki ships are instantly severed from the Astral Plane and crash into the harbor.

## Siege Resolution & Victory Points (VP)

Tally the Victory Points accumulated during the siege (Max possible: 4 from Phase 1, minus any penalties in Phase 3). The final score determines the condition of Sablehook going into Act IV.

*   **4 VP (Epic Victory):** Sablehook stands tall. The Tri-Weave Shroud works at 100% efficiency. The players begin Act IV with full supplies, a +2 bonus on all faction renown tracks, and a **Chronal Chronometer** gifted by Kael.
*   **2–3 VP (Pyrrhic Victory):** Sablehook is damaged. Several of Kael's crew are wounded. The players must spend 3,000 gp in supplies to repair the docks.
*   **1 or less VP (Disastrous Retreat):** Sablehook is overrun. The harbor burns. Survivors retreat into the deep caverns. In Act IV, all characters start with a permanent *fatigued* condition due to sleep deprivation and minor injuries.

**Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 13**.
**Loot:** *+2 Silver Greatsword* (The Tri-Weave Blade), *Githyanki Half-Plate*, 4,000 gp in astral gems.

## DM Tools & Sidebars

> [!WARNING]
> **Pacing the Siege**
> Keep the action moving at a breakneck pace. Give the players only a few seconds in real life to make tactical decisions about where to deploy their forces (like choosing between the ballistas and the bunkers). Use the sounds of shattering stone, roaring dragons, and screaming corsairs to heighten the tension. There is no time to rest in this module.

> [!NOTE]
> **Failing Forward (Ritual Interruption)**
> If the ritualists take too much damage during Phase 3, the Shroud is not completely ruined, but it becomes *unstable*. In Act IV, the party will occasionally flicker back into the Elder Node's vision, resulting in random combat encounters and penalties to stealth.
