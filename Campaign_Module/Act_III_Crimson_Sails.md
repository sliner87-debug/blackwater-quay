# Act III: Crimson Sails Over the Quay

**Campaign Level:** 12 (Milestone to 13 at completion)
**Setting:** The Harbor Vaults of Sablehook, Blackwater Quay

---

## Adventure Overview
A Githyanki raiding fleet, allied with the Elder Node, attacks Blackwater Quay. Their objective is to hunt down the deserter Zaniph, the rift-sealer Suture, and the "aberrant" fey pirate Kael. Act III is an **Event-Based Siege Module**. The party must defend Sablehook's walls, accumulate Victory Points (VP) through tactical decisions, and execute a daring boarding action against the Githyanki flagship, *Vlaakith's Pride*, to steal its core component—a silver sword—and trigger the **Tri-Weave Shroud**.

---

## The Siege of Sablehook (Event Phases)

### Phase 1: The Sky Cracks
The ceiling of the subterranean harbor cracks as three massive Githyanki spelljamming galleons break through the upper rock strata. Their silver hulls catch the green glow of the quay's alchemical lights, and psychic fire rains down.

**Event Choice:** The party must choose whether to **(A) Man the Harbor Ballistas** or **(B) Defend the Civilian Bunkers**.
*   **If A:** They face 4x Githyanki Raiders and a Red Dragon Wyrmling on the walls. If victorious, they destroy one galleon's main cannon (+2 VP).
*   **If B:** They face 3x Astral Stalkers hunting refugees in the dark. If victorious, they save Sablehook's merchants (+2 VP) and are gifted a *Potion of Haste*.

**Environmental Hazard: Psychic Bombardment**
At the top of every round (Initiative 20) during Phase 1, a random 15-ft radius area is struck by astral fire (**4d6 fire / 4d6 psychic damage**, Reflex DC 18 half).

### Phase 2: Flagship Boarding Action
To power the Tri-Weave Shroud, Banki needs an astral conduit. Kael rams the *Onyx Wake* into the side of the Githyanki flagship, *Vlaakith's Pride*, creating a bridge of splintered wood and groaning metal. 

*   **Sensory:** The roar of the red dragon overhead, the sickening crunch of timber, and the ozone smell of psychic evocation.
*   **Hazard - The Chasm:** The gap between the ships is 15 feet wide, with a 200-foot drop. 
*   **Hazard - Shifting Decks:** The ships grind together. Every round on Initiative 0, combatants must succeed on a **DC 15 Acrobatics/Balance** check or fall prone.

**Encounter (CR 13): Commander K'thrall**
*   **NPC Profile:** K'thrall (Level 11 Fighter / 2 Blackguard) is a fanatic loyal to Vlaakith. He views the party as disgusting aberrations to be purged.
*   **Tactics:** K'thrall wields a *+2 Silver Greatsword* (destined to become the Tri-Weave Blade). He focuses entirely on the PCs, trying to sunder their weapons and push them off the shifting deck into the chasm. He is accompanied by 3x Githyanki Knights.

---

## Phase 3: The Shroud Core (Defend the Ritual)
With the silver sword secured, the party retreats to the Sablehook vaults where Banki and Suture initiate the Shroud ritual. However, astral stalkers have breached the inner sanctum.

**The Defense Mechanic (CR 13)**
*   **Enemies:** 3x Astral Stalkers (invisible, highly mobile assassins).
*   **Objective:** Keep the stalkers away from Banki and Suture for **5 rounds**. 
*   **Consequence:** For every hit a ritualist takes, the Shroud's power drops by 10%. If it drops below 50%, the party loses -2 VP.

---

## Siege Resolution & Victory Points (VP)

Tally the Victory Points accumulated during the siege. The final score determines the condition of Sablehook going into Act IV.

*   **11+ VP (Epic Victory):** Sablehook stands tall. The Tri-Weave Shroud works at 100% efficiency. The players begin Act IV with full supplies, a +2 bonus on all faction renown tracks, and a **Chronal Chronometer** gifted by Kael.
*   **7–10 VP (Pyrrhic Victory):** Sablehook is damaged. Several of Kael's crew are wounded. The players must spend 3,000 gp in supplies to repair the docks.
*   **6 or less VP (Disastrous Retreat):** Sablehook is overrun. The harbor burns. Survivors retreat into the deep caverns. In Act IV, all characters start with a permanent *fatigued* condition due to sleep deprivation and minor injuries.

**Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 13**.

---

## DM Tools & Sidebars

**Sidebar 1: Pacing the Siege**
Keep the action moving at a breakneck pace. Give the players only a few seconds in real life to make tactical decisions about where to deploy their forces (like choosing between the ballistas and the bunkers). Use the sounds of shattering stone, roaring dragons, and screaming corsairs to heighten the tension. There is no time to rest in this module.

**Sidebar 2: Failing Forward (Ritual Interruption)**
If the ritualists take too much damage during Phase 3, the Shroud is not completely ruined, but it becomes *unstable*. In Act IV, the party will occasionally flicker back into the Elder Node's vision, resulting in random combat encounters and penalties to stealth.
