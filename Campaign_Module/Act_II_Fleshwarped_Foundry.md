# Act II: The Fleshwarped Foundry

**Campaign Level:** 11 (Milestone to 12 at completion)
**Setting:** Belowmarket Deep, Blackwater Quay

---

## Adventure Overview
Following the ledger found in Act I, the party discovers that the **Thessalan Consortium** has established a secret "Fleshwarped Foundry" beneath the bustling Belowmarket Deep. They are breeding mutated beasts and refining the chemical compounds for the Elder Node. Act II is a **Dungeon Crawl with multiple entry points**. The party must coordinate a diversionary surface riot with the Tarkanan Syndicate while infiltrating the foundry to destroy the growth vats and secure the chemical formulas.

---

## The Foundry Layout (Dynamic Dungeon)

### 1. The Canal Gates (The Front Door)
A dark basalt fortress looming over green-sludge canal vectors. Heavy copper pipes pulse with glowing chemical slurries.
*   **Sensory:** The air tastes of copper and burnt hair. Steam whistles from venting valves, echoing like the breathing of a massive beast.
*   **Hazards:** 15-ft wide channels of highly toxic runoff. Swimming requires a **DC 15 Swim check**, and contact deals **2d6 acid damage** per round.
*   **Encounter (CR 11):** 4x Consortium Guards (Alchemist/Fighters), 1x Mutated Sea-Wolf hiding in the water.
*   **Interactive Terrain:** Shooting the overhead pipes sprays acidic steam on the catwalks (2d6 acid damage, Reflex DC 15 negates).

### 2. The Holding Pens (The Vent Route)
If the party takes the vents, they drop into the holding pens where failed experiments are kept.
*   **Sensory:** Complete darkness, punctuated by pathetic whimpers and the scraping of claws on iron. Smells of rotting meat.
*   **Encounter:** 2x Thessalan Displacer Beasts (starving and feral).

---

## 3. The Vat Room (The Climax)
The core of the foundry. Massive glass cylinders filled with amniotic fluid contain half-formed monstrosities.

### Encounter: Vespera's Stand (CR 12)
**NPC Profile: Vespera the Fleshwarper**
*   **Motivation:** Obsessed with perfecting the fusion of fey and aberration biology. Views the PCs as fascinating test subjects.
*   **Flaw:** Arrogant and prone to monologuing. She will waste a round taking notes on a PC's unique racial abilities if they display them.
*   **Enemies:** Vespera (Level 12 Alchemist/Wizard), 1x Flesh Golem Prototype.
*   **Tactics:** Vespera drinks a mutagen on round 1, gaining massive physical buffs (+4 Str/Con, +2 AC). She uses *Bombs* to intentionally shatter vats near the PCs.

### Environmental Hazard: Shattering Vats
The room is filled with large glass cylinders. Hitting a canister (AC 10, HP 15) shatters it, releasing green acid in a 10-ft radius (**3d6 acid damage**, Reflex DC 15 half) and spawning a **Mutated Crawling Claw** swarm from the aborted flesh within.

---

## Resolution & Rewards
Behind the Vat Room lies the Archives. Securing the crystalline drives inside reveals the **Formula of Thessalan Suppressants**. Providing this to Suture back at Sablehook allows him to brew anti-compliance mutagens for the party, granting them immunity to the Elder Node's ambient psychic whispers in Act IV.

**Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 12**.
**Loot:** 18,000 gp in alchemical components, 1x *Amulet of Natural Armor +3*, and the *Formula of Thessalan Suppressants*.

## DM Tools & Sidebars

**Sidebar 1: Infiltration Options**
Present the players with three ways into the Foundry rather than forcing a frontal assault:
1. **The Front Gate:** A brutal frontal assault over the toxic canal vectors.
2. **The Maintenance Vents:** A stealthy skill-challenge through rusted, acid-dripping pipes (requires Disable Device and Escape Artist).
3. **The Disguise Route:** Ambushing a patrol and wearing Consortium uniforms to bluff past the outer guards (requires Bluff/Disguise).

**Sidebar 2: Failing Forward in the Foundry**
If the party is defeated anywhere in the Foundry, they do not suffer a TPK. Instead, they wake up in the Holding Pens, stripped of their gear. They must break out using improvised weapons and recover their items from the nearby guard room locker.

**Sidebar 3: Alert Level Mechanics**
The Consortium operates on an alert system. If a loud explosion occurs or a guard uses a sounding horn, the Alert Level increases:
*   **Low:** Standard patrols. 
*   **Medium (1 Alarm):** Guards actively search. +2 Perception to all enemies.
*   **High (2 Alarms):** The Foundry goes into lockdown. Iron bulkheads seal the main doors (Hardness 10, HP 100), and Vespera is warned of the party's approach, pre-buffing before the Vat Room fight.
