# Act II: The Fleshwarped Foundry

**Campaign Level:** 11 (Milestone to 12 at completion)
**Setting:** Belowmarket Deep, Blackwater Quay

---

## Adventure Overview
Following the ledger found in Act I, the party discovers that the **Thessalan Consortium** has established a secret "Fleshwarped Foundry" beneath the bustling commerce of the Belowmarket Deep. They are breeding mutated beasts and refining the chemical compounds for the Elder Node's telepathic network. 

Act II is a **Dynamic Infiltration Sandbox** followed by a **High-Stakes Dungeon Crawl**. The party must first choose how to infiltrate the facility (or coordinate a diversionary surface riot), navigate the Foundry's security systems, and finally destroy the growth vats and secure the chemical formulas.

---

## Part 1: The Infiltration (Sandbox)

The entrance to the Foundry is hidden behind a massive adamantine bulkhead in the flooded lower levels of the market. The party must choose one of three distinct approaches.

### Approach A: The Front Gate (Assault)
A brutal frontal assault over toxic canal vectors.
*   **Sensory Details:** The air tastes of copper and burnt hair. Steam whistles from venting valves, echoing like the breathing of a massive beast.
*   **Hazards:** 15-ft wide channels of highly toxic runoff. Swimming requires a **DC 15 Swim check**, and contact deals **2d6 acid damage** per round.
*   **Encounter (CR 11):** 4x Consortium Guards (Alchemist/Fighters), 1x Mutated Sea-Wolf hiding in the water.
*   **Interactive Terrain:** Shooting the overhead pipes sprays acidic steam on the catwalks (2d6 acid damage, Reflex DC 15 negates).
*   **Consequence:** A loud firefight here automatically raises the Foundry's Alert Level to Medium (see DM Tools).

### Approach B: The Maintenance Vents (Stealth)
A stealthy skill-challenge through rusted, acid-dripping pipes that bypasses the front door.
*   **Sensory Details:** Claustrophobic, echoing metal. The smell of rust and decaying organic matter is overpowering.
*   **The Skill Challenge:** The party must accumulate 3 successes before 3 failures using **Disable Device (DC 18)** to bypass rusted fans, **Escape Artist (DC 16)** to squeeze through collapsed vents, or **Stealth (DC 20)** to avoid the acoustic sensors.
*   **Consequence:** If they fail the challenge, they fall through a weakened grate directly into Room 2 (The Holding Pens) and suffer 2d6 falling damage. If they succeed, they drop silently into Room 1 (Decontamination).

### Approach C: The Disguise Route (Subterfuge)
Ambushing a supply patrol in the market above and wearing Consortium uniforms to bluff past the outer guards.
*   **Encounter (CR 8):** A quick, silent ambush against 2 Consortium Couriers in a back alley. If they escape, the gig is up.
*   **The Bluff:** Walking through the front gate requires a **DC 20 Disguise** check and a **DC 18 Bluff** check when challenged by the gate captain. Having the *Specimen* or *Stitch-Hand* faction perk grants Advantage on these rolls.
*   **Consequence:** Success allows the party to walk freely through Room 1 and Room 2 without rolling initiative, provided they don't do anything suspicious.

---

## Part 2: The Foundry Interior (Dungeon Crawl)

Once inside, the Foundry operates on an **Alert System** (see DM Tools). 

### Room 1: Decontamination Chambers
*   **Description:** A sterile, white-tiled room filled with hissing alchemical shower-heads. The floor is slightly sloped toward central drains.
*   **Trap - The Acid Wash (CR 8):** If the alarm is raised, the doors lock (Disable Device DC 25 to open) and the showers spray concentrated acid. Everyone inside takes 4d6 acid damage per round (Reflex DC 16 half) for 3 rounds.
*   **Investigation:** A desk in the corner contains a layout map of the Foundry, revealing a secret door connecting to the Archives.

### Room 2: The Holding Pens
*   **Description:** Complete darkness, punctuated by pathetic whimpers and the scraping of claws on iron. Smells of rotting meat. Cages line the walls.
*   **Encounter:** 2x Thessalan Displacer Beasts (starving and feral) pace the room if they broke loose, or are released by guards if the Alert Level is High.
*   **Failing Forward:** If the party is ever defeated in the Foundry, they do not suffer a TPK. Instead, they wake up in these cages, stripped of their gear, and must break out using improvised weapons.

### Room 3: The Vivisection Theatre
*   **Description:** A surgical amphitheater where captured citizens are grafted with aberrant flesh. Blood stains the observation glass.
*   **Encounter (CR 11):** 1x Consortium Surgeon (Cleric of the Flesh), 3x Flesh Golem Prototypes (lobotomized and slow).
*   **Tactics:** The Surgeon casts *Sanctuary* and commands the Golems to grapple and pin the party to operating tables.

### Room 4: The Vat Room (The Climax)
*   **Description:** The core of the foundry. Massive glass cylinders filled with amniotic fluid contain half-formed monstrosities. 
*   **Boss Profile: Vespera the Fleshwarper (CR 12)**
    *   **Motivation:** Obsessed with perfecting the fusion of fey and aberration biology. Views the PCs as fascinating test subjects.
    *   **Flaw:** Arrogant and prone to monologuing. She will waste a round taking notes on a PC's unique racial abilities if they display them.
    *   **Enemies:** Vespera (Level 12 Alchemist/Wizard), 1x Scour-Harrow.
    *   **Tactics:** Vespera drinks a mutagen on round 1, gaining massive physical buffs (+4 Str/Con, +2 AC). She uses *Bombs* to intentionally shatter vats near the PCs.
*   **Environmental Hazard - Shattering Vats:** The room is filled with large glass cylinders. Hitting a canister (AC 10, HP 15) shatters it, releasing green acid in a 10-ft radius (**3d6 acid damage**, Reflex DC 15 half) and spawning a **Mutated Crawling Claw** swarm from the aborted flesh within.

---

## Resolution & Rewards
Behind the Vat Room lies the Archives. Securing the crystalline drives inside reveals the **Formula of Thessalan Suppressants**. 
*   **The Revelation:** Providing this formula to Suture back at Sablehook allows him to brew anti-compliance mutagens for the party, granting them immunity to the Elder Node's ambient psychic whispers in Act IV.
*   **Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 12**.
*   **Loot:** 18,000 gp in alchemical components, 1x *Amulet of Natural Armor +3*, a *Bag of Holding (Type II)*, and the *Formula of Thessalan Suppressants*.

## DM Tools & Sidebars

> [!TIP]
> **Alert Level Mechanics**
> The Consortium operates on a strict security protocol. If a loud explosion occurs or a guard uses a sounding horn, the Alert Level increases:
> *   **Low:** Standard patrols. 
> *   **Medium (1 Alarm):** Guards actively search. +2 Perception to all enemies.
> *   **High (2 Alarms):** The Foundry goes into lockdown. Iron bulkheads seal the main doors (Hardness 10, HP 100), the Decontamination trap is primed, and Vespera is warned of the party's approach, pre-buffing with *Mage Armor* and *Haste* before the Vat Room fight.

> [!NOTE]
> **Roleplaying Vespera**
> Play Vespera not as a raving lunatic, but as an utterly detached, cold academic. She doesn't hate the party; she simply views their biomatter as raw material for her art.
