# Act I: Whispers in the Sluices

**Campaign Level:** 10 (Milestone to 11 at completion)  
**Setting:** The Northern Sluices, Blackwater Quay  

---

## Adventure Overview
The characters are hired by Sable (or Inquisitor Vance, depending on their Faction Renown) to investigate a series of kidnappings in the Northern Sluices. Missing citizens aren't turning up dead; they are returning to the streets with deadened eyes, chemically compliant to the Elder Node's telepathic net. 

Act I is a **Hybrid Sandbox/Dungeon Crawl**. The party must investigate three key Sandbox Nodes (A, B, and C) in any order before locating the subterranean Cult Altar (The Dungeon Crawl).

---

## Part 1: The Investigation Sandbox

The party begins at the docks of the Northern Sluices. The area is a flooded shantytown; movement through the knee-deep black water is considered Difficult Terrain (unless the party has the *Dock-Rat* faction perk or *Webbed Digits*). Gathering clues from at least two nodes reveals the location of the Cult Altar (Node D).

### Node A: The Chymist's Den
An illegal apothecary built into the hull of a rotting, capsized barge. 
*   **Sensory Details:** The air burns the lungs; bubbling glass retorts cast harsh green shadows. The smell of vinegar and sulfur is overpowering.
*   **Encounter:** The Chymist (Level 8 Alchemist) and 4 Compliant Thralls. 
*   **Tactics:** The Chymist throws *Acidic Mutagens* (see Player Options) and tries to collapse the wooden support beams to pin the party. The thralls blindly grapple.
*   **Investigation (DC 15 Search):** The Chymist's ledger shows daily deliveries of a compound called "Derivative 7" to the Old Pumping Station. 
*   **Interactive Terrain:** Throwing alchemical fire at the ingredient shelves (AC 12) triggers an immediate *Stinking Cloud* (DC 16 Fortitude) that fills the barge.
*   **Loot:** 3 vials of *Blue-Drop*, 2 *Siphon Syringes*, and 400 gp.

### Node B: The Sluice Blockade
The cult has set up a chokepoint over the main canal to tax passage and kidnap the weak.
*   **Sensory Details:** Freezing, waist-deep black water. The sound of rusted iron scraping against stone. Rain pours down through grates in the surface ceiling 100 feet above.
*   **Encounter (CR 10):** 4x Sewer Thugs (Fighters), 2x Mutated Sea-Wolves (see Bestiary) hiding in the sludge. 
*   **Tactics:** The thugs try to bull-rush PCs off the walkways into the 5-ft deep sludge channels. 
*   **Hazard - Sewer Rot:** Anyone submerged in the sludge takes 1d6 acid damage per round and must make a **Fortitude DC 14** save or contract Sewer Rot (1d4 Dex damage daily).
*   **Investigation (DC 12 Intimidate/Diplomacy):** A captive merchant tied to a piling reveals the cultists take prisoners to the "Red Sigil Doors" below the water line near the Pumping Station.

### Node C: The Acidic Locks
The primary control room of the Sluices. The Choir has sabotaged the drainage system to flood the lower vaults.
*   **Sensory Details:** Deafening roar of rushing water. Steam vents obscure vision beyond 15 feet. 
*   **Hazard - Venting Gas:** Every 1d4 rounds, a pressure pipe bursts. Everyone in a 20-ft radius must make a **DC 15 Fort save** or be *nauseated* for 1 round.
*   **The Puzzle:** PCs must succeed on three **DC 20 Disable Device** checks (or DC 25 Strength checks to manually crank the rusted gears) to drain the vault. 
*   **Failing Forward:** If the party fails 3 checks before succeeding, the vault floods completely. They must fight the Cult Altar encounter entirely underwater, changing the dynamic drastically.

## Part 2: The Red Sigil Dungeon (The Cult Altar)

Once the party pieces together the clues, they locate the submerged entrance to the Old Pumping Station. This section is a strict dungeon crawl.

### Room 1: The Drowned Vestibule
*   **Description:** The heavy iron doors open into a sloping, slick stone hallway. The walls are covered in phosphorescent purple moss that hums faintly with psionic energy.
*   **Trap - The Node's Whisper (CR 6):** A localized psionic trap. **Search DC 22** to find the triggering rune. If stepped on, everyone in a 30-ft line must make a **Will DC 18** save or suffer 4d6 psychic damage and be *confused* for 1 round.

### Room 2: The Holding Pens
*   **Description:** Six rusted iron cages hang suspended over a pool of glowing green acid. Inside are missing citizens in varying states of chemical compliance.
*   **Encounter:** 2x Scour-Harrows (see Bestiary).
*   **Tactics:** The constructs ignore the party at first, slowly lowering the cages into the acid via a winch system. The party has **4 rounds** to defeat them or jam the winches (Disable Device DC 18) before the citizens die.
*   **Reward:** Rescuing the citizens grants +2 Renown with Covenant & Crown (if turned over) or Sablehook (if smuggled out).

### Room 3: The Inner Sanctum (Boss Encounter)
*   **Description:** A cavernous cistern converted into a grotesque shrine. At the center is a massive copper vat feeding "Derivative 7" directly into the city's main water line.
*   **Boss Profile: High Priest Vane (CR 12)**
    *   **Motivation:** To prepare the surface world for the Daelkyr's arrival via total chemical compliance.
    *   **Flaw:** Utterly reliant on the Elder Node's telepathic guidance. If subjected to a *Zone of Silence* or Banki's Null-Fang, he panics, taking a -2 penalty to AC and Saves.
    *   **Enemies:** High Priest Vane (Level 10 Cleric of the Daelkyr), 2x Dolgaunt Cell-Guards, 1x Reedsinger (Summoned if Vane drops below 50% HP).
    *   **Tactics:** Vane casts *confusion* and *hold person*. The Dolgaunts target spellcasters, using their long reach (15 ft) to disrupt casting and rip weapons from hands. 

### The Overload Mechanic
If Vane drops below 30 HP, he attempts to overload the copper vat as a spiteful final act. 
*   **The Countdown:** The vat begins to glow white-hot. It explodes in 3 rounds. 
*   **The Fix:** PCs must use a **DC 25 Disable Device** check or deal 50 points of cold damage to the vat to freeze the reaction. 
*   **The Consequence:** If it explodes, it deals **8d6 acid/psychic damage** in a 40-ft radius (Reflex DC 18 half) and the party loses the primary evidence (the ledger).

---

## Resolution & Rewards

Defeating the Priest reveals a ledger in his robes (or in a lockbox if the vat explodes). 
*   **The Revelation:** The ledger proves the Choir of the Below receives their chemical supplies directly from the **Thessalan Consortium**, whose hidden "Fleshwarped Foundry" is operating beneath the Belowmarket Deep. 
*   **Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 11**.
*   **Loot:** 12,000 gp in illicit funds, 3x *Potions of Cure Serious Wounds*, a *Ring of Mind Shielding*, and the *Chronal Chronometer* (worn by Vane).

## DM Tools & Sidebars

> [!NOTE]
> **Tone & Pacing**
> The Northern Sluices are a suffocating, flooded shantytown. Emphasize the sensory details: the stinging smell of vinegar and sulfur, the oppressive humidity, and the ever-present, low-frequency hum of the Elder Node vibrating in the players' teeth. The pacing should feel like a creeping dread.

> [!WARNING]
> **High Alert Level**
> If the party triggered alarms at the Sluice Blockade (Node B) or used loud explosives at Node A, High Priest Vane expects them at Room 3. He will have already cast *Bless* and *Shield of Faith* on his minions before the party kicks in the doors.
