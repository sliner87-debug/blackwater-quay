# Act I: Whispers in the Sluices

**Campaign Level:** 10 (Milestone to 11 at completion)  
**Setting:** The Northern Sluices, Blackwater Quay  

---

## Adventure Overview
The characters are hired by Sable to investigate a series of kidnappings in the Northern Sluices. Missing citizens aren't turning up dead; they are returning to the streets with deadened eyes, chemically compliant to the Elder Node's telepathic net. Act I is a **sandbox investigation** where the party must explore three key nodes in any order before locating the final subterranean cult altar.

---

## The Investigation Sandbox (Nodes A, B, & C)

The party begins at the docks. They can tackle the following locations in any order. Gathering clues from at least two nodes reveals the location of the Cult Altar (Node D).

### Node A: The Chymist's Den
An illegal apothecary built into the hull of a rotting barge. 
*   **Sensory:** The air burns the lungs; bubbling glass retorts cast harsh green shadows.
*   **Encounter:** The Chymist (Level 8 Alchemist) and 3 Compliant Thralls. 
*   **Clue:** The Chymist's ledger shows deliveries of "Derivative 7" to the Old Pumping Station. 
*   **Interactive Terrain:** Throwing alchemical fire at the ingredient shelves (AC 12) triggers a *Stinking Cloud* (DC 16).

### Node B: The Sluice Blockade
The cult has set up a chokepoint over the main canal to tax passage and kidnap the weak.
*   **Sensory:** Freezing, waist-deep black water. The sound of rusted iron scraping against stone. 
*   **Encounter (CR 10):** 4x Sewer Thugs, 2x Mutated Sea-Wolves hiding in the sludge. 
*   **Tactics:** The thugs try to bull-rush PCs into the 5-ft deep sludge channels (1d6 acid damage/round, Fortitude DC 14 vs. Sewer Rot).
*   **Clue:** A captive merchant reveals the cultists take prisoners to the "Red Sigil Doors" below the water line.

### Node C: The Acidic Locks
The primary control room of the Sluices. The Choir has sabotaged the drainage system to flood the lower vaults.
*   **Sensory:** Deafening roar of rushing water. Steam vents obscure vision beyond 15 feet. 
*   **Hazard - Venting Gas:** Every 1d4 rounds, a pipe bursts (**DC 15 Fort save** or be *nauseated* for 1 round).
*   **The Puzzle:** PCs must succeed on three **DC 20 Disable Device** checks to drain the vault. 

---

## Node D: The Cult Altar
The water level drops, revealing a cavernous cistern converted into a grotesque shrine. At the center is a massive copper vat feeding derivative compounds directly into the city's main water line.

### Encounter: The Choir Priest (CR 12)
**NPC Profile: High Priest Vane**
*   **Motivation:** To prepare the surface world for the Daelkyr's arrival via total chemical compliance.
*   **Flaw:** Arrogant and utterly reliant on the Elder Node's telepathic guidance. If subjected to a *Zone of Silence* or Banki's Null-Fang, he panics.
*   **Enemies:** 1x Choir Priest (Level 10 Cleric of the Daelkyr), 2x Dolgaunt Cell-Guards.
*   **Tactics:** Vane casts *confusion* and *hold person*. The Dolgaunts target spellcasters, using their long reach (15 ft) to disrupt casting and rip weapons from hands.

### The Overload Mechanic
If Vane drops below 30 HP, he attempts to overload the copper vat. 
*   **The Countdown:** The vat explodes in 3 rounds. 
*   **The Fix:** PCs must use a **DC 25 Disable Device** check or deal 50 points of cold damage to the vat to freeze the reaction. 
*   **The Consequence:** If it explodes, it deals **6d6 acid/psychic damage** in a 30-ft radius and the party loses the primary evidence (the ledger).

---

## Resolution & Rewards
Defeating the Priest reveals a ledger in his robes (or in a lockbox if the vat explodes). The ledger proves the cult receives their chemical supplies from the **Thessalan Consortium**, whose hidden "Fleshwarped Foundry" is operating beneath the Belowmarket Deep. 

**Milestone:** The party advances to **Level 11**.
**Loot:** 12,000 gp in illicit funds, 3x *Potions of Cure Serious Wounds*, and a *Ring of Mind Shielding*.

---

## DM Tools & Sidebars

**Sidebar 1: Tone & Pacing**
The Northern Sluices are a suffocating, flooded shantytown. Emphasize the sensory details: the stinging smell of vinegar and sulfur, the oppressive humidity, and the ever-present, low-frequency hum of the Elder Node vibrating in the players' teeth. The pacing should feel like a creeping dread.

**Sidebar 2: Failing Forward at the Locks**
If the party fails the Disable Device checks at Node C, the vault floods completely. They must fight the Cult Altar encounter entirely underwater, changing the dynamic drastically, but they still gain access to the Altar without bottlenecking the plot.

**Sidebar 3: High Alert Level**
If the party triggered alarms at the Sluice Blockade (Node B) or used loud explosives at Node A, the Choir Priest expects them at Node D. He will have already cast *Bless* and *Shield of Faith* on his minions before the party arrives.
