# The Sovereign Corsairs Fleet: Vessel Manual & Campaign Guide

This compendium serves as the authoritative guide for the flagships, line-of-battle ships, and naval systems of the Sovereign Corsairs—led by Captain Mikhailis (Kael) and Sovereign Banki. It details the layouts, TTRPG statistics, crew rosters, and in-game upgrades for every vessel in the armada.

## 5. SHARED FLEET GRAFTS & UPGRADES

Under Suture's directives, the following biological and magical modifications are applied species-wide across the fleet's crew and vanguard forces:

*   **Phase-Stutter Skin:** Extracted Displacer Beast glands are braided into the skin of Shadar-Kai and Sea-Wolves, granting them a constant **20% miss chance** shimmer and a 10-ft. teleport (3/day).
*   **Scrag-Blood Transfusions:** Trench's regenerative Scrag marrow is synthesized, granting crew members **Fast Healing 2** while they are wet or submerged.
*   **Illithid-Kin Ganglia Injections:** Hard-wired brains allow the officers to share a telepathic network (**The Sovereign Network**), granting immunity to flanking and a +2 morale bonus on attacks when coordinating.
*   **Benthic Drake Transports:** Captured deep-water drakes are armored with adamantine plating and surgically grafted with extra-dimensional pouches, allowing them to carry up to 16 boarding troops in underwater stasis and deploy them directly onto enemy decks.

## 6. TTRPG CAMPAIGN MECHANICS & ABILITIES

### A. Flagship In-Game Upgrades & Modifications (The Onyx Wake)
*   **Sovereign Ascension (Living Construct Symbiosis):** The Abyssal Hunter-Squid is grafted into the keel. The *Onyx Wake* is a **Living Construct** (+4 handling, heals 2d6 hull HP/hour). Kael can pilot the vessel telepathically.
*   **The Sovereign Anchor (Adamantine Sphere Graft):** Grafted into the bow, providing three distinct functions:
    *   *Defensive Anchor:* Projects a **500-foot aura** blocking all enemy teleportation/planar-boarding tactics.
    *   *Gravitic Singular-Ram:* During a ram, Kael increases the ship's mass fifty-fold, dealing **12d6 crushing damage** that ignores hardness and armor class, folding target hulls inward.
    *   *Hydrodynamic Shockwave:* Distorts water geometry to project a crushing shockwave in a 120-ft. cone. Targets take **6d6 bludgeoning damage** (Reflex DC 22 half) and are pushed 30 ft. backward.
*   **The Sovereign Void-Lapse Lance:** Fuses force/fire output with an entropy void:
    *   *Discharge:* Projects a 300-ft. line dealing **10d6 unholy force damage** and **10d6 fire damage** (Reflex DC 22 half). Targets must make a DC 24 Will save or be paralyzed for 1d4 rounds.
    *   *Cavitation Implosion (The Snap-Back):* Flash-boils seawater to create a vacuum line. When deactivated, the water implodes, creating a concussive shockwave in a **150-foot radius** that deals **6d6 bludgeoning/sonic damage** (DC 20 Reflex half) and forces a DC 22 Fortitude save to avoid being **stunned** for 1d3 rounds. Paralyzed targets recharge ship shields and fuel.
*   **Gravity-Slurry Hull Hardening:** Grants a permanent **+10 Hardness** and collision immunity from creatures or ships smaller than Gargantuan.
*   **Phrenic Cloaking Field:** Cloaks the flagship and up to 3 trailing vessels from detection or scrying past 120 ft.

### B. Fleet & Boarding Combat Rules (The Bleeding Needle)
*   **Chronal Glide (Temporal Slip):** 1/day, the ship slips 1.5 seconds out of the timeline, gaining a **+8 dodge bonus to AC** against a single attack or phasing through blockades up to 60 ft. wide.
*   **Soul-Combustion Overdrive:** 1/encounter, Thrain Vrunn increases swim speed to **80 ft. for 3 rounds**. The engine deck takes 5d6 fire damage, and Deck III crew must save (Fort DC 15) or be *sickened* for 1d4 rounds by soul-fumes.
*   **Necrotic Blood-Mist Vents:** Standard action vents a 30-ft. cone of acidic mist. Targets take **4d6 fire/acid damage** and must make a DC 16 Fortitude save or be *sickened* for 1d4 rounds.
*   **The Kraken-Drop Boarding Protocol:** 1/encounter, the ship launches Slake (Trench) astride the *Abyssal Dread-Carrier* (the Hunter-Squid).
    *   *Submerged Breach:* Carrier approaches under *Deeper Darkness* and slams tentacles into the target hull (Attack +20, deals 4d10+10 bludgeoning, ignoring hardness up to 10).
    *   *Boarding Bridge:* Tentacles form bridges. Boarding troops charge, ignoring difficult terrain and gaining a **+4 morale bonus on attacks and damage** for 1 round.
    *   *Underkeel Sunder:* Carrier submerges to crush the target rudder or keel, dealing **6d6+15 bludgeoning damage per round** to the hull structure.
*   **The Runic Shroud (PC Safe Haven):** While resting aboard the vessel, PCs are completely immune to daily sanity checks, mental fatigue, or scrying sweeps.
*   **Alchemical Essence Refining:** PCs can convert captured enemies or monster carcasses into *Arcane Ink* (wizard spells at 75% cost), *Psionic Node Crystals* (restores 1d4+1 power points), or *Divine Gold Serum* (restores divine slots up to 4th level).
