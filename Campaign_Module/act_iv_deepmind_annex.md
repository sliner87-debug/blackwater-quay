# Act IV: Descent Into the Deepmind Annex

**Campaign Level:** 13 (Climax)
**Setting:** The Brine-Veins beneath Blackwater Quay

---

## Adventure Overview
Following the Githyanki air raid in Act III, Sablehook is hidden under Banki's unstable Tri-Weave Shroud. However, the Elder Node is actively pulling planar tissue from the Void of Madness into the brine-veins beneath the city. If the tear is not sealed within 24 hours, the Daelkyr's fleshwarping aura will burst onto the surface, dissolving the region.

---

## The Deepmind Annex Key (Node Map)

### Node A-1: The Weeping Vaults
The old dwarven sluice chambers have been overrun by fleshy, purple growths that weep compliance fluid (diluted Blue-Drop). 
*   **Sensory:** The air is humid and smells of vinegar and ozone. The floor feels soft and spongy, like walking on a bruised lung.
*   **Hazard - Compliance Mist:** The room is filled with vapors. Living creatures must make a **Fortitude DC 18** save every 3 rounds or become *fatigued* and take a -2 penalty to Will saves.
*   **Encounter:** 4x Compliant Sewer Enforcers, 1x Chirg-Illithid (Elder Node Avatar). 
*   **Tactics:** The Chirg-Illithid stands on a raised platform, using *Mind Blast* to stun the party while the enforcers drag stunned characters into acidic sewage channels (2d6 acid damage/round).

### Node A-2: The Gravity Flue
A vertical, 120-foot cylindrical shaft lined with rusted iron ladders. Planar energy from the rift causes gravity to fluctuate wildly.
*   **Sensory:** Complete silence, broken only by the sound of the PCs' own heartbeats echoing off the walls.
*   **Hazard - Gravity Fluctuations:** At the start of each character's turn, roll 1d4:
    1.  *Normal Gravity:* No effect.
    2.  *Reverse Gravity:* The character falls upward toward the ceiling (4d6 falling damage unless they grab a rung with a Reflex DC 18 check).
    3.  *Lateral Gravity:* Gravity shifts to the left wall. Characters are thrown sideways.
    4.  *No Gravity:* The character floats. Movement is halved, melee attacks take a -2 penalty.
*   **Encounter:** 3x Dolgaunt Cell-Guards crawl along the walls (immune to gravity shifts).

### Node A-2b: The Chronal Echo Hall (Optional Sandbox Room)
A silent cavern where shards of glass-like Sylvan crystal float in mid-air.
*   **The Puzzle:** Moving across the 5x5 grid of glowing runes activates past memories. Stepping on a rune out of sequence spawns a *Temporal Echo* of the PC that attacks them (same stats, 20 HP).
*   **The Solution:** By tracing Kael's chronal footprints (Intelligence DC 20), characters can trace the safe sequence: *North, East, North, West, North*.
*   **Loot:** The Chronal Chronometer (extra action when shattered) and 3 jars of Grafting Salts.

### Node A-3: The Cathedral of Whispers
Ancient Dhakaani goblin ruins warped by the Void. The pillars are calcified bone.
*   **Sensory:** The walls whisper in telepathic static, recounting the PCs' greatest failures.
*   **Encounter:** 2x Chirg-Illithids, 2x Mutated Displacer Beasts.
*   **Hazard - Telepathic Static:** Casting a spell with a verbal component or manifesting a psionic power requires a **Concentration DC 22** check or the action is lost.

---

## Node A-4: The Rift Climax (The Ritual)

The final chamber contains the Void of Madness Tear—a vertical fissure of shifting purple energy. The party must defend Kaelen Grey ("Suture") and Hruujj as they lay down the seals (5 rounds).

### The Siege Mechanics
*   **Banki's Anchor:** Banki must stand adjacent to the rift with the *Null-Fang*. Each round, he takes 1d3 temporary Intelligence damage.
*   **Player Actions:** Players can spend standard actions to:
    1.  **Cap Tears (Str DC 22):** Heave stone slabs over pocket tears. Reduces aberrations spawning next round by 1.
    2.  **Siphon Bleed (Spellcraft DC 20):** Redirect raw energy, reducing Banki's Int damage by 1.
    3.  **Anchor Coordinates (Knowledge: Planes DC 20):** Stabilize the room, locking gravity to "Normal" for 1 round.

### Kael's Temporal Loop (The Reset)
When the catastrophe occurs (see Sidebar 3), Kael sacrifices his physical form to activate a Temporal Loop, rewinding combat to **Round 5**. The PCs retain their memories.

**The Counter-Attack:**
*   **The Trap:** A rogue can search behind the altar (Search DC 15) and disable the siphon trap (Disable Device DC 25) before Round 6.
*   **The Ambush:** Knowing the spawn coordinates, the party can place traps or ready actions, catching the Minotaurs flat-footed (-4 AC).
*   **The Dragon:** PCs can shout the *Clause of the Sovereign Thread* (Knowledge: History DC 18, lowered due to the loop) to convince Oraxis to stand down and aid them.

## Resolution
Once the trap is disabled, the minotaurs defeated, and the rift sealed, the cavern begins to collapse. The party must carry Kael and Banki out of the annex while the Tarkanan Syndicate secures their retreat to the surface, concluding the campaign.

## DM Tools & Sidebars

**Sidebar 1: The Final Descent**
This act should feel alien, disorienting, and desperate. The Annex is a non-Euclidean space. Geometry breaks down. Describe right angles as looking "wrong," and shadows stretching in directions completely opposed to light sources. Range increments for ranged attacks are halved, and spell ranges are reduced by 50% due to spatial compression.

**Sidebar 2: Failing Forward in the Flue**
If a character falls and takes lethal damage in Node A-2 (The Gravity Flue), they don't die instantly. Instead, the spatial compression catches them in a stasis loop 10 feet off the ground. The party must spend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher (or 5 power points) to "un-stick" them from time, stabilizing them at 0 HP.

**Sidebar 3: The Double-Cross (Round 6)**
If the party seals the rift without preparing for the double-cross, Hruujj triggers a hidden trap that drains the Null-Fang, incapacitating Banki. Hruujj summons 2 Fiendish Minotaurs to execute the PCs while Oraxis (a bronze dragon) attacks the room to purge it. This kicks off the Temporal Loop.
