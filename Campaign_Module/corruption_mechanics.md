# The Static: Corruption Mechanics

In Blackwater Quay, the Void of Madness (Xoriat) constantly presses against the Tri-Weave Shroud. Exposure to this raw psychic static is physically and mentally corrupting. Game Masters should use the **Static Points** system to track this corruption.

## Accumulating Static
Players accumulate Static Points in three ways:
1.  **Taking Psychic Damage:** Whenever a player takes 10 or more psychic damage from a single source (an Aberration, an Aetheric Storm), they gain 1d4 Static Points.
2.  **Attuning to the Node:** Meditating near the Elder Node during downtime can grant massive power, but failing the save grants 1d6 Static Points.
3.  **Embracing the Static (Desperation):** At any time, a player can choose to "Embrace the Static." They can automatically turn a failed attack roll or saving throw into a success. However, doing so immediately grants them 2d10 Static Points.

## The Stages of Corruption
As a player's Static Points increase, they begin to physically warp.

*   **0-25 Points (The Whispers):** No mechanical penalty. The player occasionally hears phantom calculations in their sleep or sees geometric patterns behind their eyelids.
*   **26-50 Points (Minor Fleshwarping):** The player's biology begins to alter. Their blood turns violet, they cast no reflection, or their shadow detaches. *Mechanic:* They suffer Disadvantage on all Charisma (Persuasion) checks with mundane humans.
*   **51-75 Points (The Choir's Call):** The static becomes deafening. *Mechanic:* The player gains vulnerability to psychic damage. However, Aberrations will no longer target them first in combat, recognizing them as "kin."
*   **76-99 Points (The Breaking Point):** The character's mind is fracturing. *Mechanic:* At the start of every combat, they must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they must spend their first turn attacking the nearest creature (friend or foe) as the Void takes over.
*   **100 Points (Total Annihilation):** The character is permanently lost to the Void. They instantly transform into a horrific Aberration (use the *Scour-Harrow* or *Cerebrilith* stat block) under the GM's control. The character is dead.

## Purging Static
Static is notoriously difficult to remove. Normal healing magic (*Cure Wounds*, *Lesser Restoration*) does nothing.
*   *Greater Restoration* removes 2d10 Static Points.
*   Completing a major quest for Banki (who can manually siphon the static back into the Tri-Weave Shroud) removes 5d10 points.
