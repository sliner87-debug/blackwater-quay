# Downtime Mechanics: Life in the Quay

In Blackwater Quay, a long rest is not just for healing; the days between Acts are crucial for building the party's power base and embedding themselves in the local syndicates. During downtime (defined as a period of 3-7 days between major missions), each player character can choose **one** of the following Downtime Activities. 

*(For unique downtime activities that occur specifically between major story Acts, see [Timeline Dynamics & Interludes](timeline_and_interludes.md).)*

## 1. Carousing the Belowmarket & The Shadow Network
The character spends their days drinking, gambling, and trading secrets in the seediest taverns of the Quay, actively engaging with Sablehook's vast informant web known as the "Shadow Network."
*   **Cost:** 20 gp (bribes, drinks, losses).
*   **Mechanic:** Make a Charisma (Persuasion or Deception) check.
    *   *1-9:* You start a bar fight with rival Iron Eel thugs. You are thrown in a local holding cell for 24 hours and lose an additional 10 gp.
    *   *10-14:* You tap into the Shadow Network. The DM reveals one monster weakness or secret trap for the upcoming Act based on smuggler gossip.
    *   *15-19:* You make a high-value contact. You gain 1 favor from a mid-level Sablehook lieutenant, which can be cashed in for a free ferry ride or a distraction during a heist.
    *   *20+:* You win big at the dice tables. You recover your 20 gp, win an additional 50 gp, and gain +1 Renown with Sablehook.

## 2. Caravan Guard & Capture Specialist (Underdark Exploration)
The character signs on to ride with one of Sablehook's heavily armored caravans, plunging into the deeper, unmapped Underdark to secure smuggling routes or capture exotic biological assets for the vats.
*   **Cost:** None (Hazard Pay).
*   **Mechanic:** Make a Constitution (Athletics) or Wisdom (Survival) check.
    *   *1-9:* The caravan is ambushed by Drow or aberrations. You survive, but take 3d6 slashing damage and return with 1 level of Exhaustion.
    *   *10-14:* The route is brutal, but you pull your weight. You earn 50 gp in hazard pay.
    *   *15-19:* Flawless operation. You help capture a rare beast. You earn 100 gp, and the beast's parts grant the party Advantage on their next attempt at Biomantic Grafting.
    *   *20+:* You intercept a rival's shipment or discover a lost ruin. You earn 150 gp, +1 Syndicate Victory Point, and uncover a minor magical trinket from the deep Underdark.

## 3. Apprenticeship: The Vats & Artifice (Requires Sablehook Renown Rank 1)
The character spends their downtime working in the laboratories of Blackwater's master craftsmen, either studying Biomancy under **Suture** or Golem Artifice under **Mirell**.
*   **Cost:** 200 gp for raw materials and lab fees.
*   **Mechanic:** Make an Intelligence (Arcana or Medicine) check. You must accumulate **3 successful downtime weeks** (a cumulative 45+ total roll over multiple sessions) to complete your apprenticeship. 
*   **The Reward:** Once the apprenticeship is complete, you gain a permanent, lore-specific Feat based on your mentor:
    *   *Suture's Fleshcrafter:* You gain the ability to harvest Aberration Cores from fallen enemies (DC 15 Medicine). Once per long rest, you can consume a core to immediately roll hit dice and heal yourself, but you suffer a minor, temporary cosmetic mutation (e.g., violet eyes, scales).
    *   *Mirell's Golem-Smith:* You learn to forge *Scour-Runes*. You can craft 1 mechanical clockwork familiar (using the stats of a Spider or Raven) that shares your telepathic link. It is immune to psychic static. 

## 4. Attuning to the Static (The Elder Node)
The character meditates near an exposed vein of the Elder Node, trying to glean psionic insight from the very source of the Tri-Weave Shroud's power.
*   **Cost:** None, but highly dangerous to the mortal mind.
*   **Mechanic:** Make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 16).
    *   *1-9:* The static overwhelms you. You gain a short-term madness flaw (e.g., "I can hear Banki's calculations in my teeth") for 1d4 days, and you do not gain the benefits of a Long Rest for this downtime period.
    *   *10-14:* You resist the madness, but learn nothing from the chaotic noise of Xoriat.
    *   *15-19:* You glimpse the underlying math of the Tri-Weave Shroud. You gain Advantage on all saving throws against magical effects for the next 24 hours.
    *   *20+:* The Node whispers a secret of the universe. You can cast the *Augury* spell once without components before the next downtime period.

## 5. Sovereign Shipyards (Mikhailis' Fleet)
The character descends into the hidden subterranean docks to assist Kael (Mikhailis) and the Sablehook shipwrights in constructing, repairing, or upgrading the syndicate's dreadnoughts (like *The Bleeding Needle* or *The Onyx Wake*).
*   **Cost:** 500 gp (invested into ship materials).
*   **Mechanic:** Make an Intelligence (Woodcarver's Tools/Smith's Tools) or Strength (Athletics) check.
    *   *1-9:* An industrial accident occurs. You take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and ruin the materials. You lose the 500 gp.
    *   *10-14:* Slow progress. You contribute, but not enough to finish an upgrade this week. 
    *   *15-19:* Excellent craftsmanship. You complete a weapon mount or hull brace. You earn the **Artillery Support** perk.
    *   *20+:* Masterclass engineering. You finish a major upgrade and Kael takes notice. You earn **Artillery Support** and +1 Sablehook Renown.
*   **The Reward (Artillery Support):** Once per Act, as an Action, you can signal the underground harbor. At the start of your next turn, a devastating broadside strike obliviates a 30x30 foot area (8d10 fire and bludgeoning damage, DC 16 Dex save for half) provided you are somewhere the ship's arcanists can scry on.

## Illicit Market Purchases
During downtime, players with access to the Belowmarket can purchase highly illegal, specialized assets.

### 1. Refined Drift-Fire
*   **Cost:** 150 gp per dose.
*   **Mechanic:** A potent, chaotic magical narcotic. As a Bonus Action, a player can consume a dose to gain **Aether-Sight**. For 1 hour, they can see invisible entities, magical auras, and gain Advantage on all mental skill checks (Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) and Initiative rolls.
*   **The Drawback:** When the hour ends, the comedown is brutal. The player suffers 1 level of Exhaustion and must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they become *Addicted* (suffering Disadvantage on all Wisdom saves until they consume another dose or undergo magical rehabilitation).

### 2. Null-Steel Prosthetics (Mirell's Forge)
*   **Cost:** 1,000 gp (and the loss of a limb).
*   **Mechanic:** If a player loses a limb (or volunteers to have one amputated), Mirell can forge and attach a mechanical arm crafted from Templar Null-Steel. 
*   **Benefits:** The arm acts as a magic-dampening bludgeon. Unarmed strikes with the arm deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage, and as an Action, the player can punch a magical construct (like a *Wall of Force* or *Shield* spell), instantly casting *Dispel Magic* at 3rd level on that specific target.
*   **The Drawback:** Because the arm is not flesh, it cannot be healed or reattached by divine magic (*Cure Wounds*, *Regenerate*). If the arm is damaged (e.g., targeted by a rust monster or sundered), it must be repaired during a Short Rest using Smith's Tools.
