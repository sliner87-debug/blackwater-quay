# Encounter Scaling Guidelines

The combat encounters in the *Blackwater Quay* campaign module are balanced for a standard party of **4 Level 5 characters**. However, the brutal nature of the Quay means that Game Masters will need to adjust on the fly based on party composition, tactics, and their narrative success in earlier acts.

## 1. Scaling by Party Size
If your player count differs from the baseline of 4, use the following adjustments:

*   **For 3 Players (Understaffed):**
    *   Reduce enemy HP by 25%.
    *   Remove 1 "minion" or secondary enemy from the encounter (e.g., in a fight with 1 Biomancer and 3 Aberrants, remove 1 Aberrant).
    *   *Sablehook Rule:* Allow the party to hire a low-level syndicate mercenary for 50gp per day to act as a meat-shield.
*   **For 5 Players (Overstaffed):**
    *   Increase enemy HP by 25% (or maximize their hit dice).
    *   Add 1 additional "minion" enemy for every player above 4.
    *   Add "Lair Actions" to standard boss fights (e.g., the Elder Node pulses, forcing a DC 14 Wisdom save against psychic damage at Initiative 20).
*   **For 6+ Players (Syndicate Warband):**
    *   Double the number of minion enemies to prevent the action economy from overwhelmingly favoring the players.
    *   Grant boss monsters a "Legendary Action" allowing them to make a basic attack or reposition at the end of another creature's turn.

## 2. Scaling by Syndicate Victory Points (SVP)
*Blackwater Quay* tracks the party's influence as they complete side quests, forge alliances, and secure smuggling routes. These are tracked as **Syndicate Victory Points (SVP)**. GMs can scale the difficulty of the final acts based on the party's SVP.

*   **Low SVP (0-3 Points): The Desperate Stand**
    *   *Narrative:* The party has alienated allies or failed to secure the Shroud. 
    *   *Mechanic:* Enemies have Advantage on Initiative rolls, having ambushed or outmaneuvered the party. The party receives no backup during major boss encounters.
*   **Medium SVP (4-7 Points): The Standard Approach**
    *   *Narrative:* The party is respected but not universally feared.
    *   *Mechanic:* Encounters run exactly as written in the module.
*   **High SVP (8-10+ Points): Kings of the Quay**
    *   *Narrative:* The party owns the underground. They have bribes in place, scouts watching the tunnels, and syndicate thugs at their back.
    *   *Mechanic:* The party begins major encounters with "Tactical Superiority." The first round of combat is a Surprise Round in favor of the players. Furthermore, once per session, the players can cash in 1 SVP to summon 1d4 Sablehook Enforcers to join the fray for 3 rounds.
