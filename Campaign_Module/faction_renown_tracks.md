# Faction Renown Tracks

Players operating in Blackwater Quay can earn reputation with the three major powers. A player’s **Renown Score** with a faction starts at 0 and caps at 20. 

Earning Renown involves completing faction-specific quests, donating wealth or magical artifacts, or sabotaging rival factions. 

---

## 1. The Thessalan Consortium
*Alignment: Lawful Evil | Focus: Biomancy, Mutation, Profit*

The Consortium values ruthless efficiency, magical innovation, and biological perfection. They despise weakness and religious dogma.

*   **Earning Renown:** Harvesting rare aberration cores (+1), turning over captured Templars for experimentation (+2), assassinating rival smugglers (+1).
*   **Losing Renown:** Destroying their vats (-2), healing the sick for free (-1).

### Rank Progression
*   **Rank 1: Specimen (Renown 3)**
    *   *Perk:* You gain access to the Flesh-Markets. You can purchase basic mutagens and healing potions at a 10% discount.
*   **Rank 2: Stitch-Hand (Renown 10)**
    *   *Perk:* You earn the right to bodily modification. You can undergo one minor biological graft (e.g., darkvision eyes, retractable bone spurs) at no cost.
*   **Rank 3: Fleshwright (Renown 20)**
    *   *Perk:* You command the vats. Once per week, you can request a *Scour-Harrow* or *Gargoyle* flesh-construct to follow your orders for 24 hours.

---

## 2. Covenant & Crown (The Templar Inquisition)
*Alignment: Lawful Good (Extremist) | Focus: Purity, Eradication of Magic, Order*

The surface-dwelling Templars seek to purge the Quay of its illicit magic and chaotic elements. 

*   **Earning Renown:** Turning in unregistered spellcasters (+1), destroying black-market shipments (+2), providing actionable intel on the Tri-Weave Shroud (+3).
*   **Losing Renown:** Using arcane magic in front of them (-2), aiding smugglers (-3).

### Rank Progression
*   **Rank 1: Informant (Renown 3)**
    *   *Perk:* You receive an Inquisition Writ, granting you immunity from low-level city watch harassment on the surface.
*   **Rank 2: Hound (Renown 10)**
    *   *Perk:* You are granted access to the Templar armory. You can requisition *Null-Steel* weapons, which bypass magical resistances and deal +1d4 damage to fey and aberrations.
*   **Rank 3: Inquisitor (Renown 20)**
    *   *Perk:* You command the holy fire. Once per week, you can summon a squad of 4 Templar Knights to raid a location of your choosing.

---

## 3. Choir of the Below
*Alignment: Chaotic Neutral | Focus: The Elder Node, Prophecy, Madness*

A cult of outcasts, mind-flayers, and mutants who worship the psychic static leaking from the Elder Node. 

*   **Earning Renown:** Protecting an Elder Node crystal shard (+2), sabotaging the Shroud to let the static flow freely (+3), surviving a direct mental link with a Cerebrilith (+1).
*   **Losing Renown:** Fortifying the Tri-Weave Shroud (-2), allying with the Templars (-3).

### Rank Progression
*   **Rank 1: Whisperer (Renown 3)**
    *   *Perk:* The static becomes comforting. You gain Advantage on saving throws against charm, fear, and psychic damage.
*   **Rank 2: Resonance (Renown 10)**
    *   *Perk:* You can channel the Node. Once per long rest, you can cast *Detect Thoughts* or *Dissonant Whispers* without expending a spell slot or components.
*   **Rank 3: The Voice (Renown 20)**
    *   *Perk:* The Elder Node speaks through you. Once per month, you can ask the Node a single question and receive an absolutely truthful, omniscient answer (acting as the *Commune* spell, but not limited to yes/no).

## 4. Sablehook Syndicate
*Alignment: True Neutral | Focus: Smuggling, Survival, The Shroud*

The premier smuggling syndicate of Blackwater Quay, run by Sable and anchored by Banki. They operate by strict codes of silence and efficiency.

*   **Earning Renown:** Securing Null-Steel shipments (+1), sabotaging Templar checkpoints (+2), working in Mikhailis' Shipyards (+1), bringing rare components to Suture (+1).
*   **Losing Renown:** Breaking a contract (-2), speaking of the Tri-Weave Shroud to outsiders (-3).

### Rank Progression
*   **Rank 1: Dock-Rat (Renown 3)**
    *   *Perk:* You have free passage on any Sablehook skiff or ferry in the Sluices, and you ignore Difficult Terrain penalties when moving through the knee-deep waters of the Quay.
*   **Rank 2: Tri-Weave Resonance (Renown 10)**
    *   *Perk:* You undergo a ritual to have a micro-sliver of the Sovereign Thread "stitched" into your shadow. If you and another Rank 2 Sablehook ally are within 15 feet of each other, you can communicate telepathically in complete silence. Additionally, as a Reaction, you can magically absorb half the damage taken by a linked ally (functioning identically to the *Warding Bond* spell, but requiring no components or spell slots).
*   **Rank 3: Sovereign Enforcer (Renown 20)**
    *   *Perk:* You are granted access to Sablehook's highest tier of tactical support. Once per Act, you can call upon *The Bleeding Needle* or *The Onyx Wake* to provide devastating artillery support or immediate extraction from anywhere in the Quay.
