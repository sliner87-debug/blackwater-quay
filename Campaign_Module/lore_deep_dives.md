# Lore Deep-Dives (GM Eyes Only)

This document contains the absolute truths behind the greatest mysteries of Blackwater Quay. Game Masters should read this to understand the deeper motivations of the factions and the underlying metaphysics of the setting.

## 1. The Fall of the Consortium (10 Years Ago)
**The Public Lie:** Ten years ago, the original leaders of the Thessalan Consortium pushed their biomantic experiments too far, resulting in a horrific plague that forced the Templars to quarantine the lower levels. The Consortium fractured, and its leaders were either killed or went into hiding.

**The Truth:** There was no plague. The Consortium discovered the Elder Node and attempted to harvest it, but that was only the catalyst for a much larger catastrophe. 
The true fall was triggered by the escape of **Bancaelis** (now known as Banki) from his confinement. In a brutal uprising, Bancaelis slaughtered every member of the Consortium cell holding him. The extreme magical frequencies and psionic pulses unleashed during this slaughter resonated with the Elder Node and other experimental artifacts connected across the Consortium's global magic web. 

This resonance went catastrophically awry. It acted as a localized planar bomb, ripping open **Dimensional Tears to Xoriat (The Realm of Madness)** in almost all of the Consortium's Cell Headquarters simultaneously. Due to this sudden, extreme violence cascading across their interconnected network, 70% of the Thessalan Consortium's members were annihilated or severely altered (fleshwarped) in a single night.

Sablehook was formed in the aftermath to build the Tri-Weave Shroud—not just to hide from the Templars, but to act as a secondary, artificial seal over these cracked nodes and dimensional tears to prevent a full-scale planar invasion from Xoriat.

## 2. The Draconic Prophecy (The Quay Clauses)
The Draconic Prophecy is a sprawling, incomprehensible tapestry of events that dictate the fate of the world. Two specific clauses relate to Blackwater Quay:

*   *Clause 44-Omicron:* "When the stained-glass wings shield the sunken harbor from the eyes of the sun, the seal shall hold. But should the shadow-stitcher bleed upon the crystal, the door to the howling dark shall swing wide."
    *   **Translation:** Banki (whose fey form has stained-glass wings) is the architect of the Shroud. "Shadow-stitcher" is his true fey title. If Banki's blood is spilled directly onto the Elder Node, the Tri-Weave Shroud will collapse, and the Node will shatter completely. The villains in Act III are actively trying to capture Banki to bleed him on the Node for exactly this reason.

*   *Clause 89-Sigma:* "The child of silver and ash must choose: burn the rot to save the surface, or embrace the rot to save the damned."
    *   **Translation:** This refers to Kael/Mikhailis. He has Templar heritage (silver) but was raised in the Quay (ash). The climax of the campaign requires the players (led by Kael) to decide whether to let the Templars destroy the Quay (saving the surface world from the Node's eventual breach) or fight the Templars to protect the innocents of the Quay (dooming the surface to a slow psychic bleed).

## 3. Ecology of the Void of Madness
The realm on the other side of the Elder Node is not simply "evil." It is a dimension of non-Euclidean geometry and psychic hyper-density. 

*   **The Physics:** In the Void, thought dictates physical reality. If you believe you are burning, you will turn to ash. The entities that live there (Aberrations, Cerebriliths) are not biological; they are coagulated ideas given terrifying physical form.
*   **The Bleed Effect:** The "psychic static" leaking into Blackwater Quay is simply the physics of the Void trying to overwrite the physics of the material plane. This is why biomancy works so well in the Quay—flesh is more malleable when the laws of reality are softening. The mutations seen in the Choir of the Below are not diseases; they are physical adaptations to a new set of physical laws.
