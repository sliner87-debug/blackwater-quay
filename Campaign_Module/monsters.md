## 1. THE CHIRG-ILLITHID (Elder Node Avatar) — CR 11
*A bloated, slime-covered humanoid whose face is a mass of weeping purple tentacles. Its skin is translucent, revealing pulsing brain matter beneath. It is a biological extension of the subterranean Elder Node, acting as its voice and hands on the surface.*

*   **Size/Type:** Large Aberration (Psi-Active)
*   **Hit Dice:** 10d8+50 (95 hp)
*   **Initiative:** +4
*   **Speed:** 30 ft.
*   **Armor Class:** 22 (-1 size, +4 Dex, +9 natural), touch 13, flat-footed 18
*   **Base Attack/Grapple:** +7/+16
*   **Attacks:** 4 Tentacles +11 melee (1d6+5 plus attach)
*   **Space/Reach:** 10 ft. / 10 ft.
*   **Special Attacks:** 
    *   *Mind Blast (Sp):* 60-ft. cone, stun 1d4 rounds, Will DC 20 negates (usable once every 1d4 rounds).
    *   *Extract Brain (Ex):* If the Chirg-Illithid begins its turn with at least one tentacle attached to a grappled foe, it can make a grapple check to maintain. If successful, it attaches all remaining tentacles and extracts the brain, instantly killing the target.
*   **Special Qualities:** Spell Resistance 22, Telepathy 100 ft., Secretion Aura.
*   **Saves:** Fort +8, Ref +7, Will +9
*   **Abilities:** Str 20, Dex 18, Con 20, Int 19, Wis 15, Cha 16
*   **Feats:** Combat Casting, Improved Initiative, Multiattack, Weapon Focus (Tentacle).

### Lore Checks (Knowledge: Dungeon)
*   *DC 15:* A bloated mind-flayer variant spliced by the Elder Node. Its tentacles attach to feed.
*   *DC 20:* It projects a chemical mist (Secretion Aura) that fatigues characters and reduces their willpower, making them vulnerable to psychic control.
*   *DC 25:* Its translucent skin is weak to sonic attacks; sonic damage ignores its natural armor for 1 round.

### Combat Tactics
1.  **Round 1:** Casts *Mind Blast* to stun as many targets as possible.
2.  **Round 2:** Charges the nearest stunned target, using tentacles to grapple.
3.  **Round 3:** Attempts to maintain the grapple and extract the brain, while its Secretion Aura fatigues nearby defenders.

## 2. THE THESSALAN DISPLACER BEAST (Consortium Prototype) — CR 9
*A sleek, feline predator with dark blue fur, but mutated with six eyes, a skeletal jaw, and two long tentacles lined with copper needles and chemical conduits that bleed acidic mutagens.*

*   **Size/Type:** Large Magical Beast
*   **Hit Dice:** 9d10+36 (85 hp)
*   **Initiative:** +3
*   **Speed:** 40 ft.
*   **Armor Class:** 20 (-1 size, +3 Dex, +8 natural), touch 12, flat-footed 17
*   **Base Attack/Grapple:** +9/+18
*   **Attacks:** 2 Tentacles +13 melee (1d8+5 plus mutagenic acid) and Bite +8 melee (1d8+2)
*   **Special Attacks:** 
    *   *Mutagenic Acid (Ex):* Tentacles deal an extra 1d6 acid damage. The target must succeed on a Fort DC 18 save or be *sickened* for 1d4 rounds.
*   **Special Qualities:** 
    *   *Displacement (Su):* The creature warps light, granting a permanent 50% miss chance against all physical and spell attacks. True seeing bypasses this effect.
    *   *Spell Resistance 18.*
*   **Saves:** Fort +10, Ref +9, Will +5
*   **Abilities:** Str 21, Dex 17, Con 18, Int 6, Wis 12, Cha 8
*   **Feats:** Alertness, Combat Reflexes, Dodge, Mobility.

### Lore Checks (Knowledge: Arcana)
*   *DC 15:* A displacer beast prototype bred by the Thessalan Consortium.
*   *DC 20:* Its tentacles secrete a sickening mutagenic acid.
*   *DC 25:* If hit with cold iron weapons, its displacement effect is suppressed for 1 round.

### Combat Tactics
*   The beast uses its speed and Combat Reflexes to strike from 10 feet away. It circles the party, attacking spellcasters to disrupt casting with its sickening acid.

## 5. DREADJAW RELLIS (Orc First Mate) — CR 14
*Originally "Glassjaw Rellis"—a weak, fragile-jawed fence and dockworker of Blackwater Quay—Dreadjaw was captured, physically shattered, and rebuilt through Suture's surgery and Mikhailis's Tri-Weave grafting. Now a terrifying PC-classed enforcer, his lower jaw is a massive fey-metal and cold-iron mandible quenched in dark soul-ichor, and his brain has been integrated into Kael's command staff via a Phrenic-Node graft.*

*   **Size/Type:** Medium Humanoid (Orc, Tri-Weave Grafted)
*   **Class/Level:** Barbarian 12 / Fighter 2 (PC Classes)
*   **Hit Dice:** 12d12+72 plus 2d10+12 (173 hp; 201 hp in rage)
*   **Initiative:** +8 (+2 Dex, +2 inherent Precognitive Reflexes, +4 Improved Initiative)
*   **Speed:** 40 ft. (Fast Movement)
*   **Armor Class:** 24 (+2 Dex, +7 breastplate +2, +5 natural fey-metal jaw/grafts), touch 12, flat-footed 22 (retains Dex bonus to AC when flat-footed due to Precognitive Reflexes; 22 AC in rage)
*   **Base Attack/Grapple:** +14/+22
*   **Attacks:** *+2 Vicious Cold-Iron Greataxe* +25/+20/+15 melee (1d12+14/x3 plus 2d6 vicious damage) or Bite (Tri-Weave Jaw) +22 melee (1d8+8)
*   **Special Attacks:**
    *   *Dread-Shout (Su):* Usable 3/day. Woven with the High Cantor's Spellweaving Lattice, Dreadjaw releases a 40-ft. cone of concussive sonic energy. Deals 10d6 sonic damage and knocks all targets in the area *prone* (DC 23 Fortitude save for half damage and negate knockdown). Targets failing their save are also *deafened* for 1d4 rounds.
    *   *The Obsidian Fey-Iron Bite:* His bite attack naturally bypasses Damage Reduction as if it were Cold Iron, Magic, and Evil.
*   **Special Qualities:** Greater Rage 4/day, Damage Reduction 3/—, Trap Sense +4, Darkvision 60 ft., Orc Ferocity, Sylvan-Pirate Morale (+2 morale bonus on saves against fear/charm to crew within 30 ft.).
*   **Phrenic-Node Enforcer Grafts:**
    *   *The Sovereign Network:* Telepathic communication with Kael, other command officers, and Phrenic Dread-Drakes up to 100 feet away (or miles if routed through Kael's central lattice).
    *   *Alien Mental Fortress:* Spell Resistance (SR 29) specifically against Mind-Affecting spells and effects (Enchantments, Charms, Compulsions, Illusions).
    *   *Precognitive Reflexes:* Retains Dexterity bonus to AC when caught flat-footed, and gains a permanent +2 inherent bonus to Initiative (factored in above).
    *   *Scrag-Nerve Sheathing:* Cloned using Trench's marrow, his nervous system constantly regenerates minor synaptic damage, granting complete immunity to Stunning and Paralysis.
*   **Saves:** Fort +17, Ref +6, Will +7 (+10 in rage)
*   **Abilities:** Str 26, Dex 14, Con 22, Int 10, Wis 8, Cha 10 (Rage: Str 32, Con 28)
*   **Feats:** Power Attack, Cleave, Great Cleave, Improved Initiative, Improved Bull Rush, Shock Trooper, Weapon Focus (Greataxe), Weapon Specialization (Greataxe).

### Combat Tactics
*   **Rage & Charge:** Dreadjaw enters Greater Rage immediately. He uses a Charge action paired with the Shock Trooper feat to deliver a devastating melee strike, converting Power Attack penalties to AC rather than attack rolls.
*   **Concussive Shouts:** He releases a *Dread-Shout* cone to knock down groups of defenders, then executes full attacks against prone targets (+4 to hit prone targets in melee).
*   **Hive Coordination:** Coordinates telepathically with Kael and the Vanguard in absolute silence, targeting opposing spellcasters and prioritizing them for focused assault.

## 6. SEA-WOLF HYBRID (Sablehook Fleet Sentinel) — CR 6
*A sleek, wolf-like humanoid with webbed digits, silver-filmed mirror eyes, and a fur coat that shifts color in water. Upgraded by Suture and Mikhailis with alchemical and mechanical grafts, including adamantine bone bolts and bio-electric shunts.*

*   **Size/Type:** Medium Humanoid (Shapechanger, Tri-Weave Grafted)
*   **Class/Level:** Ranger (Combat Engineer) 5 (PC Classes; Level Adjustment bought off)
*   **Hit Dice:** 5d10+15 (47 hp)
*   **Initiative:** +3 (+3 Dex)
*   **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
*   **Armor Class:** 21 (+3 Dex, +4 natural, +4 *adamantine chest bolts/grafts*), touch 13, flat-footed 18
*   **Base Attack/Grapple:** +5/+7
*   **Attacks:** Bite +7 melee (1d6+2 plus 1d6 electricity plus trip) or 2 Claws +5 melee (1d4+1 plus 1d6 electricity)
*   **Special Attacks:** 
    *   *Trip (Ex):* If a Sea-Wolf hits with a bite attack, it can attempt to trip the opponent as a free action without making a touch attack or provoking an attack of opportunity.
    *   *Electrified Strike (Su):* Wired with bio-electric glands, natural attacks deal an extra 1d6 electricity damage (factored in above).
*   **Special Qualities:** Low-light vision, scent.

    *   *Thermal Nullification:* Invisible to infravision, blindsight (heat/scent), and thermal-tracking arrays.

*   **Trip & Devour:** In melee, they focus on tripping opponents to render them prone, then tear through armor with electrified claw and bite attacks.
