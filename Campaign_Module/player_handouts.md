# Player Handouts (Blackwater Quay)

Game Masters can print these handouts or drop them into their VTT to provide immersive, in-universe lore to the players.

## Handout 1: Smuggler's Contract (Sablehook)
**Given when:** The players accept their first job from Sable in Act I.

> **SABLEHOOK SYNDICATE: FREIGHT MANIFEST & BINDING ACCORD**
> 
> **Cargo:** 3 Crates of Null-Steel Ingots, 1 Crate of Unrefined Blue-Drop.
> **Destination:** The Greybridge Sluices (Checkpoint 4).
> **Courier:** [Player Party Name]
> 
> **Terms of Carriage:**
> 1. The cargo must remain sealed. Exposure to the unrefined Blue-Drop will cause immediate aetheric burn and nullify this contract.
> 2. The couriers are authorized to use lethal force against Consortium biomancers or Choir cultists interfering with the route.
> 3. If Templar patrols are encountered, the couriers must scuttle the cargo in the deep water. Do NOT engage the Inquisition unless trapped.
> 4. Payment (500 gp) will be rendered by Quartermaster Kael upon visual confirmation of delivery at Checkpoint 4.
> 
> *By carrying this manifest, you submit to the jurisdiction of the Sovereign Anchor. Betrayal is punishable by terminal psychic disassembly.*
> 
> **Signed:** *Sable*

## Handout 2: Intercepted Templar Writ
**Given when:** The players loot a dead Templar or raid the Covenant's outpost in Act II.

> **INQUISITION ORDER 88-V**
> **From:** High Inquisitor Thorne
> **To:** Field Commander Aris
> 
> Aris,
> We have confirmed that the psychic static leaking into the lower wards is not a natural phenomenon. The heretics in the "Consortium" are attempting to crack a deep-earth arcane node. 
> 
> You are authorized to deploy the *Scourge-Hounds*. Do not bother arresting the mutated ones—purge them with holy fire. If you encounter the syndicate known as Sablehook, treat them as hostile. Our scryers indicate they are hiding something even more dangerous than the Consortium's vats. We must find the source of the static and seal it, even if we have to collapse the entire cavern to do so.
> 
> Walk in the Light of the Dawn.

## Handout 3: Blood-Stained Note (The Choir)
**Given when:** The players find a dead cultist of the Choir of the Below in Act III.

> *The static sings. It sings!*
> *They think the Shroud protects them, but it only muffles the music. The beautiful, chaotic music of the Void.*
> *The Silver Boy with the fangs... he holds the thread. He holds the needle that sews the Shroud. If we break the thread, the Node will bloom. It will bloom and consume the stone and the water and the Templars.*
> *Find the Silver Boy. Bleed him on the crystal. Let the music out.*
