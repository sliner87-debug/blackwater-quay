# Narrative Random Encounters

When the party is traveling through the Sluices, the Belowmarket, or the unmapped caverns of Blackwater Quay, roll a d20 on the table below. These are not just combat encounters; they provide lore, faction standing opportunities, and narrative hooks.

## The Quay Encounter Table (d20)

| Roll | Encounter | Narrative Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1-2** | **Templar Raid:** A squad of 4 Covenant Templars is kicking down the door of a suspected smuggler den. | Aiding the Templars grants +1 Covenant Renown. Attacking them grants +1 Sablehook Renown but risks a bounty. |
| **3-4** | **The Leaking Vat:** The players find a shattered Thessalan glass vat in an alley. A confused, malformed *Scour-Harrow* is weeping black bile nearby. | The creature is not initially hostile. If calmed (Animal Handling DC 15), it follows the party like a lost dog. |
| **5-6** | **The Whisper-Addict:** A beggar with glowing violet eyes clutches a raw piece of Elder Node crystal, babbling prophecies. | If given 10 gp, he recites a line from the Draconic Prophecy (Clause 44-Omicron), hinting at Banki's true nature. |
| **7-8** | **Githyanki Scouting Skiff:** A small astral skiff silently glides overhead. 2 Githyanki snipers are scoping out Sablehook territory. | If the party lets them go, the Githyanki ambush in Act III is stronger. If destroyed, the party gains +1 Sablehook Renown. |
| **9** | **The Blood-Tax:** 3 thugs from a rival syndicate (The Iron Eels) demand a 50 gp toll to cross a pontoon bridge. | Paying makes the party look weak (Disadvantage on Intimidation checks for 24 hrs). Fighting triggers a 3v4 brawl. |
| **10-12** | **Aetheric Storm:** The Tri-Weave Shroud momentarily fluctuates. Violet lightning arcs through the cavern, dealing 2d6 psychic damage (DC 14 Dex save half). | Spellcasters must succeed on a DC 15 concentration check or lose a random 1st-level spell slot as the static drains their mind. |
| **13-14** | **The Fleshwright's Cart:** A Thessalan merchant is selling "upgrades" out of a reinforced wagon. | Players can purchase a minor mutagen (e.g., +2 Strength for 1 hour, followed by 1 level of Exhaustion) for 100 gp. |
| **15-16** | **Choir Procession:** A dozen cultists of the Choir of the Below march through the knee-deep water, humming a dissonant chord. | If a player joins the humming (Performance DC 13), they are blessed with temporary hit points equal to their level. |
| **17-18** | **Mikhailis's Wake:** The party witnesses Kael brutally executing a traitor on a gantry above them, laughing maniacally before flying away on leathery wings. | An immediate reminder of their patron's terrifying power. Looting the body yields a smuggler's map and 25 gp. |
| **19** | **The Null-Zone:** The party steps into a 30-foot pocket of absolute anti-magic. All spells end, and magic items become mundane. | A remnant of Banki's calculations. It fades after 1d4 hours. A safe place to rest if they are being scried upon. |
| **20** | **Dimensional Bleed:** A localized tear to Xoriat opens for 6 seconds. A *Cerebrilith* steps through, confused and furious. | A highly lethal combat encounter. If the beast is slain, it drops a flawless Aberration Core (worth 1,000 gp). |
