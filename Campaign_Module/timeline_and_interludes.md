# Timeline Dynamics & Inter-Act Interludes

While standard downtime activities exist in Blackwater Quay (carousing, crafting, operating the Shipyards), the periods *between* the major campaign Acts are driven by a dynamic timeline and unique narrative events.

## 1. The Consortium Threat Tracker (The Doomsday Clock)

The Consortium Inquisition does not wait idly for the players to strike. A hidden (or visible, at the DM's discretion) tracker dictates how much downtime the players have between Acts. 

*   **Base Timeline:** Between each major Act, there is typically a **3-Week Window (21 Days)** of downtime for the party to rest, craft, and build renown.

### Accelerators (The Clock Ticks Faster)
If the party leaves a messy trail, the Inquisition mobilizes faster, reducing the available downtime window.
*   **Leaving Witnesses/Survivors:** -3 Days
*   **Massive Public Destruction (Explosions, collapsing buildings):** -5 Days
*   **Failing a Primary Stealth Objective:** -7 Days
*   *Consequence:* The party might only have 1 week of downtime, forcing them to skip apprenticeships, rapid-crafting, or full healing before the next Act begins.

### Decelerators (Buying Time)
If the party covers their tracks or sabotages the enemy, they buy Blackwater Quay precious time to prepare.
*   **Flawless Stealth (No alarms raised):** +5 Days
*   **Assassinating a Key Inquisition Scout/Officer:** +7 Days
*   **Planting False Evidence (Misdirecting the Templars):** +7 Days
*   *Consequence:* The party could push the timeline out to 5 or 6 weeks, allowing them to fully upgrade their dreadnoughts and master new Biomantic grafts.

## 2. Themed Interlude Activities

During the specific gaps between the major Acts, players can choose to engage in **Themed Interludes**. These replace or supplement standard downtime activities and provide unique mechanical benefits for the upcoming Act.

### Interlude 1: The Sluice Fallout (Between Act I & II)
*Following the awakening of the Elder Node and the slaughter in the Sluices, the underworld is in chaos. The Inquisition is sweeping the upper levels, and Sablehook needs to secure its borders.*

*   **Activity A: Scouring the Wreckage**
    *   *The Job:* A high-risk scavenging run back to the Sluices to recover lost Consortium tech before the Inquisition locks it down.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Dexterity (Stealth) or Intelligence (Investigation) check against DC 15.
    *   *Reward:* Success yields 1d4 pieces of **Templar Null-Steel** (worth 500 gp each, or usable to craft magic-dampening armor). Failure results in a close call with an Inquisitor, granting the party a level of Exhaustion from the frantic escape.
*   **Activity B: The Smuggler's Trial**
    *   *The Job:* Proving worth to the Sablehook lieutenants by running a localized, highly illegal spice route through the toxic sewers.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Charisma (Deception) or Wisdom (Survival) check against DC 14.
    *   *Reward:* Success grants +1 Sablehook Renown and a **Smuggler's Token** (can be cashed in to automatically succeed on an Urban Navigation check in Act II).

### Interlude 2: The Flesh & Iron Arms Race (Between Act II & III)
*With the Iron Eels moving against Sablehook and the need for a naval fleet becoming apparent, the focus shifts to war production.*

*   **Activity A: Aberration Harvesting**
    *   *The Job:* Delving into the deep, unmapped caverns to hunt specific aberrations needed by Suture for biomantic ship upgrades.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Strength (Athletics) or Wisdom (Medicine) check against DC 16.
    *   *Reward:* Success yields a **Fleshwarped Carapace Node**. Applying this to a ship grants it Regeneration (10 HP/turn) during the naval battles of Act III. Failure means the hunter becomes the hunted, taking 4d10 acid damage.
*   **Activity B: Industrial Espionage**
    *   *The Job:* Infiltrating the rival Iron Eel syndicate's foundries to steal schematics for their heavy ship weaponry.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) or Charisma (Stealth) check against DC 16.
    *   *Reward:* Success grants the blueprints for the **Eldritch Gatling** or **Lightning Emitter**, unlocking them in the Sovereign Shipyards for half their normal gold cost.

### Interlude 3: The Psionic Siege (Between Act III & IV)
*The dimensional seal is fracturing. Xoriat's madness leaks into the Quay. The final preparations must be made before the Descent.*

*   **Activity A: Static Meditation**
    *   *The Job:* Highly dangerous communion with the Elder Node to build psionic resistance against the incoming Deepmind assault.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Constitution or Wisdom saving throw against DC 18.
    *   *Reward:* Success grants the character a **Mind-Vault Aura** (Advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws for the entirety of Act IV). Failure results in rolling on the Short-Term Madness table and starting Act IV with 1 level of Exhaustion.
*   **Activity B: The Last Call**
    *   *The Job:* Securing final alliances (or assassinating dissenting gang bosses) to ensure the entire underworld is unified to hold the line while the PCs descend.
    *   *Mechanic:* Make a Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check against DC 17.
    *   *Reward:* Success grants the **United Front** perk. During Act IV, if a PC drops to 0 hit points, a loyalist sniper or saboteur from the surface triggers a distraction, allowing the PC to immediately roll a hit die and stabilize.
