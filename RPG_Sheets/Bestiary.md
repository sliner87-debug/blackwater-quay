# 1. Reedsinger (The Hollow Stag)
*CR 9 | Large Fey (Plant Subtype)*
*Alignment: Neutral (N)*

### Description
The Reedsinger is an ancient forest guardian—a walking mountain of bark and braided roots. Its hollow chest burns with a deep, liquid amber light, and golden sap drips from its sprawling antlers. It radiates a territorial pressure that physically compresses the lungs of mortals.

---

* **HD:** 12d6+48 (90 hp)
* **Initiative:** +1 | **Speed:** 40 ft.
* **AC:** 21 (-1 size, +1 Dex, +11 natural), Touch 10, Flat-footed 20
* **Base Attack/Grapple:** +6/+16
* **Attack:** Antlers +11 melee (2d6+6 plus amber sap)
* **Full Attack:** Antlers +11 melee (2d6+6 plus amber sap) and 2 hooves +6 melee (1d8+3)
* **Space/Reach:** 10 ft./10 ft.
* **Special Attacks:**
  * *Amber Sap (Ex):* A target hit by the Reedsinger’s antler attack must make a DC 20 Reflex save or become *entangled* by sticky golden sap for 1d4 rounds.
  * *Territorial Pressure (Su):* The Reedsinger radiates an aura of heavy gravity. Creatures within 30 ft. must make a DC 18 Fortitude save each round or become *fatigued* and take a -2 penalty to attack rolls and checks.
* **Special Qualities:** DR 10/Cold Iron, low-light vision, plant immunities, vulnerability to fire.
* **Saves:** Fort +8, Ref +9, Will +10
* **Abilities:** Str 22, Dex 12, Con 18, Int 8, Wis 16, Cha 14
* **Skills:** Hide +12, Listen +15, Spot +15, Survival +12
