# Appendix: Biomantic Rules & Playable Races
This appendix details the unique mechanics for playing characters who have survived the Thessalan Consortium's experiments in Blackwater Quay, compatible with Pathfinder 1E and D&D 3.5e.

---

## Playable Racial Templates

### PixIllithid (Fey/Aberration Hybrid)
Like Banki, these creatures were once fey who had their neural pathways spliced with illithid tadpoles. They possess wings, but their minds are cold, alien calculators.

**Racial Traits (CR +1 Template or Base Race):**
*   **Type:** Aberration (Augmented Fey).
*   **Size:** Small or Medium.
*   **Speed:** Base speed 30 ft. Fly speed 30 ft (Average).
*   **Stat Adjustments:** +2 Int, +2 Dex, -2 Str, -2 Cha.
*   **Closed-Loop Telepathy:** Can communicate telepathically with any willing creature within 60 ft. Immune to the Elder Node's ambient psychic whispers and *detect thoughts*.
*   **Psionic Spark:** Gains 3 Power Points (or a 1st-level spell slot) and the ability to manifest *Mind Thrust* or cast *Magic Missile* once per day as a spell-like ability (CL = character level).
*   **Vulnerability:** Takes a -2 penalty on saves against sonic effects, as their delicate cranial wings are sensitive to vibrations.

### Half-Minotaur Fey (Monstrous Humanoid)
Like Kael, these towering brutes are the result of splicing sylvan bloodlines with labyrinthine behemoths. They are inherently tied to temporal magic.

**Racial Traits (CR +1 Template or Base Race):**
*   **Type:** Monstrous Humanoid.
*   **Size:** Large (Tall). Space 10 ft., Reach 10 ft.
*   **Speed:** Base speed 40 ft.
*   **Stat Adjustments:** +4 Str, +2 Con, -2 Int.
*   **Natural Armor:** +2 natural armor bonus.
*   **Chronal Charge:** Once per day, when making a charge attack, the Half-Minotaur can bend time. They do not provoke attacks of opportunity during the charge and gain a +4 bonus to the attack roll instead of +2.
*   **Eldritch Claws:** Possesses two primary claw attacks (1d6 damage). These claws are treated as magic weapons for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

### Void-Touched Aberration (Humanoid/Aberration)
Like Mira, these subjects were exposed directly to planar radiation from The Void of Madness, warping their flesh and granting them unsettling spatial awareness.

**Racial Traits (Base Race):**
*   **Type:** Humanoid (Aberrant subtype).
*   **Size:** Medium.
*   **Speed:** Base speed 30 ft.
*   **Stat Adjustments:** +2 Dex, +2 Wis, -2 Con.
*   **Void-Sight:** Darkvision 60 ft. Furthermore, they can see invisible creatures and objects (as *see invisibility*) out to 15 ft.
*   **Spatial Glitch:** Once per day, as an immediate action when hit by an attack, the Void-Touched can phase out of reality, granting them a 50% miss chance against that specific attack.
*   **Unsettling Presence:** -2 penalty on Diplomacy and Handle Animal checks.

## Alchemy & Surgery Mechanics

### Thessalan Mutagens (Alchemical Crafting)
Using the formulas stolen from the Fleshwarped Foundry, characters (especially Alchemists like Suture) can brew specialized mutagens. 

*   **Blue-Drop (Craft DC 20, 200 gp):** A glowing blue fluid. When ingested, the user gains a +4 alchemical bonus to Intelligence and Wisdom, and telepathy 30 ft for 1 hour. However, they take 1d4 points of Charisma damage as their emotions flatten.
*   **Thessalan Suppressant (Craft DC 25, 500 gp):** A vile green paste. When applied topically, the user becomes completely immune to the Elder Node's ambient psychic attacks, *dominate person*, and *confusion* for 24 hours. However, magical healing spells only heal half the normal amount while suppressed.

### Aberration Core Harvesting
When a monstrous aberration (like a Dolgaunt or Cerebrilith) is slain, a character with the **Heal** or **Knowledge (Dungeoneering)** skill can attempt to harvest its cerebral core.

*   **The Process:** Harvesting takes 10 minutes and a successful **DC 20 skill check**.
*   **The Result:** A successful harvest yields 1d4 **Aberrant Cores** (worth 500 gp each on the Sablehook black market, or usable as material components to reduce the crafting cost of magic items by 10%).
*   **Failure:** Failing the check by 5 or more bursts the core, dealing 2d6 acid damage to the harvester.

### Suture's Graft Surgery (Optional Rule)
A character can undergo surgery to attach aberrant limbs or organs (e.g., displacer tentacles or chameleonic skin). This requires a DC 25 Heal check by the surgeon and takes 8 hours. 

If the surgery is successful, the PC gains the graft's benefit (e.g., a secondary tentacle attack). If the surgery fails, roll on the **Graft Failure Table**:

| d% Roll | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **01-40** | **Rejection:** The graft dies and falls off. The PC takes 2d6 Con damage. |
| **41-70** | **Nerve Damage:** The graft works, but the PC takes a permanent -2 penalty to Dexterity. |
| **71-90** | **Psychic Echo:** The graft connects to the Elder Node. The PC takes a -4 penalty on saves against mind-affecting effects. |
| **91-100**| **Fleshwarp:** The PC mutates uncontrollably, becoming an NPC under the DM's control until a *Heal* or *Greater Restoration* spell is cast. |
