# 1. Aldric Thorne
*Role: Paladin of the Sun-Spear / Inquisitor*
*Alignment: Lawful Neutral (LN) (former Lawful Good)*

### Description
Aldric Thorne is a crusader dedicated to the Dawnfather. Dressed in common wool but carrying the rigid bearing of plate armor, he is a zealot driven by absolute certainty. Methodical and patient, he is willing to commit brutal acts (such as torture) if it serves the greater good and saves innocent lives. He recently lost his sword hand to an abyssal creature on a rain-slicked bridge, now wielding his relic blade in his off-hand.

---

* **Race/Class:** Human Paladin 9 / Grey Guard 3
* **Abilities:** Str 16 (14 without hand), Dex 12, Con 16, Int 10, Wis 15, Cha 16
* **Combat:** HP 96, AC 21 (Touch 11, Flat-footed 20), Init +1, Speed 20 ft.
* **Saves:** Fort +12, Ref +6, Will +9; Divine Grace (+3 to all saves)
* **Skills:** Concentration +10, Diplomacy +8, Intimidate +14, Knowledge (Religion) +8, Sense Motive +12
* **Feats:** Divine Might, Extra Smiting, Iron Will, Power Attack, Shield Specialization, Weapon Focus (Longsword)
* **Special Abilities:**
  * *Sacrament of Trust (Ex):* As a Grey Guard, Aldric can perform acts that would normally violate a paladin's code of conduct (such as using deception, torture, or dirty tactics) without losing his paladin class features, as long as it serves the faith or saves innocents.
  * *Lay on Hands (Su):* Can heal up to 36 HP/day.
  * *Smite Evil (Su):* 3/day, +3 attack, +12 damage against an evil foe.
  * *One-Handed Fighting (Ex):* Aldric has adapted to the loss of his primary hand, taking no penalties for off-hand combat but unable to wield two-handed weapons or use a shield.
* **Equipment:** *Consecrated Relic Sword* (+2 holy lawful longsword), masterwork full plate.
