# 1. Banki (Sovereign of the Third Quiet)
*Role: Syndicate Leader / Neural Savant*
*Alignment: Neutral Evil (NE)*

### Description
Banki is a Pixie who underwent horrific experimental surgeries by the Thessalan Continuum, transforming his fragile fey biology into a fluid, highly malleable weapon. He hides behind a remarkably unremarkable human sellsword face. He is the telepathic ruler of the syndicate, managing his network via a psionic "Sovereign Thread." He prefers cold, surgical strikes and patience to anger.

---

* **Skills:** Bluff +18, Concentration +18, Diplomacy +16, Gather Information +14, Intimidate +12, Knowledge (Arcane) +18, Knowledge (Psionics) +22, Psicraft +22, Sense Motive +18

* **Psionic Powers (ML 13, Power Points 188):**

  * *2nd—* Brain Lock, Ego Whip, Mental Barrier, Share Pain.
  * *3rd—* Crisis of Breath, Hostile Empathic Transfer, Mind Trap.
  * *4th—* Dominate (Psionic), Modify Memory (Psionic), Schism.
  * *5th—* Mind Probe, Psychic Crush, Telepathic Send.
  * *6th—* Mind Switch, Retrieve.

  * *Fey Polymorph (Su):* As a swift action, Banki can alter his flesh, adopting any humanoid form as per *Polymorph* but retaining his stats and psionic abilities.
  * *Sovereign Thread (Su):* Banki can maintain permanent telepathic links with up to 10 willing or dominated subjects. This functions as a continuous *Mindlink* and allows him to direct them telepathically as a free action.
  * *Neural Consumption (Su):* If Banki dominates a target, he can spend 1 hour of concentration to permanently consume their memories, absorbing 1 skill rank or specific memory. This causes the target 1d4 Int drain.
