# Appendix: Relics of the Deepmind Tear
This appendix details the unique magical items and biomantic tools utilized in the *Blackwater Quay* campaign. All items include mechanics for both **Pathfinder 1E** and **D&D 3.5e**.

---

## The Null-Fang
*Wondrous Item (Implement/Dagger), Minor Artifact*
**Slot:** None (Held) | **Weight:** 1 lb. | **Aura:** Overwhelming Abjuration

Wielded by Banki, this obsidian ritual dagger is carved with pulsing blue ley-runes. It does not deal physical damage; instead, it severs the "Sovereign Thread" of magic.

**Mechanics (PF1E & D&D 3.5e):**
*   **Arcane Severance:** As a standard action, the wielder can make a melee touch attack against a spellcaster or psion. On a hit, the target must succeed on a **DC 22 Will save** or lose 1d4 of their highest-level spell slots (or 1d4 x 5 Power Points) and be unable to cast spells or manifest powers for 1 round.
*   **Tri-Weave Negation Field:** Once per day, as an immediate action, the wielder can plunge the Null-Fang into the ground. This creates a 30-ft radius *Antimagic Field* (as the spell, CL 20) that lasts for 1 minute. However, allies of the wielder can ignore the field if they are attuned to the Tri-Weave Shroud.

## Tri-Weave Silver Blade
*Weapon (Greatsword or Shortsword), Legendary*
**Slot:** None | **Weight:** 8 lbs. | **Aura:** Strong Evocation and Abjuration

Reforged from the Githyanki Commander's silver sword and tempered in Thessalan mutagens, this blade phases between three states of existence: Arcane, Psionic, and Divine.

**Mechanics (PF1E & D&D 3.5e):**
*   **Enhancement:** *+3 Silver Greatsword* (or Shortsword).
*   **Phase Strike:** On a successful hit, the wielder can choose to change the damage type to Force, Sonic, or regular slashing. 
*   **Spell Resistance Bypass:** Attacks made with this weapon ignore up to 10 points of the target's Spell Resistance or Power Resistance. If the target has DR/Epic, this weapon bypasses it.
*   **Astral Severance:** On a critical hit against an extraplanar creature, the creature must succeed on a **DC 18 Will save** or be affected by *Dimensional Anchor* for 1d4 rounds.

## Chronal Chronometer
*Wondrous Item, Rare*
**Slot:** Neck or Pocket | **Weight:** 1 lb. | **Aura:** Moderate Transmutation

A jagged, brass pocket watch used by Captain Kael to navigate the gravity flues of the Deepmind Annex. Its hands spin wildly until it approaches a planar tear, where it locks onto the local timeline.

**Mechanics (PF1E & D&D 3.5e):**
*   **Chronal Anchor:** The wearer gains a +4 insight bonus to Initiative checks and cannot be caught flat-footed. 
*   **Time Slip (3/day):** As a swift action, the wearer can activate the watch to gain the effects of *Haste* for 1 round. 
*   **Gravity Navigation:** The wearer is immune to the negative effects of the "Gravity Distortion" daily intrusion and gains a fly speed equal to their base land speed (average maneuverability) while within 100 ft of a planar tear (like the Elder Node).

## Siphon Syringe
*Wondrous Item (Alchemical Tool), Uncommon*
**Slot:** None | **Weight:** 0.5 lbs. | **Aura:** Faint Necromancy

A cruel-looking glass and brass syringe used by Suture. It extracts vital humors from freshly killed aberrations to distill into potent, if unstable, mutagens.

**Mechanics (PF1E & D&D 3.5e):**
*   **Harvest Essence:** As a full-round action, the user can extract the essence of a dead aberration (must be dead no longer than 1 hour). This generates 1 "Daelkyr Charge". The syringe holds a maximum of 3 charges.
*   **Inject Mutagen:** As a standard action, the user can inject themselves or a willing ally with a charge. The recipient heals **3d8+5 HP** but must succeed on a **DC 15 Fortitude save** or suffer a random minor mutation for 1 hour (e.g., -2 Charisma, light blindness, or a vestigial tentacle).

## FateShear (Sentient Greatsword)
*Weapon (Greatsword), Major Artifact*
**Slot:** None | **Weight:** 8 lbs. | **Aura:** Overwhelming Divination

A haughty, intelligent greatsword forged from a shard of the Draconic Prophecy. It views itself as a grand conductor of fate and talks down to its wielder.

**Mechanics (PF1E & D&D 3.5e):**
*   **Enhancement:** *+4 Keen Adamantine Greatsword*.
*   **Sentience:** AL: LN; INT 18, WIS 14, CHA 20. Telepathy (120 ft), darkvision, hearing. Speaks Common, Draconic, and Deep Speech.
*   **Prophetic Strike:** Once per day, before making an attack roll, the wielder can declare a Prophetic Strike. The attack automatically rolls a natural 20. However, FateShear demands a price: the next time the wielder is attacked, the attacker gains a +10 insight bonus on their attack roll.
*   **Precognitive Parry:** The wielder gains a +2 insight bonus to AC as the sword twitches to intercept incoming blows.
