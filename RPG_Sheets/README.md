# Blackwater Quay RPG Sheets Index (D&D 3.5 & Pathfinder 1E)

Welcome to the RPG translation index for *Blackwater Quay* (Sablehook Master Draft). This directory contains roleplaying game character sheets (D&D 3.5 / Pathfinder 1E) and bestiary stat blocks for every named individual and creature in the novel. 

The conversions prioritize **flavour, feel, and immersion** over mechanical rigidity, using diverse official and third-party material (such as psionics, warlock invocations, binders, shadow magic, and unique templates) to reflect the dark, gritty fantasy of the book.

## 🐉 Named Monsters & Beings (Bestiary)

Stat blocks for the unique fauna and planar monstrosities of the setting:
1. **Reedsinger (Hollow Stag)**: Fey giant of bark, golden sap, and amber light.
2. **Dire Displacer Rhino**: Armored hybrid beast of burden with spatial displacement.
3. **Displacer Beast**: Sinuous six-legged feline predator with spiked tentacles.
4. **Cerebrilith**: Psionic abyssal demon that feeds on neural essence.
5. **Elder Node**: Abyssal entity that manifests as a shadow, draining Tri-Weave and mind.
6. **Scour-Harrow**: A horrific, segmented tunneling siege beast made of vertebrae and silver wire.
7. **Larklet & Cinder**: Tiny stitched construct companions designed for mental grounding.
8. **Aurelion**: Massive warding construct acting as a mobile containment field.
9. **Manta**: Shimmering, drifting planar membrane beast attracted to magic.
