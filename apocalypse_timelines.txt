========================================================================
THE 28 TRAGIC PATHWAYS: APOCALYPSE METRICS AND THE TOP 5 WORST REACTIONS
========================================================================

This document details the fallout of the twenty-eight (28) timelines where Banki and Kael (Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor) lost themselves to rage, trauma, and hatred during their alliance with the Thessalan Consortium. It categorizes the scale of destruction and highlights the five most horrific outcomes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METRICS OF DESTRUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Across the 28 failed timelines:

* EXTREME BYSTANDER CASUALTIES: 19 Instances
  In these timelines, the physical or psionic fallout of Banki and Kael's rage leveled the city of Blackwater Quay, wiped out the syndicate's enclaves, or killed tens of thousands of innocent civilians in the surrounding Western Approach.

* APOCALYPSE SCALE REACHED: 9 Instances
  In these timelines, the failure to secure the rift or the chaotic magic unleashed by their madness resulted in global or regional cataclysms—collapsing the boundaries between Khorvaire and Xoriat, merging planes, or creating self-expanding void-storms.

* DIVINE VENGEANCE ON AN UNHEARD-OF SCALE: 6 Instances
  In these timelines, Banki or Kael tapped into the divine channels of the Tri-Weave or their racial/planar connections (Fey courts, Shadar-kai death-rites) to ascend into semi-divine agents of torment, rewriting the physical laws of life and death to punish the Consortium.

---

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TOP 5 WORST REACTIONS TO THE CONSORTIUM
------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCENARIO C5: THE SOVEREIGN SINGULARITY (The Dual-Mind feedback)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Trigger:
  Banki and Kael attempt to permanently fuse their minds into a dual-identity to withstand the Elder Brain's psychic signals.
* The Reaction:
  The union of Kael's crushing guilt (as the architect of the cages) and Banki's agonizing trauma (as the subject) creates an infinite mental feedback loop. The merged entity ascends into a colossal, semi-divine aberration of psionic glass and shadow. Driven mad by its own conflicting memories, it declares both the Consortium and the city of Blackwater Quay to be "imperfect, painful constructs."
* The Vengeance:
  It channels raw divine and psionic authority through the Tri-Weave, systematically dismantling the molecular and spiritual bonds of everything within a fifty-mile radius. The entire Western Approach is instantly transformed into a silent, crystalline desert of screaming, glass statues.
* Scale: Regional Apocalypse / Extreme Bystander Casualties.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCENARIO D7: THE NIGHTMARE COLLISION (The Dream-Madness Warzone)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Trigger:
  Desperate to close the Xoriat tear, Kael uses his chronal magic to tear open a portal to Dal Quor (Plane of Dreams), intending to let the Quori fight the Daelkyr.
* The Reaction:
  The two extraplanar energies do not cancel out; they merge. Xoriat's madness infects the dreamscape of Dal Quor. Tapping into fey-divinity and psionics, Banki constructs a physical "nightmare army" out of the Consortium's worst fears.
* The Vengeance:
  The entire city of Blackwater Quay is dragged into the rift. The citizens' dreams are turned into physical claws that rip their hosts apart. Banki and Kael, completely lost to the horror, rule over a shifting, screaming landscape of dream-madness that slowly expands across the continent of Khorvaire.
* Scale: Global Apocalypse / Divine Vengeance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SCENARIO B1: THE HUSHFORGE SHATTERING (The Great Silence)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Trigger:
  Banki attempts to use the Null-Fang to negate the Consortium's neural collars, but the Consortium triggers an overload, shattering the implement's hushforge core.
* The Reaction:
  The psychic and acoustic dampers fail. Every silent, repressed scream of Banki's childhood is amplified into a physical shockwave. Banki taps into the Tri-Weave's divine channel to enforce the "Great Hush."
* The Vengeance:
  A wave of absolute, deadly silence sweeps through the city. Anyone who speaks, screams, or thinks too loudly has their head implode. In his madness, Banki uses the Null-Fang to sew the mouths and ears of the Consortium directors shut with divine silver thread, keeping them alive as silent, sensing puppets in the dark vaults below Sablehook.
* Scale: Extreme Bystander Casualties.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SCENARIO A7: THE SHADOW-PLAGUE OF KAEL (The Raven Queen's Descent)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Trigger:
  Kael (Mikhailis) sacrifices his own chronal anchor, decaying into a mindless shadow-wraith to shield Banki.
* The Reaction:
  Seeing Kael decay, Banki loses all restraint. He taps into Kael's Shadar-kai connection to the Shadowfell, channeling the divine authority of death and fate.
* The Vengeance:
  He turns Kael's decaying essence into a contagious "Shadow-Plague." The plague sweeps through the Consortium's labs and out into the city streets. Anyone touched by the shadow has their soul shredded and spun into alchemical binders, just like the old Stitchroom. The city becomes a silent necropolis of empty leather clothes and floating, weeping spirits.
* Scale: Extreme Bystander Casualties / Divine Vengeance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SCENARIO B8: THE TOVIN REBOUND (The Flesh-Fused City)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Trigger:
  They attempt to infect the Consortium with a psionic virus reverse-engineered from Tovin's memory of the "biscuit," but the virus mutates in the presence of Xoriat's energy and rebounds.
* The Reaction:
  The mutated virus infects the Vael-Kaelor collective. In their madness, Banki and Kael perceive the entire city of Blackwater Quay as a "malfunctional, disorganized laboratory" that needs to be refined.
* The Vengeance:
  They use their divine Tri-Weave magic to perform a massive, city-wide "refinement surgery." They physically and psionically fuse every citizen, guard, and Consortium member into a single, massive, writhing collective organism—a city-sized hive mind that shares only Banki and Kael's trauma, screaming in unison under the foggy skies.
* Scale: Extreme Bystander Casualties / Local Reality Broken.
