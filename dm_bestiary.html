1. THE CHIRG-ILLITHID (Elder Node Avatar) — CR 11

A bloated, slime-covered humanoid whose face is a mass of weeping purple tentacles. Its skin is translucent, revealing pulsing brain matter beneath. It is a biological extension of the subterranean Elder Node, acting as its voice and hands on the surface.

  • Size/Type: Large Aberration (Psi-Active)
  • Hit Dice: 10d8+50 (95 hp)
  • Initiative: +4
  • Speed: 30 ft.
  • Armor Class: 22 (-1 size, +4 Dex, +9 natural), touch 13, flat-footed 18
  • Base Attack/Grapple: +7/+16
  • Attacks: 4 Tentacles +11 melee (1d6+5 plus attach)
  • Space/Reach: 10 ft. / 10 ft.
  • Special Attacks:
    • Mind Blast (Sp): 60-ft. cone, stun 1d4 rounds, Will DC 20 negates (usable once every 1d4 rounds).
    • Extract Brain (Ex): If the Chirg-Illithid begins its turn with at least one tentacle attached to a grappled foe, it can make a grapple check to maintain. If successful, it attaches all remaining tentacles and extracts the brain, instantly killing the target.
  • Special Qualities: Spell Resistance 22, Telepathy 100 ft., Secretion Aura.
  • Saves: Fort +8, Ref +7, Will +9
  • Abilities: Str 20, Dex 18, Con 20, Int 19, Wis 15, Cha 16
  • Feats: Combat Casting, Improved Initiative, Multiattack, Weapon Focus (Tentacle).

Lore Checks (Knowledge: Dungeon)

  • DC 15: A bloated mind-flayer variant spliced by the Elder Node. Its tentacles attach to feed.
  • DC 20: It projects a chemical mist (Secretion Aura) that fatigues characters and reduces their willpower, making them vulnerable to psychic control.
  • DC 25: Its translucent skin is weak to sonic attacks; sonic damage ignores its natural armor for 1 round.

Combat Tactics

  1. Round 1: Casts Mind Blast to stun as many targets as possible.
  2. Round 2: Charges the nearest stunned target, using tentacles to grapple.
  3. Round 3: Attempts to maintain the grapple and extract the brain, while its Secretion Aura fatigues nearby defenders.

A sleek, feline predator with dark blue fur, but mutated with six eyes, a skeletal jaw, and two long tentacles lined with copper needles and chemical conduits that bleed acidic mutagens.

  • Initiative: +3
  • Speed: 40 ft.
  • Armor Class: 20 (-1 size, +3 Dex, +8 natural), touch 12, flat-footed 17
  • Base Attack/Grapple: +9/+18
  • Attacks: 2 Tentacles +13 melee (1d8+5 plus mutagenic acid) and Bite +8 melee (1d8+2)
  • Special Attacks:
    • Mutagenic Acid (Ex): Tentacles deal an extra 1d6 acid damage. The target must succeed on a Fort DC 18 save or be sickened for 1d4 rounds.
  • Special Qualities:
    • Displacement (Su): The creature warps light, granting a permanent 50% miss chance against all physical and spell attacks. True seeing bypasses this effect.

  • Abilities: Str 21, Dex 17, Con 18, Int 6, Wis 12, Cha 8

  • DC 25: If hit with cold iron weapons, its displacement effect is suppressed for 1 round.

Combat Tactics

  • The beast uses its speed and Combat Reflexes to strike from 10 feet away. It circles the party, attacking spellcasters to disrupt casting with its sickening acid.

Combat Tactics

5. DREADJAW RELLIS (Orc First Mate) — CR 14

Originally "Glassjaw Rellis"—a weak, fragile-jawed fence and dockworker of Blackwater Quay—Dreadjaw was captured, physically shattered, and rebuilt through Suture's surgery and Mikhailis's Tri-Weave grafting. Now a terrifying PC-classed enforcer, his lower jaw is a massive fey-metal and cold-iron mandible quenched in dark soul-ichor, and his brain has been integrated into Kael's command staff via a Phrenic-Node graft.

  • Size/Type: Medium Humanoid (Orc, Tri-Weave Grafted)
  • Class/Level: Barbarian 12 / Fighter 2 (PC Classes)
  • Hit Dice: 12d12+72 plus 2d10+12 (173 hp; 201 hp in rage)
  • Initiative: +8 (+2 Dex, +2 inherent Precognitive Reflexes, +4 Improved Initiative)
  • Speed: 40 ft. (Fast Movement)
  • Armor Class: 24 (+2 Dex, +7 breastplate +2, +5 natural fey-metal jaw/grafts), touch 12, flat-footed 22 (retains Dex bonus to AC when flat-footed due to Precognitive Reflexes; 22 AC in rage)
  • Base Attack/Grapple: +14/+22
  • Attacks: +2 Vicious Cold-Iron Greataxe +25/+20/+15 melee (1d12+14/x3 plus 2d6 vicious damage) or Bite (Tri-Weave Jaw) +22 melee (1d8+8)
  • Special Attacks:
    • Dread-Shout (Su): Usable 3/day. Woven with the High Cantor's Spellweaving Lattice, Dreadjaw releases a 40-ft. cone of concussive sonic energy. Deals 10d6 sonic damage and knocks all targets in the area prone (DC 23 Fortitude save for half damage and negate knockdown). Targets failing their save are also deafened for 1d4 rounds.
    • The Obsidian Fey-Iron Bite: His bite attack naturally bypasses Damage Reduction as if it were Cold Iron, Magic, and Evil.
  • Special Qualities: Greater Rage 4/day, Damage Reduction 3/—, Trap Sense +4, Darkvision 60 ft., Orc Ferocity, Sylvan-Pirate Morale (+2 morale bonus on saves against fear/charm to crew within 30 ft.).
  • Phrenic-Node Enforcer Grafts:
    • The Sovereign Network: Telepathic communication with Kael, other command officers, and Phrenic Dread-Drakes up to 100 feet away (or miles if routed through Kael's central lattice).
    • Alien Mental Fortress: Spell Resistance (SR 29) specifically against Mind-Affecting spells and effects (Enchantments, Charms, Compulsions, Illusions).
    • Precognitive Reflexes: Retains Dexterity bonus to AC when caught flat-footed, and gains a permanent +2 inherent bonus to Initiative (factored in above).
    • Scrag-Nerve Sheathing: Cloned using Trench's marrow, his nervous system constantly regenerates minor synaptic damage, granting complete immunity to Stunning and Paralysis.
  • Saves: Fort +17, Ref +6, Will +7 (+10 in rage)
  • Abilities: Str 26, Dex 14, Con 22, Int 10, Wis 8, Cha 10 (Rage: Str 32, Con 28)
  • Feats: Power Attack, Cleave, Great Cleave, Improved Initiative, Improved Bull Rush, Shock Trooper, Weapon Focus (Greataxe), Weapon Specialization (Greataxe).

Combat Tactics

  • Rage & Charge: Dreadjaw enters Greater Rage immediately. He uses a Charge action paired with the Shock Trooper feat to deliver a devastating melee strike, converting Power Attack penalties to AC rather than attack rolls.
  • Concussive Shouts: He releases a Dread-Shout cone to knock down groups of defenders, then executes full attacks against prone targets (+4 to hit prone targets in melee).
  • Hive Coordination: Coordinates telepathically with Kael and the Vanguard in absolute silence, targeting opposing spellcasters and prioritizing them for focused assault.

6. SEA-WOLF HYBRID (Sablehook Fleet Sentinel) — CR 6

A sleek, wolf-like humanoid with webbed digits, silver-filmed mirror eyes, and a fur coat that shifts color in water. Upgraded by Suture and Mikhailis with alchemical and clockwork prosthetics, including adamantine bone bolts and aether-conductive nerve grafts.

  • Size/Type: Medium Humanoid (Shapechanger, Tri-Weave Grafted)
  • Class/Level: Ranger (Combat Engineer) 5 (PC Classes; Level Adjustment bought off)
  • Hit Dice: 5d10+15 (47 hp)
  • Initiative: +3 (+3 Dex)
  • Speed: 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
  • Armor Class: 21 (+3 Dex, +4 natural, +4 adamantine chest bolts/grafts), touch 13, flat-footed 18
  • Base Attack/Grapple: +5/+7
  • Attacks: Bite +7 melee (1d6+2 plus 1d6 electricity plus trip) or 2 Claws +5 melee (1d4+1 plus 1d6 electricity)
  • Special Attacks:
    • Trip (Ex): If a Sea-Wolf hits with a bite attack, it can attempt to trip the opponent as a free action without making a touch attack or provoking an attack of opportunity.
    • Electrified Strike (Su): Wired with bio-electric glands, natural attacks deal an extra 1d6 electricity damage (factored in above).
  • Special Qualities: Low-light vision, scent.
    • Aqueous Camouflage: Grants a permanent +8 racial bonus to Hide checks underwater.
    • Thermal Nullification: Invisible to infravision, blindsight (heat/scent), and thermal-tracking arrays.
    • Lycanthropic Shunt: Swim speed 40 ft. Breathes underwater, and can hold breath for up to 1 hour per Constitution modifier (3 hours).
    • Adamantine Chest Bolts: Interlocking metal plates bolted to the collarbones and ribcage grant Damage Reduction 2/adamantine.
  • Saves: Fort +7, Ref +7, Will +2
  • Abilities: Str 14, Dex 16, Con 16, Int 10, Wis 12, Cha 8
  • Feats: Combat Reflexes, Multiattack, Stealthy, Track.

Combat Tactics

  • Aqueous Ambush: Sea-Wolves hide in underwater locks and bypass channels using Aqueous Camouflage and Thermal Nullification. They wait for target vessels to pass, swimming silently at 40 ft., climbing the hull, and dragging guards into the water.
  • Trip & Devour: In melee, they focus on tripping opponents to render them prone, then tear through armor with electrified claw and bite attacks.

10. SHADAR-KAI CORSAIR (Sablehook Shock-Trooper) — CR 6

A cold, pragmatic shadow-fey corsair dressed in leather brigandine, wielding spiked chains and hand crossbows. Rebuilt by Suture and Kael with displacer glands and Illithid-kin brain nodes, they operate as a highly synchronized, out-of-phase boarding force.

  • Size/Type: Medium Humanoid (Elf, Shadar-Kai, Tri-Weave Grafted)
  • Class/Level: Rogue 3 / Fighter 3 (PC Classes; Level Adjustment bought off)
  • Hit Dice: 3d6+6 plus 3d10+6 (41 hp)
  • Initiative: +8 (+4 Dex, +2 inherent Spinal Surge, +2 improved initiative)
  • Speed: 30 ft.
  • Armor Class: 19 (+4 Dex, +4 mithral shirt, +1 natural), touch 14, flat-footed 15 (retains Dex bonus to AC when flat-footed due to Precognitive Reflexes)
  • Base Attack/Grapple: +5/+6
  • Attacks: +1 Cold-Iron Spiked Chain +10 melee (2d4+2, reach 10 ft.) or Hand Crossbow +9 ranged (1d4/19-20 plus poison)
  • Special Attacks: Sneak Attack +2d6, Poison Use (standard drow/shadar-kai knockout poison).
  • Special Qualities: Darkvision 60 ft., Shadow Blend, Hide in Plain Sight.
    • Phase-Stutter Skin (Displacer Graft): Grants a permanent 20% miss chance (concealment) from the Displacement weave. As an immediate action 3/day, they can "stutter" in response to an attack, teleporting up to 10 feet instantly to avoid a hit or area effect.
    • Spinal Surge (Illithid Graft): Grants lesser mindsight (30 ft.) and telepathic communication with the ship's Command Triad up to 100 feet.
    • Precognitive Reflexes: Retains Dexterity bonus to AC when caught flat-footed, and gains a permanent +2 inherent bonus to Initiative (factored in above).

  • Abilities: Str 12, Dex 18, Con 14, Int 12, Wis 10, Cha 8 (Base stats adjusted by level upgrades)
  • Feats: Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Spiked Chain), Combat Reflexes, Weapon Finesse, Improved Initiative, Dodge.

Combat Tactics

  • Silent Boarding: Shadar-Kai corsairs use Shadow Blend and Hide in Plain Sight to climb ropes and slip aboard target vessels completely unseen.
  • Chain & Trip: They use spiked chains to trip and disarm defenders from a 10-foot reach, setting up sneak attacks for their allies.
  • Phase-Stutter Defense: When targeted by high-damage spells or critical attacks, they use Phase-Stutter to teleport 10 feet away, leaving behind a fading mirror image.

9. SLAKE (TRENCH) THE DEEP-TRENCH SCRAG (Heavy Vanguard / Frenzied Berserker) — CR 15

A massive, armor-plated aquatic scrag upgraded by Suture and Kael. It rests in a flooded hold on Deck IV and is deployed directly into the water during boarding actions to tear apart target hulls or rudders. Level Adjustment buyoff (+5 LA completely bought off) allows him to hold racial Giant 6 HD plus Barbarian 5 / Frenzied Berserker 4.

  • Attacks: The Sovereign's Harpoon-Ballista (+2 Endless Black-Steel Harpoon) +23/+18/+13 melee (2d6+15 piercing/slashing, 19-20/x2, reach 10 ft.) or Harpoon-Ballista +17 ranged (4d6+2 bludgeoning/piercing, x3, range increment 100 ft.) or Claw +21 melee (1d6+10) and Bite +16 melee (1d8+5)

The target must make a Fortitude save (DC 26, or DC 31 in rage/frenzy) or suffer a permanent -4 penalty to Natural Armor or Shields.

The target also takes a -4 penalty on saving throws against Kael's effects for the next 24 hours.
    • Frenzy 2/day (Ex): As a free action, Slake can enter a frenzy. This lasts for 8 rounds and grants him a +6 bonus to Strength and an additional attack at his highest base attack bonus when making a full attack (which stacks with Alchemical Surge / Haste). He takes 2 points of nonlethal damage per round and a -2 penalty to AC. Stacks with Rage.
    • Supreme Cleave (Ex): Slake can take a 5-foot step between each cleave attack.
    • Hull Breaker (Ex): Slake's melee attacks deal double damage to objects, vehicles, and structures. He ignores up to 10 points of hardness on hulls and rudders.
  • Special Qualities: Darkvision 90 ft., Low-Light Vision, Scent.
    • Deathless Frenzy (Ex): While frenzied, Slake is immune to death from hit point damage. He continues to fight normally even if reduced to 0 or fewer hit points (all the way to negative infinity), only dying when the frenzy ends if his hit points are still at -10 or lower and he is not healed.

His threat sphere is a hard-denial zone: any creature that takes a 5-ft. step, uses a defensive cast, or attempts a slide within reach provokes an attack of opportunity, completely overriding standard 3.5e exceptions.
    • Planar Slipstream Pounce (Su): Trench gains the Pounce special quality. When initiating a charge, he may choose to travel through a localized planar fold. This functions as Dimension Door with a range up to his current land speed (30 ft.) as part of the charge movement, allowing him to bypass physical obstacles, walls of force, or enemy screeners without provoking AoOs.
    • Aura of Non-Concordance (Su): Trench projects a continuous 30-foot aura. Any spell/power with [Shatter], [Disruption], or [Telekinesis] descriptors has its DC increased by +4. Conversely, any active abjuration spells, defensive wards, or magic circles against alignment suffer a caster level penalty equal to Trench's Con modifier (-8 caster level penalty, or -10 in rage/frenzy).
    • The Inquisitor's Absolute Mortality (Ex): Trench’s natural attacks permanently ignore any form of Damage Reduction (including DR/Epic, DR/Evil, or DR/-) and completely suppress the regeneration factors of any creature he damages for 24 hours. If he hits a target protected by Death Ward, Mind Blank, or Freedom of Movement, his touch acts as an automatic Greater Dispel Magic (Caster Level 15th), targeted explicitly at those defensive spell layers.
    • Trench-Adaptation (Ex): Immune to pressure damage from deep-ocean depths. +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks when submerged in murky or black waters.
    • Regeneration 5 (Acid/Fire) - Synergized: Because of the Amulet of Adaptation keeping him in a micro-layer of native deep-trench water, Slake regenerates 5 HP per round even on dry land, unless dealt Acid or Fire damage.
    • Alchemical Surge (Ex): 1/day, as a swift action, Slake can trigger Haste upon himself for 8 rounds (+1 dodge bonus to AC, +1 Reflex, extra attack on a full attack, +30 ft. speed).
    • Rage 2/day: (+4 Str, +4 Con, +2 Will saves, -2 AC. Stacks with Frenzy for a total of +10 Strength and +4 Constitution).
  • Saves: Fort +20, Ref +6, Will +9 (+11 in rage/frenzy)
  • Abilities: Str 28, Dex 14, Con 26, Int 12, Wis 12, Cha 7 (Base stats adjusted by Kael's Sovereign Psionic/Arcane Reformation and level upgrades; Rage/Frenzy: Str 38, Con 30)
  • Feats: Power Attack, Cleave, Great Cleave, Improved Bull Rush, Iron Will, Awesome Blow, Weapon Focus (Harpoon-Ballista), Improved Natural Attack (Claw), Diehard (bonus).
  • Gear:
