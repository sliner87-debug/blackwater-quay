Blackwater Quay: Faction & Renown Mechanics

In the subterranean, magic-choked environment of Blackwater Quay, survival often depends on who you know—and who you owe. Players operate in a fragile ecosystem balanced between four major powers.

A player’s (or the party's collective) Renown Score with a faction starts at 0 and caps at 20. Earning Renown involves completing faction-specific quests, donating wealth or magical artifacts, or sabotaging rival factions. Note that earning Renown with one faction will almost always cost you Renown with their ideological enemies.

1. The Thessalan Consortium

  • Alignment: Lawful Evil
  • Focus: Biomancy, Controlled Mutation, Profit, Weaponized Evolution
  • Key NPC Contact: Overseer Varak (Head Fleshwright of the Foundry)

The Consortium views the Sluices as an unrestricted testing ground. They value ruthless efficiency, magical innovation, and biological perfection. They despise weakness, religious dogma, and anyone who stands in the way of "progress."

  • Earning Renown: Harvesting rare aberration cores intact (+1), turning over captured Templars for live experimentation (+2), assassinating rival smugglers disrupting supply lines (+1), mapping un-charted planar rifts (+1).
  • Losing Renown: Destroying their vats (-2), healing the sick for free (-1), destroying proprietary research (-3).

Consortium Rank Progression

  • Rank 1: Specimen (Renown 3)
    • Perk (The Flesh-Markets): You gain access to the Consortium's black market. You can purchase mutagens, alchemical weapons, and rare poisons at a 15% discount. You are no longer targeted by their feral test-subjects in the wild.
  • Rank 2: Stitch-Hand (Renown 10)
    • Perk (Bodily Perfection): You earn the right to sponsored modification. You can undergo one biological graft (up to 5,000 gp value) at no cost. Additionally, you can rest in Consortium safehouses, guaranteeing a safe Long Rest.
  • Rank 3: Fleshwright (Renown 20)
    • Perk (Master of the Vats): You command the foundry's output. Once per Act, you can requisition a Scour-Harrow or Dire Displacer Rhino to follow your orders unconditionally for one week.

2. Covenant & Crown (The Templar Inquisition)

  • Alignment: Lawful Good (Extremist)
  • Focus: Purity, Eradication of Arcane/Psionic Magic, Surface Order
  • Key NPC Contact: Inquisitor-General Vance

The surface-dwelling Templars view the Quay as a festering wound of heresy. They seek to purge the Quay of its illicit magic, destroy the Elder Node, and execute anyone bearing an aberrant mark or mutation.

  • Earning Renown: Turning in unregistered spellcasters or psions (+1), destroying black-market shipments (+2), providing actionable intel on the Tri-Weave Shroud (+3), burning mutant nests (+1).
  • Losing Renown: Using arcane magic in front of them (-2), aiding smugglers (-3), bearing visible biological mutations (-2).

Covenant Rank Progression

  • Rank 1: Informant (Renown 3)
    • Perk (Inquisition Writ): You receive a sealed writ granting you immunity from low-level city watch harassment on the surface, and allowing you to carry weapons in restricted surface districts.
  • Rank 2: Hound (Renown 10)
    • Perk (The Holy Armory): You are granted access to the Templar armory. You can requisition Null-Steel weapons, which bypass magical resistances and deal an extra 1d6 damage to fey, aberrations, and undead.
  • Rank 3: Inquisitor (Renown 20)
    • Perk (The Holy Fire): You command the military arm of the church. Once per Act, you can summon a strike team of 6 heavily armored Templar Knights to raid a location of your choosing, or call down a Flame Strike (CL 15) via a localized orbital array.

3. Choir of the Below

  • Alignment: Chaotic Neutral / Chaotic Evil
  • Focus: The Elder Node, Prophecy, Psionic Ascension, Madness
  • Key NPC Contact: The Speaker in the Static (A Cerebrilith Avatar)

A terrifying cult of outcasts, mind-flayers, and mutants who worship the psychic static leaking from the Elder Node. They believe the whispers of the Void are the only true reality, and seek to tear down the Tri-Weave Shroud to let the "truth" flood the world.

  • Earning Renown: Protecting an Elder Node crystal shard (+2), sabotaging the Shroud's anchor points (+3), surviving a direct mental link with a Cerebrilith (+1), sacrificing a Templar to the Node (+2).
  • Losing Renown: Fortifying the Tri-Weave Shroud (-2), allying with the Templars (-3), curing madness in others (-1).

Choir Rank Progression

  • Rank 1: Whisperer (Renown 3)
    • Perk (Comforting Static): The whispers in the dark become comforting rather than terrifying. You gain Advantage (or a +4 bonus) on saving throws against charm, fear, and psychic damage.
  • Rank 2: Resonance (Renown 10)
    • Perk (Channel the Node): You can tap into the local hive-mind. Once per long rest, you can cast Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, or Clairvoyance without expending a spell slot or components.
  • Rank 3: The Voice (Renown 20)
    • Perk (Omniscient Static): The Elder Node speaks through you directly. Once per month, you can ask the Node a single question and receive an absolutely truthful, omniscient answer (acting as the Commune spell, but not limited to yes/no questions). Additionally, aberrations will not attack you unless provoked.

4. Sablehook Syndicate

  • Alignment: True Neutral
  • Focus: Smuggling, Survival, Maintaining the Shroud, Independence
  • Key NPC Contacts: Banki (The Weaver), Sable (The Pirate Queen), Suture (The Biomancer)

The premier smuggling syndicate of Blackwater Quay, anchored by Banki's magic. They operate by strict codes of silence and efficiency. They are the only faction actively trying to protect the Quay from both the Templars and the Elder Node.

  • Earning Renown: Securing Null-Steel shipments for Sable (+1), sabotaging Templar checkpoints (+2), working in Mikhailis' Shipyards to build new dreadnoughts (+1), bringing rare spell components to Banki (+2).
  • Losing Renown: Breaking a contract (-2), speaking of the Tri-Weave Shroud to outsiders (-3), stealing from a fellow syndicate member (-5).

Sablehook Rank Progression

  • Rank 1: Dock-Rat (Renown 3)
    • Perk (The Waterways): You have free passage on any Sablehook skiff, submarine, or ferry. You ignore Difficult Terrain penalties when moving through the knee-deep waters, mud, or sewage of the Quay.
  • Rank 2: Tri-Weave Resonance (Renown 10)
    • Perk (Shadow Stitched): Banki physically stitches a micro-sliver of the Sovereign Thread into your shadow. If you and another Rank 2 Sablehook ally are within 30 feet of each other, you can communicate telepathically in complete silence. Additionally, as a Reaction, you can magically absorb half the damage taken by a linked ally (functioning identically to the Warding Bond spell, no components required).
  • Rank 3: Sovereign Enforcer (Renown 20)
    • Perk (The Big Guns): You are granted access to Sablehook's highest tier of tactical support. Once per Act, you can call upon The Bleeding Needle or The Onyx Wake to provide devastating artillery support (3 rounds of cannon-fire that deals 8d6 bludgeoning/fire damage in a 20-ft radius) or immediate naval extraction from anywhere in the Quay.
