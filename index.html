The Districts of Blackwater Quay

Northern Sluices

Sulfurous sewer fumes, weeping brickwork, alchemical runoff

A sprawling network of stone conduits, iron lock gates, and sludge channels where the city's waste is filtered. Above the channels, the poorest citizens build hanging wooden structures. The Choir of the Below has established distribution vectors here for compliance serums.

Key Interest Locations

  • The Gull & Gasket Vaults A repurposed sluice station acting as a low-profile tavern. Heavily warded vaults below hide Sablehook's surface intelligence.
  • Sluice Gate S-1 A heavily rusted iron blockade guarded by compliant cult thugs.
  • The Distribution Vault S-3 Where Choir priests brew and pack the alchemical Blue-Drop droplets.
Syndicate Leaders & Planar Shadows

Antihero & Faction Roster

Banki (Human Disguise)

Sovereign Anchor

"Survival belongs to the thing that can become unrecognizable."

A middle-aged man of average build and grey-green eyes, appearing as an ordinary sellsword. This shape hides his true nature from prying eyes.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Tri-Weave Negation: All magic and psionics within 100 ft of the inner vaults are automatically negated.
  • Shroud Anchor: Maintains the Tri-Weave Shroud to keep Sablehook invisible to all detection.

Dr. Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor

Planar Compass

"A lock is just a door that hasn't met the right timeline. Stand back, let's see how this stone tasted."

Kael is a fey monstrosity of the Clan Vael-Kaelor. Beneath his Shadar-kai disguise is a towering minotaur-framed horror with displacer tentacles, claws, and phase-wings. He leads a corsair fleet from the flagship Onyx Wake.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Biomantic Disassembly: Dissolves target's cells with a thought. Attackers face immediate destruction.
  • Fractured Compass: Guides allies through non-Euclidean layouts, granting immunity to Xoriat madness.

Sable

Tactical Coordinator

"If you want to live in the Quay, count the seconds between the guard's sweeps."

A cold, pragmatic female Shadar-kai rogue who wears a heavy, lead-lined coat. She manages day-to-day operations and coordinates field agents with clockwork efficiency.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Sneak Attack +6d6: Devastating striker from the shadows using sleep-poisoned arrows.
  • Lead Shielding: Distributes warded lead cases shielding magic items from detection scans.

Kessler

Tarkanan Commander

"My mark doesn't ask for permission. It just burns."

A battle-scarred fighter with an acid-weeping aberrant dragonmark climbing her neck. She leads House Tarkanan's tactical strike units and coordinates surface diversions.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Aberrant Mark: Channels acid blasts and death attacks. Synergizes with dragonmark magic items.
  • Shadow Assassins: Coordinates sniper support bolts in the sluices and surface streets.

Zaniph

Githyanki Deserter

"Vlaakith's knights think the silver sword makes them gods. A broken blade cuts just as deep."

A scarred Githyanki who deserted the lich-queen's fleet. Pursued by astral stalkers, Zaniph fights with the jagged remnant of a shattered silver sword.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Psionic Disruption: Attacks disable target's casting and manifestations for 1 round.
  • Silver Cleave: Heavy power attacker focusing on shutting down planar summons.

Terik / Hruujj

Rakshasa Broker

"Coins bear many faces. Fortunately, so do I. Shall we write a new transaction?"

A shape-shifting rakshasa representing the Lords of Dust. He coordinates black market trade and occult transactions under the guise of a tiefling merchant.

Faction Influence & Powers

  • Detect Thoughts & Disguise: Master of social evasion and magical alignment shielding.
  • Sorcerer Spellcasting: Manifests lightning and displacement fields with sorcerous efficiency.

Adventure Arc progression

The Campaign Narrative

Act I

Whispers in the Sluices

Goal: Discover how the Elder Node is manipulating the city's surface.

The characters are hired by Sable to investigate a series of kidnappings in the Northern Sluices. They discover that a local cult, the Choir of the Below, is distributing "Elder Node derivative compounds" to chemically comply the populace.

The air in the Northern Sluices is thick with the stench of vinegar, rotting refuse, and sulfurous sewage gas. Sludge overflows the stone gutters, weeping down from the brick vaults above in greasy streaks. Below the hanging wooden shacks of the city's outcasts, you can hear the telepathic resonance—a low, rhythmic thrumming that vibrates in the back of your skull.

Act Encounters

  • I-1: Sluice Blockade CR 10
  • I-2: The Acidic Locks CR 11
  • I-3: Cult Altar (Choir Priest) CR 12
Tactical Environment: Flooded Walkways Walkways require a DC 14 Balance check to run. Slipping plunges characters into 5-ft deep sewage canals, dealing 1d6 acid damage and exposing them to sewer rot.
Act II

The Fleshwarped Foundry

Goal: Destroy the Thessalan Stronghold & secure biological suppressants.

The Thessalan Consortium has established a secret foundry in the Belowmarket Deep, breeding mutated prototypes. The characters must coordinate a surface riot with House Tarkanan while Kael's boarding crew holds the canal vectors.

The Belowmarket Deep opens into a gargantuan cavern illuminated by volcanic fissures. Amidst duergar forges, a dark basalt fortress looms over the green-sludge canal vectors. Heavy copper pipes run along the exterior walls, pulsing with glowing green chemical slurries.

Act Encounters

  • II-1: Canal Gates (Mutated Sea-Wolf) CR 11
  • II-2: The Vat Room (Vespera Thran) CR 12
  • II-3: The Archives (Flesh Golem Prototype) CR 12
Tactical Environment: Growth Vats Shattering large glass cylinders (AC 10, HP 15) releases green mutagenic acid in a 10-ft radius (3d6 acid damage) and spawns a mutated crawling claw natural hazard.
Act III

Crimson Sails Over the Quay

Goal: Defend Sablehook from Githyanki air raid & steal shield tech.

Githyanki ships breach the subterranean vaults. You must defend Sablehook alongside Kael's crew. In the chaos, scan the Githyanki displacement shield runes to reverse-engineer them, culminating in a boarding action on Vlaakith's Pride.

The ceiling of the subterranean harbor cracks as three massive Githyanki spelljamming galleons break through the upper rock strata. Their silver hulls catch the green glow of the quay's alchemical lights, and from their ballista ports, psychic fire rains down.

Act Encounters

  • III-1: Harbor Walls Defence CR 12
  • III-2: Flagship Boarding (Githyanki Knights) CR 13
  • III-3: The Shroud Core (Astral Stalkers) CR 13
Victory Tracker Outcomes Accumulate Victory Points (VP) by repelling boarders and starting the Shroud. 11+ VP secures a full victory; 6 or less VP results in an overrun and permanent fatigue for Act IV.
Act IV

The Deepmind Annex

Goal: Descend into the brine-veins and seal the Xoriat Tear.

Deep beneath the Quay lies the Annex, a non-Euclidean gateway controlled by the Elder Node. Guided by Captain Mikhailis's timeline senses, navigate the gravity flues, combat the dragon Oraxis, and utilize chronal loopholes to bypass the rakshasa double-cross.

Reality unravels below. Gravity does not pull—it twists. Sluggish saltwater rises toward the high vaults, hanging in mid-air in massive floating bubbles. Glowing green nerves stretch across the stone pillars like cables, pulsing to the telepathic rhythm of the overmind.

Act Encounters

  • IV-1: Gravity Flues Navigation CR 13
  • IV-2: The Cathedral of Whispers CR 13
  • IV-3: Oraxis & Sealing the Tear CR 14
Tactical Environment: Chronal & Gravity Distortions Failing gravity shifts coordinates randomly. Only Kael's presence within 60 ft protects players from Xoriat's compliance madness.
