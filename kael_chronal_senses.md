# Character Profile Addition: Mikhailis's Fractured Chronal Senses
## Narrative Integration for the Novel & Epic TTRPG Rules

This document defines how **Captain Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor (Kael)** utilizes his **Fractured Chronal Senses** in both the novel's narrative and in high-level/epic TTRPG campaign systems. Unlike the scaled-down feat for player characters, this represents Kael's full power as a biomantic and chronal weapon of mass destruction.

---

# I. NARRATIVE MANIFESTATION (For the Novel)

Exposure to the raw, reality-dissolving radiation of the Xoriat tear during the destruction of the Thessalan Consortium's annex did not just drive Mikhailis mad—it fractured his linear perception of time. 

### 1. Chronal Echoes & Temporal Sight
Mikhailis does not see the world in a single static frame. To him, every object, person, and space leaves a trailing ribbon of "chronal echoes"—faint, smoke-like outlines of where they were seconds ago, and blurry projections of where they might be seconds from now. 
*   **Novel Application:** In combat, Kael doesn't dodge a sword sweep; he steps out of the path of the *future echo* of the blade before the metal even swings. When investigating a room, he can see the faint chronal dust of who stood there hours ago, reading their movements like footprints in the mud.
*   **The Sensory Toll:** This sight is a constant, overwhelming flood. To keep from drowning in infinite potential timelines, Kael wraps himself in his manic, pirate-captain persona. The loud slang, violent outbursts, and theatrical swagger serve as a cognitive anchor to force his mind back into the *present* timeline.

### 2. Sideways Stepping (Non-Euclidean Navigation)
Because Kael perceives space and time as a tailorable tapestry (a trait of the Vael-Kaelor clan), he can physically step "sideways" through reality.
*   **Novel Application:** If a solid stone door blocks his path, Kael does not break it. He focuses his chronal sight to find a timeline where the door was open, or where the stone had crumbled into dust, and steps *through* that seam. To observers, he simply phases through solid matter, accompanied by a brief flash of violet drift-fire and the smell of ozone.

# II. EPIC TTRPG MECHANICS (For Kael's NPC Profile)

In high-level systems, Kael's chronal senses are represented by legendary, reality-warping abilities that bypass standard combat math.

### 1. Passive Abilities

#### Chronal Foresight (Ex)
Kael cannot be surprised, flanked, flat-footed, or caught in a sneak attack. He continuously perceives potential futures 3 seconds before they occur.
*   **System Rule:** Kael adds his Intelligence modifier (+7) as an insight bonus to his Armor Class and Initiative rolls.

#### Distortion Navigation (Ex)
Kael is immune to spatial disorientation, falling damage, and planar alignment penalties.
*   **System Rule:** He can walk on walls or ceilings as if under a permanent *Spider Climb* spell, and he ignores the effects of gravity shifts, moving at full speed in no-gravity zones.

---

### 2. Active Abilities

#### Timeline Stepping (Su) — Swift Action
Kael slips sideways through time, bypassing physical barriers.
*   **System Rule:** Once per round, Kael can teleport up to 60 feet. This teleportation does not provoke attacks of opportunity. He can pass through solid objects, walls, or barriers as long as there is an open space at his destination.

#### Chronal Siphon (Su) — On Hit with Eldritch Claws
When Kael claws a foe, he can choose to drain the speed of their personal timeline.
*   **System Rule:** The target must succeed on a Will DC 28 saving throw or be affected by a permanent *Slow* spell (caster level 15th). For each creature slowed this way, Kael gains the benefits of a *Haste* spell for 5 rounds. This ability can be used 3 times per day.

#### The Temporal Loop (Su) — Immediate Action (Epic Action)
The ultimate manifestation of Kael's chronal force. If Kael or his primary crew (Suture, Banki, Sable) faces imminent death or total tactical failure, he can violently collapse the local timeline.
*   **System Rule:** Once per day, Kael can snap the local space (100-foot radius) back in time by exactly **3 rounds (18 seconds)**. 
    *   **The Reset:** All damage taken, spells expended, deaths occurred, and tactical movements made during those 3 rounds are completely undone. The state of all entities in the zone resets to exactly how they were at the start of the 3-round window.
    *   **The Memory:** Only Kael retains full, vivid memory of the erased timeline. He instantly gains a +8 insight bonus to his next initiative roll, and all allies he telepathically alerts gain a +4 insight bonus to AC and saving throws for the first round of the restarted timeline.
    *   **The Cost:** This ability places a staggering physical toll on Kael's hybridized brain. Immediately after activating the loop, his nose and half-minotaur jaw bleed, and he suffers a temporary **-4 penalty to all ability scores** and cannot cast spells or manifest psionics for 24 hours.
