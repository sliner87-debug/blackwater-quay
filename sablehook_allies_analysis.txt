========================================================================
SABLEHOOK ALLIES & ADVERSARIES: SHADY AND EVIL COALITIONS AGAINST THE RIFT
========================================================================

This document explores how other planar and criminal organizations—specifically evil or morally bankrupt ones—would react to the Elder Brain (Elder Node) attempting to open a rift to Xoriat. It details how they would work with Sablehook, Banki, and Kael (utilizing the unique tactical advantage of the Vael-Kaelor collective consciousness).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE VAEL-KAELOR ADVANTAGE: AN ISLAND OF SANITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Correction Note:
  The Vael-Kaelor's illithid hybridization does NOT connect them to the Illithid hive mind or the Elder Brain. Instead, the Vael-Kaelor share their own closed-loop collective consciousness.
* Tactical Implications:
  1. Immunity to Domination: The Elder Brain cannot psionically command, hijack, or dominate the Vael-Kaelor through their hybridization.
  2. Telepathic Stealth: The Elder Brain cannot track or detect them using its standard hive-mind locator networks.
  3. High-Trust Unit: Banki, Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor, and the other survivors function as a single, highly coordinated, telepathic strike unit. 

Because of this unique mental independence, other powerful factions will view the Vael-Kaelor as the perfect scalpel to cut out the Elder Brain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVIL ORGANIZATIONS ALLYING WITH SABLEHOOK
------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE LORDS OF DUST (Fiends of the Overlords)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Motivation:
  The Lords of Dust serve the Overlords (ancient fiends bound in Khyber). Their goal is to release their masters to rule Khorvaire. The Daelkyr and Xoriat are alien invaders whose madness would dissolve reality into formless chaos, rendering the Overlords' prisons—and their future rule—entirely meaningless. 
* Why They Help Sablehook:
  They need Eberron intact and structured to free the Overlords. They cannot rule a world of raving, mutated aberrations.
* Tactical Interaction:
  - The Deal: A rakshasa agent (disguised as a wealthy patron or city magistrate) contacts Sablehook.
  - Support: They provide secret fiendish lore on planar seals, supply raw byeshk ore, and use their political influence to redirect city watch patrols away from Sablehook's operations.
  - The Catch: They are highly manipulative. They will try to steer the Vael-Kaelor into sealing the rift in a way that accidentally weakens a nearby Overlord seal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE DREAMING DARK (The Quori / Inspired)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Motivation:
  The Quori feed on structured mortal dreams (fear, devotion, complacency) to maintain their dominion over Dal Quor. Xoriat's madness dissolves mortal minds, making them completely useless as a psychic food source or for Quori possession.
* Why They Help Sablehook:
  A Xoriat rift would permanently ruin their "mortal herd" and threaten the stability of Dal Quor.
* Tactical Interaction:
  - The Deal: They operate entirely in the shadows. They see the Vael-Kaelor's independent collective consciousness as a rare asset immune to both the Elder Brain and standard mortal corruption.
  - Support: They will quietly supply psionic shielding technology, psychic counter-measures, and raw intelligence on the Elder Brain's telepathic nodes. They may "Mind Seed" key templars to act as defensive muscle for Sablehook.
  - The Catch: They will constantly monitor the Vael-Kaelor, hoping to dissect the secret of their closed-loop consciousness once the battle is won.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE THESSALAN CONSORTIUM (The Former Captors)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Motivation:
  An evil biomantic syndicate obsessed with the capture, refinement, and splicing of exotic biological assets. They view Banki and the Vael-Kaelor as their stolen property.
* Why They Help Sablehook:
  A Xoriat invasion would destroy their laboratories, resources, and potential test subjects. Furthermore, they view the Elder Brain's biological structure as a prize to be harvested, not a master to bow to.
* Tactical Interaction:
  - The Deal: A Consortium Director proposes a temporary, high-tension truce.
  - Support: They provide highly advanced biomantic suppressants—specialized chemical compounds that disrupt illithid secretion chains, weaken the Elder Brain's thralls, and temporarily suppress the regeneration of Xoriat aberrations.
  - The Catch: They will demand the return of Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor's stolen research journals and a tissue sample of Banki's fey-illithid skin in exchange for their alchemical aids.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOUSE TARKANAN (The Aberrant Dragonmark Syndicate)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Motivation:
  A criminal syndicate of outcasts bearing volatile, aberrant dragonmarks. They are persecuted by the Dragonmarked Houses and the law.
* Why They Help Sablehook:
  They share a natural solidarity with Sablehook as fellow criminal outcasts. Furthermore, aberrant dragonmarks react violently to Xoriat's planar proximity, causing excruciating pain, mutation, or death to their members.
* Tactical Interaction:
  - The Deal: An open alliance between two underworld syndicates.
  - Support: They provide elite assassins, saboteurs, and secure smuggling routes through the Underbelly. They excel at urban guerrilla warfare and physical infiltration.
  - The Catch: They are street-level criminals. They cannot help with planar magic or psionics, but they will demand sanctuary in Sablehook's warded vaults for their members if the rift begins to bleed.
