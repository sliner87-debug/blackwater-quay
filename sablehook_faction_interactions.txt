========================================================================
SABLEHOOK FACTION INTERACTIONS: THE REACTION TO THE VAEL-KAELOR MERGING
========================================================================

This document analyzes how the four Eberron-aligned planar factions—The Kech Volaar, The Gatekeepers, The Kalashtar, and The Chamber—would interact with Banki's compound, "Sablehook," upon discovering the forced fey-illithid biological merging (the "PixIllithid") engineered by Dr. Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHO WILL ATTACK?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE KECH VOLAAR (Dhakaani Goblins): MOST LIKELY TO LAUNCH AN IMMEDIATE MILITARY ASSAULT.
   They view illithids and fleshwarping as the ultimate ancestral enemy that destroyed their empire. They will deploy militarized strike forces to put Sablehook to the sword and cleanse the corruption with byeshk steel and fire.

2. THE GATEKEEPERS (Orc Druids): HIGHLY LIKELY TO LAUNCH A SURGICAL, COVERT ATTACK.
   They view the PixIllithid as an unnatural affront to the natural order. They will not launch a siege but will send elite "sealer" strike teams to assassinate Vael-Kaelor and quarantine the compound.

3. THE KALASHTAR (Path of Light): UNLIKELY TO ATTACK DIRECTLY; WILL SEEK INTERVENTION.
   They view the merging as a tragic violation of mental and spiritual sovereignty. They will attempt to psionically intervene to guide Banki or shield the innocent, but will quietly assassinate Vael-Kaelor if he attempts to expand the telepathic network.

4. THE DRAGONS OF THE CHAMBER: NEUTRAL / TACTICAL STRIKE (PROPHECY-DEPENDENT).
   They are morally indifferent to the horror of the merging. However, if the Prophecy dictates that Banki's network will break the seals of the Daelkyr, they will manipulate other factions to destroy Sablehook or vaporize it from above.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DETAILED FACTION ROSTERS & INTERACTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE KECH VOLAAR (Dhakaani Goblins)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Doctrine on Fleshwarping & Aberrations:
  The Dhakaani civilization was shattered by the Daelkyr War. Aberrations (especially Mind Flayers) are their historical tormentors. They view fleshwarping with absolute disgust and physical revulsion.
* Reaction to the Vael-Kaelor Merging:
  The discovery of a PixIllithid (Banki) and the presence of Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor (the biomantic architect) signals to them that the Daelkyr's legacy is active on the surface. They will view Sablehook not as a syndicate, but as an active breeding ground for Xoriat horrors.
* Interaction with Sablehook:
  - The Assault: They will deploy a stealthy but heavily armed Dhakaani unit ("Kech") into Blackwater Quay. They do not negotiate.
  - Tactical Tools: They will utilize "byeshk" weapons to bypass the fleshwarpers' biological defenses. Their "duur'kala" (dirgesingers) will sing martial chants that bolster mental resolve, making their warriors immune to Banki's telepathic dread or Vael-Kaelor's psychic attacks.
  - Objective: Total eradication of Vael-Kaelor, destruction of the "illithid cradles," and the execution of Banki to ensure the aberrant strain is wiped out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE GATEKEEPERS (Orcish Druidic Sect)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Doctrine on Fleshwarping & Aberrations:
  The Gatekeepers are sworn to protect the natural order from the "unnatural" works of Xoriat. They view fleshwarping as a cancer on the world.
* Reaction to the Vael-Kaelor Merging:
  They will perceive Banki's PixIllithid form as a grotesque, living violation of the natural cycle. They will feel the localized disruption in the planar boundaries caused by Kaelen the Stitcher's abjuration experiments.
* Interaction with Sablehook:
  - The Assault: Unlike the militaristic goblins, the Gatekeepers act like a surgical strike force ("Men in Black"). They will slip through the sewers and docks unnoticed.
  - Tactical Tools: They will use natural rot-wards, plant-based entanglements, and dimensional anchor magic to shut down the abjuration circles of Kaelen the Stitcher.
  - Objective: They will target Vael-Kaelor first, viewing him as the corrupting center. If Banki demonstrates autonomy and opposes the Elder Node, some younger Gatekeepers might hesitate, but the elders will insist that a PixIllithid is too unstable to live and must be sealed away or put down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE KALASHTAR (The Path of Light)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Doctrine on Fleshwarping & Aberrations:
  Focused primarily on their war with the Dreaming Dark (Dal Quor). However, their philosophy of il-Yannah (the Path of Light) requires them to banish spiritual and mental darkness.
* Reaction to the Vael-Kaelor Merging:
  They will sense the psychic resonance of the "Sovereign Thread" and the agonizing history of Banki's surgeries. They will view Banki as a victim of ultimate mental violation (the forced merging of minds/biology).
* Interaction with Sablehook:
  - The Approach: They will not attack. Instead, a "shadow watcher" (Sheshantol) or psionic emissary will attempt to infiltrate the mind-space of Sablehook.
  - Tactical Tools: Psionic mind-links, emotional calming, and mental shields.
  - Objective: They will attempt to contact Banki telepathically to see if his fey spirit can be liberated from the illithid secretion chain. If they realize Vael-Kaelor is attempting to exploit Banki's network to dominate others, they will quietly assassinate Vael-Kaelor or provide intelligence to the Gatekeepers to help them cleanse the compound.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE DRAGONS OF THE CHAMBER (Argonnessen)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Doctrine on Fleshwarping & Aberrations:
  Strategic indifference. Mortals and their experiments are beneath them. They care only for the Draconic Prophecy.
* Reaction to the Vael-Kaelor Merging:
  A dragon views a PixIllithid as an interesting anomaly. They do not care about the moral horror or pain of the merging.
* Interaction with Sablehook:
  - The Approach: Complete neutrality unless the Prophecy shifts.
  - The Catalyst: If their reading of the Prophecy indicates that the "Vael-Kaelor line" or Banki's psionic network is a key trigger that will weaken the Khyber seals, their reaction will be swift and absolute.
  - Tactical Tools: They will not send soldiers. They will manipulate the Kech Volaar or the local authorities (like Aldric Thorne's templars) to assault the compound, or simply send a single dragon disguised as a mortal to trigger a planar explosion that wipes Sablehook off the map.
