====================================================================
           THE BLEEDING NEEDLE - VEHICLE & CREW REFERENCE
====================================================================
This document contains the complete visual descriptions, TTRPG statistics,
crew rosters, and detailed NPC dossiers for Suture's signature dreadnought,
"The Bleeding Needle," and its 18 House Acolytes. It is structured to serve
as a direct reference file for roleplay prompts and campaign integration.

---

## SECTION 1: SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS & SYSTEMS
*   Vessel Class: Colossal Mobile Abattoir Dreadnought (Refitted Vanguard Dreadnought)
*   Dimensions: 220 ft. long, 60 ft. wide, drawing 24 ft. of water.
*   Hull Sheathing: 4-inch duergar-forged blackwater ironwood plating (DR 15/adamantine; renders the ship invisible to all divination, scrying, and telepathic detection while running cold).
*   Suture's Gaping Bow: A massive set of ironwood jaws (spliced from a colossal deep-sea kraken) fitted to the bow. When ramming, it automatically chews through target hulls, dealing 12d6 ram damage and pinning the target vessel flush.
*   Propulsion:
    1. Fleshgrafted Oar-Limbs: Sixteen massive, webbed claws spliced from kraken-spawn and deep-sea scrags. They emerge from reinforced ports in the lower hull, rowed by a shared nervous system.
    2. Bound Trench-Spirits: Dozens of drowned phantoms bound into the timber of the sails and rudder, catching invisible planar winds and steering the ship against currents without throwing a wake.

---

## SECTION 2: LORE & DESIGN DOCTRINE (SUTURE'S DIRECTIVES)
*   Origin & Trauma: The Bleeding Needle is a physical manifestation of Captain Mikhailis (Kael) and Banki’s burning, absolute hatred of the Thessalan Consortium—under whose scalpels the brothers spent decades imprisoned, tortured, and subjected to horrific biomantic experiments. 
*   Scorning the sanitized, academic pretense of surface medicine, the dreadnought is a floating Magic Pirate Abattoir designed to tear Consortium remnants apart, weaponize their stolen research, and turn their tools of horror back upon them.
*   The Hostages as "Living Reparations": Because the noble houses of Blackwater Quay turned a blind eye to—or directly profited from—the Thessalan Consortium’s kidnapping ring, the brothers view their children as living reparations (the "Flesh Debt" clause of the Shadow-Vassal Decree). Drafted as hostages to ensure their families' compliance, they are apprenticed to Suture to learn the raw, anti-ethical biomancy born of their families' complicity.
*   The Laboratory Environment: Rejects all sterilization. The basalt flags of the abattoir are coated in a thin layer of nutrient slime and scrap-skin to keep harvested cells alive during surgeries.
*   The Abattoir-Loom: A ceiling-mounted, writhing web of spliced chitinous claws, fey-metal needles, and razor-sharp bones that descend from the rafters, frantically sewing, cutting, and siphoning under Suture's direct phrenic control.

## SECTION 3: DECK-BY-DECK KEY & LAYOUT
*   Deck I: The Upper Deck (The Boneshard Catapults & Gaping Bow)
    *   The Boneshard Catapults: Four heavy telekinetic catapults (psionic-mage-tech) that launch barbed harpoons made of calcified leviathan bone wound with silver cables. Bone spindles turned by bound skeletal thralls wind the cables, dragging the prey into the Gaping Bow.
    *   The Runic Conduit Rails: Glowing brass rails that pulse with planar ichor, allowing Suture to vent a cone of Necrotic Blood-Mist (4d6 fire/acid damage + sickness, Fort DC 16).
*   Deck II: The Mid Deck (The Holding Pens & Specimen Cabin)
    *   The Specimen Cabin: Warded shelves crowded with clay and bone jars containing aberration eyes, pulsing organs, and stolen Dhakaani scroll tubes.
    *   The Iron Pens: 24 heavy iron cages fitted with psionic-suppression runes to hold high-tier captives.
    *   The Stasis Caskets: 16 horizontal stone containers filled with alchemical preservation gel.
*   Deck III: The Lower Deck (Suture’s Slaughter-Floor / The Abattoir)
    *   The Basalt Slaughter-Tables: Three massive stone slabs fitted with "Suture-Clamps"—living, skeletal claws grafted to the table that physically hold subjects down.
    *   The Abattoir-Loom: Ceiling-mounted surgical rig of chitin claws and bone needles.
*   Deck IV: The Biomass Core (Growth & Incubation Bays)
    *   The Siphon Vats: Three biological stomach-vats harvested from sea-monsters. Inside, hydrochloric enzymes and troll blood digest waste to biomass while keeping the nerves of the tissue firing.
    *   The Birthing Sacks: 16 pulsing membranes harvested from giant squids, gestating Vanguard constructs in amniotic fluid.
*   The Keel: The Leviathan Spine & Heart-Chamber
    *   The Leviathan Spine: Fossilized vertebrae of a deep-sea leviathan reanimated with necrotic veins, allowing the ship to feel currents and telepathically report structural status.
    *   The Heart-Cluster: Three separate Leviathan Hearts fused into a pulsing cluster, pumping necrotic blood through veins lining the ship's frames to feed the oar-limbs.

## SECTION 4: TTRPG STATISTICS (D&D 3.5 / PATHFINDER 1E)
*   Size: Colossal Vehicle (Space 220 ft. by 60 ft.; Height 40 ft. plus mast).
*   Seaworthiness: +12; Ship Handling: +6
*   Speed: Swim 40 ft. (flesh-rowed/spirit-driven)
*   Overall AC: 22 (-8 size, +20 natural/ironwood sheathing)
*   Hardness: 15; HP: 900 (break threshold 300); Rigging HP: 160
*   Ram: 12d6 bludgeoning/piercing damage (ignores up to 10 points of target hardness due to the Gaping Bow).
*   Planar Glide: Once per day, the ship can slip "sideways" through space, acting as a *Dimension Door* spell (caster level 15th) for the entire vessel, teleporting up to 1,000 feet as a full-round action.
*   Shroud of the Grave (Passive Defense): Bound spirits project a distortion cloud, granting the ship permanent Displacement (50% miss chance against all physical and spell attacks).

## SECTION 5: CREW ROSTER & COMMAND TRIAD (88 TOTAL ENTITIES)
*   The Command Triad:
    1. Suture (Commander — Void-Touched Fleshwarper): Kael's crazed apprentice. Coordinates all laboratory work, mutagens, and integrations. (Level 12 Alchemist/Fleshwarper; Heal modifier +23).
    2. Stitch (Lab Warden — Flesh Golem Prototype): A 9-foot construct stitched from aquatic scrags and duergar mercenaries. Guards Deck IV. (CR 8; 110 HP; immune to magic; slam +15).
    3. Vespera Thran (Chief Apothecary): Suture's primary assistant, managing mutagen synthesis and specimen stabilization. (Level 9 Cleric/Transmuter; CR 9).
*   The Lab Staff:
    *   18x House Acolytes (Suture's Surgical Assistants): See detailed dossiers below.
    *   40x Bound Trench-Spirits: Perform all sailing and rigging automatically.
*   The Deck Force & Sentinels:
    *   20x Sea-Wolf Hybrids (CR 5): Spliced aquatic sentinels patrolling upper decks. (Swim speed 40 ft., aqueous camouflage, tripping bites).
*   The Vanguard Security (Stasis Holds):
    *   4x Blink-Hound Shock-Troopers (CR 6): Phase-shifting combat beasts.
    *   4x Trench-Fiend Guardians (CR 7): Aquatic scrag defenders armed with harpoons.

## SECTION 7: EXPEDITION LAUNCHPAD OPERATIONS (PC SERVICES)
When the player characters utilize the *Bleeding Needle* as their expedition launchpad and mobile sanctuary, they gain access to the following:

*   The Runic Shroud (Safe Haven Rest): The dreadnought's blackwater ironwood sheathing and lead-silver warded chambers completely shield the interior from the Elder Node's telepathic reach. While resting aboard, PCs are immune to the daily sanity checks, mental fatigue, or psychic sweeps of the Campaign's daily intrusion tables.
*   Stasis Recovery: Placing a PC in a stasis casket halts the progression of all poisons, diseases, Xoriat madness, and cellular decay, freezing their status.
*   Biomantic Grafting: Suture and his 18 House Acolytes perform any biomantic grafts listed in the player options. All Heal checks receive a +5 masterwork bonus, and recovery times are reduced by 50%.
*   Alchemical Essence Refining: PCs can trade captured enemies or monster carcasses to the acolytes to refine:
    *   *Arcane Ink:* Refines spellbooks/scrolls at 75% of the standard gold cost.
    *   *Psionic Node Crystals:* Dissolves to restore 1d4+1 power points or recharge psionic items.
    *   *Divine Gold Serum:* Consumed as a standard action to restore a single expended divine spell slot of up to 4th level.
*   Telepathic Bridge: Linking to the reanimated Leviathan Spine grants a +4 bonus on Knowledge (dungeoneering) and Psicraft checks to navigate trenches, detect thermal vents, or avoid gravity anomalies.
*   Acolyte Crew Support: The 18 House Acolytes handle all routine ship management and weapon operations (including the harpoons) during encounters, allowing the PCs to focus entirely on combat, spellcasting, or boarding strategies.
