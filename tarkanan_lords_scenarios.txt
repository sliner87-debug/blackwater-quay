NARRATIVE TREATMENT: DETAILED COALITION SCENARIOS
========================================================================

This document provides extensive narrative and tactical depth to the four campaign scenarios, mapping out setting, planning, step-by-step execution, and the underlying tension between Sablehook, House Tarkanan, and the Lords of Dust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 1: THE SIEGE OF THE ELDER NODE'S HIVE (The Grand Design)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Logline: Sablehook and House Tarkanan launch a coordinated subterranean assault to sever the Elder Node's surface supply chain and collapse its psionic nursery before it can mentally enslave the city.

### 1. Setting & Atmosphere
The setting is the ** Northern Sluice District**, specifically the flooded, brick-lined sewers directly beneath the Gull & Gasket tavern. The air is thick with the smell of river silt, rotting fish, and a sharp, ozone-metallic scent—the psionic discharge of the Elder Node's secretion chain. A dim, bioluminescent pink glow bleeds from the cracks in the masonry where the Node's roots have calcified the walls.

### 2. The Alliance & Planning
The meeting takes place in the Gull & Gasket's subterranean vault. 
* **The Players:** Banki (standing stiffly in his human disguise, his grey-green eyes reflecting the dim lanterns), Kael (monitoring the Vael-Kaelor collective loop), **Vellin Shara**, a rakshasa agent of the Lords of Dust disguised as a wealthy tiefling merchant, and **Kessler**, a local lieutenant of House Tarkanan who bears a weeping, acid-dripping mark on her neck.
* **The Tension:** Vellin offers weapons of byeshk and promises that the city watch will look the other way, but his smooth, oil-like telepathy is a constant irritation to Banki's closed-loop mind. Kessler is desperate; several of her street-orphans have been taken by the Node's "Choir." She demands blood, not politics.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution
* **Phase 1: Infiltration & Cut:** Kessler leads a strike team of Tarkanan enforcers through the upper sewers. Using their aberrant marks, they melt iron grates with acid and create diversionary gas explosions to draw away the Node's thralls (city guards under chemical compliance).
* **Phase 2: The Fiendish Diversion:** Vellin Shara activates a pocket dragonshard, summoning two minor bone devils in the eastern canals. The sudden, violent disruption forces the Elder Node to redirect its primary defender thralls to protect its main surface portal.
* **Phase 3: The Breach & Negation:** Banki and Kael lead the apex survivors (including Vespera and Kaelen the Stitcher) into the Node's core—the "Pink Lab." The Elder Node tries to speak to them using Tovin's voice, projecting waves of telepathic grief. 
* **The Climax:** Banki collapses his human form, transforming into the **PixIllithid WarForm**—a terrifying three-inch shape with writhing purple facial tentacles and stained glass wings, channeling psionic purple fire. He drives the **Null-Fang** directly into the Node's central secretion cradle. The Tri-Weave channels flare, negating the Node's telepathic beacon.

### 4. The Aftermath & Double-Cross
The nursery is collapsed, and the children are rescued. However, Vellin Shara secretly pockets a vial of the Elder Node's concentrated secretion fluid, intending to use it to study how the Vael-Kaelor collective resists telepathic domination, setting up a future betrayal.

SCENARIO 2: THE INCURSION OF THE CRIMSON SAILS (Githyanki Air Raid)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Logline: When a Githyanki raiding party raids Blackwater Quay to hunt down a Githzerai rift-sealer and a Githyanki deserter, Sablehook must coordinate a street-level defense with House Tarkanan to ground the astral fleet.

### 1. Setting & Atmosphere
The foggy docks of Blackwater Quay during a sudden, unnatural storm. The sky is a bruised violet, torn open by the planar entry of three Githyanki spelljamming galleons with crimson sails. The ships float silently above the masts of the merchant vessels, their ballistas firing psychic bolts that leave the sailors screaming without physical wounds.

### 2. The Alliance & Planning
The Githyanki's target is specific: **Kaelen Grey** (the Githzerai rift-mage) and **Zaniph** (the deserter guard hiding in Sablehook). 
* **The Players:** Banki, Kaelen Grey (his skin etched with silver runes that hum with violet drift-fire), and **Terik**, a rakshasa coordinator who represents a faction of the Lords of Dust interested in keeping the Githyanki away from the city's dragonshard vaults.
* **The Tension:** Kaelen wants to flee back to Limbo, but Kael points out that the Githyanki will burn the entire district to find him. Terik agrees to provide defensive illusions, but only if Sablehook agrees to let Zaniph be used as bait to draw the flagship into an ambush.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution
* **Phase 1: Grounding the Skiffs:** As Githyanki knights descend on silver-corded harness lines, House Tarkanan enforcers open fire from the warehouse roofs. They use aberrant marks of telekinesis and elemental fire to tear the rigging of the smaller skiffs, forcing them to crash into the harbor.
* **Phase 2: The Stressed Shield & The Scan:** Terik's fiends cast a massive shroud of displacement magic over the Sablehook compound. As the Githyanki ships bombard the barrier with psychic bolts, the kinetic and mental stress causes the shield to flicker. 
  - *The Defense:* **Sable** coordinates the physical defense on the walls, using her shadows and Tarkanan enforcers to hold off Githyanki skirmishers attempting to slip through the flickering blind spots.
  - *The Scan:* In the deep vaults, **Kaelen Grey ("Suture" / "The Stitcher")** uses his rift-sealing senses to analyze the cracks exposed by the bombardment. He traces the flow of Terik's fiendish equations. 
  - *The Tri-Weave Blueprint:* **Banki** uses the Vael-Kaelor collective consciousness to link his mind to Kaelen and Kael, funneling the scanned structural data into the Null-Fang. Together, they begin reverse-engineering the shield, adapting it to run on psionic-fey frequencies.
* **Phase 3: The Boarding Action:** Zaniph stands on the deck of a hijacked merchant ship, drawing the Githyanki flagship, the *Vlaakith's Pride*, down to investigate. 
* **The Climax:** Banki and Kael lead the Vael-Kaelor collective in a boarding action, teleporting onto the flagship's deck. While Kaelen Grey uses his abjuration magic to seal the ship's planar engine, Banki uses the Null-Fang to negate the helmsman's connection to the spelljamming helm, causing the flagship to collide with a second ship, breaking the Githyanki attack.

### 4. The Aftermath & The Loophole
The Githyanki fleet retreats into the Astral Sea, leaving a mark of blood on Sablehook's gates. When Terik and the Lords of Dust step forward to collect their payment, Banki plays his hand. Having explicitly written the payment terms in the contract to deliver "the broken silver sword of Zaniph," Banki places the fractured shards of the weapon on the table.

Before Terik can touch them, Banki channels the Tri-Weave and the Sovereign Thread through the Null-Fang. A blinding surge of psionic fire, silver runes, and divine light floods the shards. The metal flows together, not just repairing the break, but enhancing the blade into a complete, humming **Tri-Weave Silver Blade**.

Because the weapon is now a restored, enhanced artifact, it no longer fits the literal definition of "the broken silver sword" promised in the contract. Under the laws of fiendish contract magic, the promised item has ceased to exist, leaving Terik empty-handed.

### 5. Terik's Reaction & The Counter-Stroke
Terik, bound by the literal text of the pact, cannot legally seize the enhanced blade. He stares at Banki, his tiger-headed form baring its fangs in a low, purring laugh. 

"A clever trick, Pixie," Terik whispers. "You have kept the steel. But you have defaulted on the literal payment, rendering the contract unfulfilled. Enjoy the sunlight while it lasts."

With a wave of his hand, Terik revokes his magic, dropping the displacement shield to expose Sablehook to the harbor and the local templars.

### 6. The Tri-Weave Shroud
To Terik's shock, the compound does not appear. 

In the final seconds before Terik dropped the magic, Kaelen ("Suture") and Banki finalized their replica. The moment Terik's magic faded, Sablehook's own **Tri-Weave Shroud** snapped into place. Only Banki has access to the Sovereign Thread—an immutable Rule of Law existing on a layer more foundational than even Divinity. By using this absolute authority to anchor the shroud, he forces the illusion to adapt in real-time to the minds of anyone searching for the compound, keeping them invisible.

Terik's amusement turns to genuine alarm. He realizes they didn't just cheat him—they stole his technology. However, the victory has a cost:
1. **The Mental Strain:** While the Sovereign Thread is an immutable law that cannot be drained or depleted, using it to enforce such an extreme, constant physical rule is thoroughly mentally taxing. It places an immense cognitive and psychological burden solely on Banki to keep the shroud active.
2. **The Beacon Leaked:** Enraged, Terik's agents leak Sablehook's exact coordinates to the returning Githyanki scouts anyway, knowing that while the shroud hides them from mortal eyes, the Githyanki's astral tracking will target the Tri-Weave Silver Blade's resonance.
3. **The Target on Zaniph:** Zaniph now wields a legendary weapon that can slice planar rifts, but he is a marked man, hunted by Vlaakith's elite inquisitors.

SCENARIO 3: THE FLESHWARPED FOUNDRY (Assault on the Consortium)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Logline: Sablehook allies with House Tarkanan to infiltrate and destroy a secret biomantic foundry established by the Thessalan Consortium in the city's deep foundations.

### 1. Setting & Atmosphere
The **Belowmarket Deep**, an ancient dwarven ruin beneath the city. The air is hot and dry, smelling of copper, grease, and burning flesh. The Consortium has constructed massive copper vats (the "Preservation Line") where they are breeding war-beasts—hybrids of displacer beasts and subterranean predators—using mutagens refined from the brine-veins.

### 2. The Alliance & Planning
* **The Players:** Banki, Mikhailis Vael-Kaelor (operating under his false name, his Shadar-kai eyes cold and calculating), and **Grik Clayfoot** representing the Tarkanan scouts.
* **The Tension:** Mikhailis knows the layout of the foundry—he helped design its early prototypes before the fire. He wants to retrieve his stolen journals, while Grik wants to free several Tarkanan members who were captured for biological grafting. The Lords of Dust (operating through a middleman) provide alchemical formulas to poison the vats.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution
* **Phase 1: The Surface Riot:** House Tarkanan coordinates a massive underworld riot in the Belowmarket, drawing the Consortium's security guards and construct sentries to the surface gates.
* **Phase 2: The Infiltration:** Grik Clayfoot guides Mikhailis, Banki, and a team of sappers through the narrow steam-vents directly into the heart of the Preservation Line.
* **Phase 3: Destabilizing the Vats:** They inject the Lords of Dust's alchemical poisons into the growth vats. The war-beasts inside begin to mutate uncontrollably, tearing through the copper containment walls.
* **The Climax:** The Consortium's chief warden activates the security override, trapping the team in the vat-room. Banki transforms into his PixIllithid WarForm and uses the Null-Fang's covenant-coil chain to anchor Kael's chronal spells, allowing Kael to temporarily reverse the lock on the blast doors while Banki negates the warding runes.

### 4. The Aftermath & Double-Cross
The foundry is destroyed, but Mikhailis secretly recovers a chest of his old research notes on the "Refinement Line" instead of burning them as promised to Banki. This creates an immediate crack in the trust between Banki and Kael.

SCENARIO 4: SEALING THE XORIAT TEAR IN THE DEEPMIND ANNEX
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Logline: In a desperate final battle, the coalition descends into the deepest brine-veins beneath the city to seal a widening planar tear to Xoriat before the Daelkyr's madness dissolves Khorvaire.

### 1. Setting & Atmosphere
The **Deepmind Annex**, a non-Euclidean cavern where the walls are composed of shifting, semi-liquid stone and the gravity shifts unpredictably. The Xoriat tear appears as a vertical slit of absolute, blinding pink-and-white light that distorts the shapes of anything nearby. aberrant tentacles and floating eyes bleed through the gap.

### 2. The Alliance & Planning
* **The Players:** Banki, Kael (his chronal sense fractured by the proximity to Xoriat), **Thora** (leader of House Tarkanan), and **Hruujj**, a rakshasa lord who reveals his true, fiendish form for the first time.
* **The Tension:** Thora's enforcers are in agony; their aberrant dragonmarks are weeping blood and discharging uncontrollable elemental energy due to the rift's resonance. Hruujj demands that the Vael-Kaelor collective act as the front line, openly intending to let them die to ensure the seals are applied.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution
* **Phase 1: The Aberrant Shield:** Thora leads the Tarkanan enforcers. Using their aberrant marks of disintegration and plague, they create a physical shield of volatile energy, burning through the hordes of dolgaunts and Xoriat void-monsters crawling from the rift.
* **Phase 2: The Chronal Guide:** Kael uses his fractured chronal sense to read the shifting gravity of the cavern, guiding Hruujj's fiendish casters to the optimal locations to erect the sealing runes.
* **Phase 3: The Negation Lock:** Banki steps into the direct light of the tear. The raw madness of Xoriat threatens to dissolve his mind-link. 
* **The Climax:** Banki channels the Tri-Weave through the Null-Fang, driving its cowlspike head into the stone at the base of the rift. He negates the reality-warping energy, stabilizing the cavern's geometry for a few precious seconds. Hruujj and his fiends immediately slam the ancient seals of the Overlords into the stone, locking the rift shut.

### 4. The Aftermath & Double-Cross
The rift is sealed, but Hruujj attempts to use the closing energy to siphon the power of Banki's Null-Fang to weaken the seals of a nearby Overlord. Kael foresees this and uses his last chronal spell to force a temporal loop, allowing Sablehook and Tarkanan to escape the cavern before Hruujj can spring the trap, leaving the alliance broken but their minds intact.
